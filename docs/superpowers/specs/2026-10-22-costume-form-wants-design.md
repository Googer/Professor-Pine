# Want enhancements (8R-4): costume and form wants, Change…, the Team GO Rocket dialog, grunt lineups (8R-4L): design

Status: draft, agreed with Matt in conversation on 2026-10-22; fresh review next. Gantt phase 17, plan 8R-4, before the stagings of 8R-1 to 8R-4 so that `/want`'s dialog is staged once. Builds on the stop reports design (`2026-10-21-stop-reports-design.md`, `/want`'s dialog of boxes) and on what 8R-1 to 8R-3 built. The exploration of where costumes are lost today is in the ledger, `.superpowers/sdd/8r-4-wants/explore.md`.

## 1. What members get

- **A costumed Pokémon can be wanted by itself.** PokémonXP 2026's Cosmog-suit Pikachu (`PIKACHU_PXP_2026`) was a research reward many chased; costumes also come as wild spawns and raid bosses (Halloween 2026 Part I: "Zubat wearing a top hat" in the wild, "Pikachu wearing a hat and capelet" in one-star raids).
- **"Costumed" means any costume of that Pokémon**, now or later, never one particular costume (Matt: only one or two are active at a time, and Pikachu has had 68). There is no list of costumes anywhere.
- **A plain want still covers costumes** (Matt: yes). Ticking a form includes its costumes; "Only costumed" narrows (Matt: anyone ticking the base form expects the costumed ones too).
- **The dialog asks which forms** when a Pokémon has several (Matt's idea): ticking every form is the whole species, forms added later included.
- **Future-proof** (Matt): a Pokémon with no costume or one form today may get them later. Nothing stored names a costume, a want stays valid as forms and costumes come, and the choices appear by themselves once the game data has them.
- Megas stay wantable (Matt: the Mega Energy of a mega raid is the reward many want); they are listed among the forms (section 3).

## 2. What a want is

A Pokémon want today is a species (every form, megas included) or one form (a catalogue key), with Raids/Spawns as its scope, a shadow row, and `TargetWant` rows for Research, Dynamax and Gigantamax.

8R-4 adds one property, **costumed only** (`costumed`), to every Pokémon want, species or form: a property of the row, like the scope, never part of what the row is for (review R1):

- `PokemonNotification` and `TargetWant` each get a column `costumed` (tinyint, not null, default 0) in one additive migration (029, or the next free number at merge). The legacy bot neither reads nor writes it; until the cutover the legacy bot never runs on a database with such rows (it runs on production, which stays at its own schema).
- Tokens, unique keys and ids are unchanged: `TargetWant` tokens (`research:pokemon:<dex>[:<form>]`, `dynamax:…`, `gigantamax:…`), `wanting(guildId, targets)`, `wantToken`, `sameNotificationTarget`, `stopArgs` / `parseStopArgs`, `targetWantArgs`. A Stop or Remove takes the row away whatever its `costumed`.
- The matchers hand back the want with its `costumed` (beside the target), so a DM can name "Costumed Pikachu".
- Duplicate rows of one target (`readWants`' merge): plain wins over costumed, as `widerScope` lets the wider scope win.

**Which forms** are stored as today: every form ticked is one species row (`form` null); some ticked are one row per ticked form. The boxes and `costumed` are the same for every row of one species the dialog wrote (section 3, "One answer per species").

**Matching** (`pokemonWantFits` and its callers): a want fits a sighting as today (the species, or exactly the form's entry), and, when the want is costumed, only a sighting that is costumed. A plain want ignores whether the sighting is costumed. A costume belongs to the form it copies, which the game data already says (`GameData.aliases`: every Pikachu costume copies `pikachu`, `corsola_spring_2026` copies `corsola_galarian`), so a costumed sighting is matched under that form.

## 3. The dialog

`/want <Pokémon>` opens "Pikachu: what for?" as now, with the boxes in "Tell me about" (Raids, Dynamax, Gigantamax, Spawns, Research, Shadow, each only where it can happen, as 8R-1 built). **Research is no longer shown for a legendary, mythical or ultra beast** (`PokemonEntry.class`; Matt, 2026-10-22: they have never been ordinary task rewards), unless today's task list has it as a reward or the member already holds its Research box (then it shows, ticked, so it can be taken off). Such a dialog may have Raids and Shadow only. **One box only** (Arceus: Raids; Matt, 2026-10-22): no Tell me about field; the text says what it is told for ("Arceus is told for raids, in the regions you can see. Pick the forms you want; none picked takes it off your list."), and the dialog opens only for its Which, Costumes or Remove field; with none of those it writes at once, as a one-box Pokémon does today (Mew). Two fields may follow:

- **Which <species>** (label `Which Corsola`), only when the species has more than one entry, megas included (Charizard: Charizard, Mega Charizard X, Mega Charizard Y). Each entry by its name. Up to 10 entries a checkbox group like the boxes; 11 to 25 a multi-pick dropdown (string select, `min_values` 0) (Arceus, Silvally 18; Vivillon, Spinda, Scatterbug, Spewpa 21; Furfrou 11). Over 25 (Unown, 29) no Which field: `/want unown` is the species row only, and its Submit leaves letter rows alone; a letter picked in the autocomplete opens today's dialog for that one form (`wantbox:p201:set:<mask>:unown_a`); `/wants` shows Unown's species row and each letter as lines of their own (review R4a). A mega counts for Raids only: the writer gives a ticked mega a raid row and nothing else (no Spawns scope, no shadow row, no `TargetWant`; review R4c); a mega ticked without Raids gets nothing (the answer says so). `/want mega charizard y` opens the species dialog with only that mega ticked (one model; review R4b), no longer writing at once.
- **Costumes**: one Checkbox (`ComponentType.Checkbox`; its description goes on the Label, since a Checkbox has only `custom_id` and `default`), `Only costumed <species>` (`Only costumed ones` under a Which field), shown when the species has a costume in the game data (any form's) or when a raid boss list, an active event's spawns or the research list has it costumed now, as `shownBoxes` does for Dynamax and Shadow (review R7: Zubat in a top hat before the game master has it). Description: "any costume, now or later" / "of the forms ticked".

What is ticked when it opens:

- A species with no want yet: every box that can happen as 8R-1 does, every form, Costumed off. Picked as a form in the autocomplete (`/want galarian corsola`): only that form ticked.
- A species with wants: the member's rows. Forms as they are stored; a box ticked when any of the species' rows has it; Costumed only when every row is costumed (review R6: a wrong narrowing loses DMs, a wrong widening only adds some).
- **One answer per species.** The Submit writes the ticked boxes and Costumed to every ticked form and removes the species' rows that are not ticked, in one transaction under the member's lock, as `WantBoxStorage.set` does now. Rows of one species that disagree (a member's older Ponyta raid want beside a Galarian Ponyta spawn want) are made alike by a Submit; the dialog's text says so when it opens on such rows ("Your Ponyta wants differ by form; Submit sets them alike.", in the dialog's one text, so the field count stays within five). Boxes the dialog did not show are kept per row as they were (`planBoxes`; review R4d).
- Nothing ticked in the boxes, or no form ticked, takes the species off.
- The species' rows are every row of that Pokédex number in both tables (`WantBoxRepository.rows()` / `tokens()` widened from the subject to the species), Unown's letters excepted (review R4f).

The Submit's custom id stays `wantbox:…:set` with what the dialog showed: the boxes as now, whether Costumes and Remove were shown, and a short hash of the keys shown in the Which field (review R5: a mask in catalogue order would name other forms after a game data refresh). A Submit whose hash no longer matches is `STALE_COMPONENT`, and so is an id of a dialog opened before the deploy. The ticked forms are the select's values, which are keys.

**The answer after Submit** names the want as `/wants` does and adds, in small print, a warning where Costumed meets a box that has never had costumes (Shadow, Dynamax, Gigantamax: a fixed list in the code, `NEVER_COSTUMED_YET`): "Costumed Pokémon haven't appeared in max battles or as shadows so far, so Gigantamax and Shadow stay quiet unless that changes." It is a warning, never a refusal: a future costumed shadow or max boss gets through. A mega ticked without Raids is said the same way.

## 4. Naming, lists, autocomplete, DMs

- Names: "Costumed Pikachu", "Costumed Galarian Corsola"; a want of all forms and costumed is "Costumed Corsola". There are no costume names (the game's text file has none, and the feeds' names are per costume).
- `/wants`: one line per species, its forms named together when some are ticked, up to three by name ("Arceus, Dragon Arceus, Fire Arceus: Raids") and above that by count ("Arceus (17 of 18 forms): Raids"; review R11), and a **Change…** button for the line (Matt, 2026-10-22: as on the DMs), which opens the species' (or tier's) dialog with Remove; a Submit shows the list's page again as a press on the list does. Lines without a dialog (IV wants, items and Mega Energy) keep the red Remove and come last, after every Change… line (Matt, 2026-10-22; the IV wants get no dialog). The list's order (Matt: it looked random; today it is the rows' order): the Pokémon by species name A–Z (a costumed or form want sorts under its species: Galarian Corsola under C, Costumed Pikachu under P), the raid tiers in tier order, Team GO Rocket, then the Remove lines: items and Mega Energy A–Z, Perfect IVs, Zero IVs; the Rocket targets are one line with Change… (section 4b). The list's ids gain a `change` action beside `remove` (`wantlist:<page>:change:sp:<dex>` / `st:<level>`); "Remove all…" stays. `/settings show` counts those lines.
- `/unwant`: as today a pick removes every box of what it names; a species removes all its rows. A form picked while the member holds only the species row (`/unwant galarian corsola`) removes nothing and says that their Corsola want covers it, pointing to `/want corsola`; the row is never split silently (review R4e).
- Autocomplete of `/want`: unchanged (species, forms, megas, tiers, IVs, rewards, Rocket targets); no costume choices. Typed text: unchanged (no "costumed pikachu").
- DMs: the reason line names the want ("Costumed Pikachu"). On a Pokémon or tier reason the button is **Change…** (Matt, 2026-10-22), not Stop: it opens the same dialog for that server with the member's choices as stored (read in time, as `/want` does), and its Submit shows the pressed button as a disabled `Changed` (as `withStopped` does). Reasons without a dialog (gym targets, new trains, items and Mega Energy, IV wants, boss-set) keep the one-press Stop; Rocket reasons get Change… (section 4b). The ids are unchanged (review R1).
- **Remove** (Matt: unticking several things is tedious): a fifth field, one Checkbox `Remove <species> from my list`, shown when the member has a want of that species (from `/want` and from Change…). Ticked, the Submit removes every row of the species in both tables, whatever else is ticked. With it the dialog has at most five top-level components (the text, Tell me about, Which, Costumes, Remove), Discord's limit for a modal: every other sentence (rows that differ, the mega note) goes into the one text, the limit is stated where the fields are built, and a test builds the largest dialog (Charizard: Which, Costumes, Remove). A tier's dialog is the text, its boxes and Remove. Remove is the Label's own text (at most 45 characters, truncated). Remove wins over everything else ticked and removes every row of the species in both tables, forms not shown included; "nothing ticked takes it off" stays for a dialog without Remove (review R19).

**Change… in detail** (review R15–R20):
- Ids: DMs already sent keep their `dm:<server>:stop:…` buttons, read as today. Change… is a new action, `dm:<server>:change:sp:<dex>` or `…:st:<level>` (`stopArgs`' subject spelling): the dialog always opens for the whole species or tier with the stored rows. Pokémon reasons `p`, `ps`, `rp`, `dx`, `gx` open the species; `t`, `ts` the tier; IV wants keep Stop. Two reasons of one species in one DM give one Change… button.
- Opening: the press branches before `deferUpdate` (the dialog must be the first answer), takes no message lock before `showModal`, reads the rows with `readInTime` for the server of the id. A member no longer in the server, or a server with notifications off, is told so instead of a dialog. The press counts against `MEMBER_WRITE_THROTTLE` (its key must work without a server).
- The Submit from a DM: the dialog's id carries the server's snowflake when it was opened from a DM (about 60 characters with the masks and the Which hash), and the Submit takes the server from the id when `!interaction.inGuild()`. The `wantbox` guard becomes "a server, or a snowflake in the id"; the Submit writes only the presser's own rows.
- Acknowledging: from a DM (`interaction.isFromMessage()`) the Submit acknowledges with `deferUpdate`, edits the message under the `messages` lock (`fetchReply` first) so that every Change… button of that species or tier reads a disabled `Changed` (`Removed` when Remove was ticked), then answers with `followUp`, as a Stop press does. The answer is given even when the edit fails; a classic DM from before 8B-1 has its button taken off (`withoutButton`). A Submit with an out-of-date Which hash is refused and leaves the button as it was.

## 4b. One Team GO Rocket dialog (Matt, 2026-10-22)

The Rocket targets of 8R-2 (`grunt:<type>`, `grunt:none`, `decoy`, `leader:<cliff|arlo|sierra|any>`, `giovanni`; `TargetWant` rows, tokens unchanged) get one dialog instead of one `/want` each:

- `/want rocket`, or picking or typing any Rocket target (`/want water grunt`, `arlo`), opens "Team GO Rocket: what for?". A picked target opens with the member's rows ticked plus that one (a first want: only that one).
- Fields: the text; **Grunts**, a multi-pick dropdown of the 18 types, "Grunts without a type" and "Decoy grunt" (20 options); **Leaders**, a checkbox group of Cliff, Arlo, Sierra, Any leader, Giovanni; **Remove**, when the member has any Rocket want (removes every Rocket row). Nothing picked in either field takes them all off.
- "Any leader" stays its own target (it covers the three and any future leader); ticked beside named ones, the answer says the named ones add nothing. The rows are written as picked.
- The Submit writes the Rocket rows in one transaction under the member's lock, as the Pokémon dialog does; its custom id carries a hash of the options shown (types and leaders as of the lineups), as the Which field does.
- `/wants`: one line "Team GO Rocket: Water and Fire grunts, Arlo, Giovanni" (over three grunt types by count: "7 grunt types") with Change…. A DM's Rocket reason gets Change… (`dm:<server>:change:rocket`), which opens the same dialog from the DM as in section 4.
- Unchanged: matching, a grunt type at most one DM an hour, a hideout once a day, the labels of 8R-2 in DMs.

**Two lineups of one grunt type** (Matt, 2026-10-22; a change to 8R-2's `/rocket report` and the map's Rocket choices). ScrapedDuck lists a type's male and female grunt apart ("Water-type Male Grunt": Magikarp, Feebas → Magikarp → Magikarp, Gyarados; "Water-type Female Grunt": Mudkip, Tentacool, Krabby …; the untyped grunts likewise), and 8R-2 merges them (`parseRocketLineups`: a type of two lineups is their union), so a Shadow Magikarp want fires on every water grunt.
- Reporting offers, for a type with two lineups, one choice per lineup named by its first slot only, plus the merged one: "Water-type grunt · Magikarp, Feebas", "Water-type grunt · Mudkip, Tentacool, Krabby", "Water-type grunt · not sure which" (both lineups' catches, as today). The gender is never shown (Matt: members tell the two apart by the lineup, not by the grunt's gender; the gender is only how the feed keeps them apart). A type with one lineup is one choice, as today.
- Keys: `grunt:<type>:male` / `:female` for a lineup, `grunt:<type>` for not sure (today's key, so a standing report keeps its meaning); stable across rotations. A report's catches are the chosen lineup's (not sure: both). One Rocket report per stop is unchanged; another lineup of the same type replaces a standing one as another grunt does, the same key is `already`.
- Wants stay by type: `grunt:water` fires on all three choices (one DM an hour per type, unchanged). A catch is wanted with the Pokémon's Shadow box, which now fires only on the lineup that gives it (and on "not sure which" when either does), across rotations.
- `/rocket report who:` and the map's list: the three choices together, by type; typed `water` / `water grunt` reads as not sure which.

## 5. Knowing that a sighting is costumed

A sighting (raid boss, spawn, research reward, Rocket catch, max boss) carries `costumed: boolean` to the matchers next to `{ dex, key }`.

**The costume test** (one pure function, `isCostumed(name, image, ids)`), any of:
- the game data says the form id is a costume: `GameData` keeps a set of costume aliases, `GAME_DATA_VERSION` 7 — the aliases of `isCostume` forms plus `*_COPY_2019` and `PSYDUCK_SWIM_2025`, never the `_S` legendaries of `COSMETIC_FORMS` (`RAIKOU_S` …, review R3; Matt's answer 1) and never a flagged costume with stats of its own (Armored Mewtwo is a form); a fixture test pins it (Pikachu 68, `raikou_s` not costumed);
- the picture names a costume (`.c<COSTUME>` part, or an `.f<FORM>` the game data has as a costume alias);
- a form id the game data does not know yet that `isCostumeForm` (`raid-boss-feed.ts`) calls a costume;
- as the last fallback, the name says so: Leek Duck names them "<Pokémon> wearing …" and uses hand-named pictures (`zubat-top-hat.png`) before the game data has them; other wordings exist ("Flying Pikachu", "… (Costume)"), so the picture and id tests come first and the name test is tested with names from real pages (review R10).

**By source:**
- **Raids.** Worked out when the DM goes out, not stored on the raid (review R2): `NotifyService.sighting()` already finds the list's boss of the raid's tier (`shownBoss`), so `ShownBoss` gets `costumed` from the listed boss, set in the `familyBosses` merge when any listed variant of that entry and tier is costumed (a costumed and a plain Pikachu in one tier stay one boss). No party state change. A tier listing the species both plain and costumed makes the raid costumed for matching (Matt agreed: a costume hunter would rather a false alarm than a miss; a plain want fires either way). A staff override (`/admin boss add`) has no name, so it is costumed only by its form ids.
- **Spawns.** Inferred from the active events (Matt: nobody bothers reporting the plain one during such an event), in the one funnel `SpawnService` (`spawnReported`), so `/wild` and the map's reports alike (review R8). The spawns of an active event: a community day's from ScrapedDuck's `extraData.communityday.spawns` (no page request); for an ordinary event that the feed marks `generic.hasSpawns` (the feed has no list for those), the Spawns section of its Leek Duck page, read when it becomes active and every six hours, kept in memory. A report of a species is costumed when an active event lists that species (or the form) costumed; listed both plain and costumed counts as costumed. The reporter is not asked. A page that cannot be read gives no event spawns: costumed wants stay quiet and plain wants fire. `/wild`'s answer and the spawn message say "costumed" when it is.
- **Research.** A reward is costumed by the costume test on its Leek Duck name and picture. Rewards are kept as JSON (`StopReport.detail`, the task list's `rewards`; review R9), so no migration: `costumed` is computed again from name and picture when read, so rows written before 8R-4 count too. The reward's dedup key within a task includes it (a plain and a costumed reward of one species stay two); no report's key changes. A reward a member typed is not costumed.
- **Team GO Rocket and max battles.** Never costumed so far (Matt). Their sightings carry `costumed: false`; the code path is the same, so a later source can set it.

## 6. What does not change

`/counters`, `/rank` (a costume has its species' stats), the raid post's name and picture (the post shows the costume picture already where the list has it), the history (`bossKey`), tier wants, IV wants, Rocket targets, gym targets, the legacy codec.

## 7b. Second review (S1–S13), as taken

- **S1, `/want`'s deferral.** Whether `/want` opens a dialog is decided synchronously (`opensDialog`), from game data, lists and the task list only: it opens when Tell me about (two boxes or more), Which or Costumes would show. A species with one box and neither field writes at once, as Mew does today, whatever the member holds (Research held on a legendary is shown only in a dialog that opens anyway; `/unwant` and the list remove the rest). The one-box Submit reads one box in the mask and no group as that box ticked (`tickedOf` today throws `STALE_COMPONENT`).
- **S2, Change… never writes.** A press reads the rows in time; with nothing held it answers "X is not on your list; /want adds it." (the Rocket dialog likewise). With a want held it always opens the dialog, Remove at least (Mew from a DM or the list: Remove only).
- **S3, Change… from `/wants`.** The dialog's id carries the list's page (`:l<page>`); the Submit acknowledges with `update` (`showInPlace`, as the list's buttons do; Discord accepts it for a modal opened from a component, ephemeral included) and shows that page again with the answer as a note at its top; a warning (Costumed on a never-costumed box, a mega without Raids) also goes as an ephemeral `followUp`. "Remove all…" keeps answering apart. `renderMemberList` / `memberListControls` get a per-item button (action, label, style), shared with `/targets` (which keeps Remove); the `change` branch reads in time and shows the dialog before any `update`.
- **S4, Unown letters.** A reason or line of a form beyond the Which cap opens that one form's dialog (`dm:<server>:change:sp:201:unown_a`, `wantlist:<p>:change:sp:201:unown_a`), as `/want` does for a letter.
- **S5–S7, lineup keys.** The grunt `RocketWho` gets `lineup?: 'male' | 'female'`; `rocketKey` / `parseRocketKey` read and write the suffix; `isRocketWho` (a want) refuses a lineup; one `typeKeyOf(subjectKey)` gives the type key wherever a want, the hour (`quietKey`) or the announce line is meant (`rocketTargets`, `rocketWantsFiring`, `rocket:<server>:<type key>`); `rocketShadowFiring` stays per lineup (the report's catches). `decideRocket`, `isHideoutReport`, `ofHideout`, `rocketOptions`, `readWho` learn the key. Storage: the lineups stay one `RocketLineup` row per type, the lineups inside its JSON (no migration; its `varchar(20)` would not hold `grunt:electric:female`); lineup keys exist only in reports and choices. The feed's names are read "female" before "male".
- **S8, 25 choices.** `/rocket report who:` with nothing typed offers the hideouts and one choice per type (not sure which); the lineup choices once the typed words narrow to a type.
- **S9, Take back id.** `replaceAll` of `:` by `.` on both sides, a fourth argument refused; the longer keys push more ids onto the bare fallback.
- **S10, same type.** Not sure which over a standing lineup of that type is `already`; a lineup over not sure replaces it (it refines; the hour per type keeps a second DM away); the "was" line names lineups.
- **S11, labels.** The choice keeps its lineup's first slot (new data beside the catches, also a label per lineup in the map's `/api/rocket-options`). Two lineups with the same first slot, or a feed name without a gender word, give the merged choice only. Typed `water` reads only the merged choice. A lineup choice carries a short fingerprint of its lineup; a report after a rotation changed it is `LINEUPS_CHANGED`.
- **S12, the Rocket dialog's options** are fixed: all 18 types, the untyped grunts, the decoy, the leaders, Giovanni, whatever today's rotation; a version tag in the id, no hash of the lineups.
- **S13.** `/unwant` gains a "Team GO Rocket" choice (removes every Rocket row) and keeps the single targets typed; `/want rocket` and "Team GO Rocket" in `rocketWantChoices` / `readWanted` are new; the list's Rocket line is `wantlist:<p>:change:rocket`; `/want`'s help loses "Team GO Rocket is wanted at once".
