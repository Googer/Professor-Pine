/rank answer: mockups (2026-10-23)

Matt's three ideas: pictures for the evolution line, a small link beside each "Up to level" block instead of a row of pvpivs buttons, and switching the shown Pokémon to another of the line. What Discord allows decides the shape:
  • A message holds at most 40 components, every nested one counted (a section with a picture is 3: the section, its text, the picture).
  • A section's accessory is one thing: a picture or a button. So a line member cannot have both its picture and a "Show" button beside it.
  • A link button always carries Discord's own ↗ mark; a label can be just an emoji (🔗).
The numbers are Pine's real ranks today. Each mockup ends with its component count.

The rule: A while it fits in 40, that is 18 + one per level shown + three per member (a line of six with four Super Mega levels, seven with two); otherwise B. Of all the game's lines only Eevee's (nine) is longer; Ralts' (six, with its megas) fits A.

C. "Show another of the family…" opened

One menu instead of a button per member: it fits every line (Eevee's nine too) in two components, and each option can carry the member's rank. Picking one redraws the answer for that Pokémon, same IVs, league and filter, line still shown. As with the league buttons, the member who asked changes the answer; anybody else gets a private copy.
