# One Join, a Group… button and a Meet… button (plan 8X-1B)

Worked out with Matt in chat on 2026-10-20, after 8X-1's final review found that a post of several groups has a Join per letter, which never reaches `chooseGroup`. Matt: the UI is inconsistent between one group and several (Join beside each group, not below), and members are lazy: buttons and dialogs over typed commands. Raids and trains (meetups have no groups). Every question was answered by Matt the same day, after a fresh review (its other findings are settled in the plan: `.superpowers/sdd/8x-1b-buttons/progress.md`). **Approved by Matt, 2026-10-20.** The mockup of the posts at phone and desktop widths, with Pine's button icons and the three dialogs: https://claude.ai/artifact/GQCrLuouMyfqLR1kNnNnkw (version 8; the "Raid reported without times" post shows Set time; Group… is drawn enabled, the design disables it while nobody is signed up).

## Decided

1. **One layout for one group and several** (Matt, 2026-10-20: "we'll want it to _always_ be how the multiple groups UI is", like legacy Pine's reactions): the statuses are text lines (`Coming (3): Ash +1, Misty`, a status nobody is in has no line), under a group's heading line (with one group no letter: `4 trainers · meeting at …`); every button is in the rows at the bottom: row 1 Interested, Join, Here, Done (raids), Leave (legacy Pine's order, Matt; the status lines are in the same order: Interested, Coming, Here, Done); row 2 Meet… (its icon), −1, +1, Remote; row 3 the buttons with a label, which open something: Group… first, then Set time (in Meet…'s place while a raid has no times) and Screenshot feedback (Matt, 2026-10-20: the first two rows icons only, and the same places in raids and trains). Meet… (the game's Select Time icon, which members know from the game), Leave, −1, +1 and Remote become icons without a label too, like the statuses (Matt, 2026-10-20; Group… keeps its label, its meaning is not obvious from an icon; the hints that name Meet… show its icon instead: Remote's icon matches the game's remote icon, members know it). The groups lose their letter buttons. This replaces the one-group layout of 8D-F/8D-G (a status's button beside its line, empty statuses shown so their buttons had a place). Trains: row 1 without Done, row 2 −1, +1, Remote (no Meet…), row 3 Group…, Move train…, Split group…, Whole route (a region copy keeps Group… and Whole route). Mockup in chat, 2026-10-20.
2. **Which group, asked only of a newcomer.** In a post of several groups, Join, Interested and Here ask in a dialog which group, only of a member not signed up. A member signed up changes status in their own group, no dialog. Where the party has a lobby size the first choice is "Any group with room" (`chooseGroup`), chosen to start with; elsewhere the newest group is. What changed while the dialog was open is decided at the submit. Done never asks.
3. **"Group…"**, a grey button, always shown (Matt, 2026-10-20: a button that appears moves the others), first in row 3, with one group too; **disabled** (Discord's `disabled`, for everybody) while nobody is signed up, when it could do nothing but what Join does; the first sign-up redraws the post anyway, which enables it. Where it can do nothing for one member only (alone in a one-group party), it stays enabled and that press is refused privately (below): a dialog like `/group`: move to another group (the member's own left out), or a new group (with an optional label). In a max battle New stays. A member not signed up is signed up by it at once, as Coming in the group they choose (an existing one or New; Matt: "the user's intent is easily inferred"), as legacy Pine's `/group` did (`setMemberGroup`: Coming first) and the new bot's `joinInto` does.
4. **Split stays apart** ("Split group…" on trains, plan 8T-H): only the conductor and staff, a different act; no split for raids (8T design: a raid's groups share one gym and boss).
5. **"Meet…"**, for raids, in the place of "Set time" (which shows only while a raid has no times; a meeting time needs them, so the two never show together). Its dialog sets the meeting time of the member's own group, and says so: title "Meeting time for group B" ("Meeting time" with one group), "For your group: everybody in it is told." No typing: one choice, in time order, of the game's in-game RSVP times (the hatch, then every 15 minutes while the raid lasts, each marked `· in-game RSVP`) and round clock times every 5 minutes between them (7:25, 7:30 …), past times left out, "No meeting time" when one is set. The hatch is the most common meeting time (Matt: legacy Pine's `!meet hatch`): it is the first choice, "At hatch (7:22) · in-game RSVP", and on an egg the one chosen to start with (it is the next in-game RSVP time then); a meeting time at the hatch already follows a corrected hatch (`raid.ts`, the hatch change). Offered on an egg too once its hatch is known (the game offers Select Time before the hatch: the iPhone T5 egg screenshot of the OCR corpus, 30 minutes before; Matt confirmed). The button shows the game's Select Time icon (a calendar page with a plus, from PokeMiners' `pogo_assets`, as application emoji `pinemeet`, plain ⏰ until uploaded). Chosen to start with: the group's time, else the next in-game RSVP time. The rules are `/meet`'s (signed up, own group, the others told, `MEET_THROTTLE`); `/meet` stays. The in-game RSVP rule is one function: Matt checks on a Wednesday evening whether other raid lengths offer other times (assumed: the same 15-minute steps for every tier). Trains and meetups get no Meet… in this plan (there `/meet` is the whole party's start, with a ping).
6. **Empty groups are pruned** (a wish of legacy Pine's, Matt: "I like group pruning"): one pure step after every change inside `PartyStore.update` removes a group nobody is in (Interested and Done members count; Done members are the raid's record), unless it has a label or a meeting time, and never the party's last group. Silently: no line, no ping, the post redrawn. Letters are not renamed (C stays C: members were told "group C"); a new group takes the first free letter (as 8T's splits). The default group, when pruned, becomes the newest left. A group Join opened in a max battle goes again once it empties.
7. **The web map mirrors the post** (Matt: "we want the web UX to be similar to the Discord one for the party UI"): one Join that asks which group in the map's own popup where there are several, by the same rules. Group… and Meet… on the map are left to the web session's UX sweep (follow-ups); this plan does the Discord side and as much of the map as must follow.

8. **After the fresh review (Matt took both recommendations, 2026-10-20):** Meet…'s list is a dropdown (a radio choice holds at most 10): from now on every in-game RSVP time and the 5-minute times of the next hour only, 24 at most, and "No meeting time"; a time typed through `/meet` off the grid is added as a choice; a raid with no future time left refuses Meet… at the press; max battles get their own rule in 8X-3. Group… with nothing to choose: a member alone in a one-group party is told a new group would change nothing; where one way is left, a dialog without a choice ("Submit opens group B"); the label field reads "Label for a new group (optional)" and is ignored for a move. The review's other findings (a group stamp, state version 12, pruning in the store, throttles, the map's press body …) are settled in the plan (ledger).

9. **Radio choices on a phone (Matt's check, 2026-10-20, `/delete` on the dev bot):** Android shows them well, each option with its description line, and **selects the first option itself** when the dialog opens, although Pine marks none (`dialog.ts` sends no `default`; the desktop showed none selected, 2026-10-18). So every choice Pine wants selected to start with goes **first** in its list ("Any group with room", else the newest group first), besides any `default` mark; and an option that must not be picked by accident is never first (`/delete` and `/region remove` already put the gentler way first). Discord adds its "Do not share passwords" box at the top of every bot dialog: keep the dialogs short. iPhone: the same look, **nothing selected** at the start, and Submit refused until one is picked (required). So the `default` mark decides whether an iPhone member must tap before Submit; whether iOS honours it is a staging step of 8X-1B. The train split's group picker (8T-H) has the first group selected on Android before anybody picks: a Minor point for the plan.
