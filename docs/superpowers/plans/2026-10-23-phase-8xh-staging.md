The findings are in the follow-ups, "From the staging run of 8X-G" (G1 to G7); the brief is `.superpowers/sdd/8x-h-staging-fixes/design.md`. ## Part two: the catch-up on reconnect

11. **Ready the outage.** First, in #north-side, a train with a route and a max battle if one is possible. Then, last, a raid (`/raid`) whose end is one minute away (`/end` in 1), and go straight on to step 12. Note the time. Read the log's tail: `connected to Discord`, the clock running. **The rule** (review F1): an ended raid is closed (its last showing, the archiving) at its end + `deletionGraceTime` + `deletionWarningTime` + up to one clock interval (`cleanupInterval`), and that closing must fall inside the outage with a minute to spare at its end. `var/stage/settings.json` has `deletionGraceTime` 1 (minute), `deletionWarningTime` 1 (minute) and `cleanupInterval` 15000 (15 s): a raid ending at +1 min is closed between +3:00 and +3:15, inside an outage of 5 minutes from about +0:00 with 1:45 to spare. Change the times below if those settings change.
12. **Take the network away (Matt: Wi-Fi off) right after the `/end` of step 11, for 5 minutes**, then Wi-Fi on. Not shorter (review r0 B7): with the network gone an open socket is given up only as a zombie, about 82 seconds after Discord's last acknowledgement, and DNS fails only while reconnecting, so a short outage may never be seen. Do nothing on Discord meanwhile (Discord cannot reach the bot).
13. **The log of the outage** (`bot-8xh-1.log`; the first shard error can come up to about 2 minutes after Wi-Fi off, see step 12): one `discord shard error` at warn (ENOTFOUND, Windows' DNS) and, for an outage over a minute, `discord shard errors repeated` at most once a minute with a count; no `discord shard error` at ERROR, no line per reconnection. The raid's end (about +1:00): its showing with Ended, `could not update a party post` at warn. Its archive warning (about +2:00): `could not do what the clock asked for` at warn; that warning is lost, not sent again after the outage (not caught up by design, review F8). Its closing (about +3:00 to +3:15): `could not show an ended party one last time` and `could not archive the post of an ended party` at warn. A post update during the outage: `could not update a party post` at warn (not ERROR). No ERROR line for the network at all.
14. **The return:** `discord gateway back` at info with `awayMs` (about the outage), `shardErrors` and `unsaid`; then `closed ended parties again` at info (`redone` 1: the raid that ended meanwhile, the upkeep's first step, review r0 B4) and `caught up` with `ran` holding at least `posts`, `upkeep`, `stop posts`, and the feeds under `notOwed` unless one of their reads came during the outage (then under `ran`, with the feed's own read line: `raid bosses read, unchanged` at debug, or its info line). Between them the debug lines of the upkeep: `swept the posts of ended parties` (archived, forums) and `read the emoji of the application`.
15. **What the members see:** the raid that ended during the outage: its post shows no controls, has the Ended tag (set by the redo also where the clock's showing with Ended failed, review F2) and is archived (the forum's open posts no longer list it); no archive warning stands in it (none was sent, step 13); the summary of #north-side no longer lists it. A post that a change during the outage could not reach is current (sign up on the train during the outage is not possible; instead, in step 11, set something with the bot's own clock in it that falls due during the outage, such as a meeting time; the raid's own end shows it too) and the summary's counts are right.
16. **A routine resume** (if the log shows one during the run, `discord gateway resumed` at debug): no `caught up` follows it. A routine resume (a second or two away, no shard error) logged as `discord gateway back` is a failure (review r0 B1). Not a failure: `discord gateway back` with `newSession` true after a connection reset (ECONNRESET on a live connection, a second or two away): discord.js comes back from it with a new session, whose missed events are lost, so it counts as real and is caught up (review F7).
17. **A short outage** (Wi-Fi off for 10 seconds): either nothing at all (the socket survived: no heartbeat was missed) or a real return with shard errors, `discord gateway back` and `caught up` (only `upkeep`, `posts`, `stop posts` under `ran`, the rest not owed). Both pass; neither may log an ERROR line (review r0 B7).
18. **Stop during a catch-up** (optional): Ctrl+C right after `discord gateway back`: the stop finishes cleanly (no crash marker at the next start), `caught up` may be cut short. The party clock's step (6 s) starts no job of the catch-up more and waits for the upkeep, which starts no step more and whose closings end between parties; a feed's read under way ends at that feed's stop step, a drawing of the stops' pinned posts at `posts of the stops`; the step `catch-up` (2 s) after that one waits for the job left (review r0 B6, r1 C3). `shutdown step finished` for `party clock` and `catch-up`, no `shutdown step failed` (`… timed out after … ms`) for either.

## Notes

- In production the watchdog restarts the bot after about seven minutes without the gateway: the catch-up covers outages of about 30 seconds to 7 minutes there. In Docker (Linux) a DNS outage is EAI_AGAIN, which gives no shard error at all: the return is real by its length or a new session.
- Not caught up by design: DMs that failed meanwhile (dropped, as always), lines in posts that failed, the clock's archive warning and the completion question that failed (the clock asks each once; the closing comes all the same, review F8), interactions Discord could not deliver.
