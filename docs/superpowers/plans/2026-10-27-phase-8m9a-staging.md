# Phase 8M-9A staging checklist: the Report button, max battles and Power Spots on the map

Every step needs Matt's go-ahead. Matt starts the bot and the tunnel; Claude drives Discord and the map in its own tab, Matt the phone steps.

- **Accounts:** Googer (owner, staff), Tester (a member).
- **Brief:** `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-27-phase-8m9a-report-and-max/design.md` (its last section, "After the builder check", A1 to A11).
- **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-web-map-polish-design.md` ("Reporting", "Markers", "The list").
- **Conventions:** `docs/architecture/web.md` ("The Report button", "Max battles on the map", "The map's frame").
- **Devices:** a desktop browser (light and dark, and narrowed to 768 and 1000 pixels), an Android phone and an iPhone if at hand, Discord's in-app browser and the phone's own.
- **Compare with Discord:** every report beside its command on Discord: the same pick lists, questions, words and answers (`/raid`, `/max`, `/wild`, `/train`, `/meetup`).

## Before

0. **The page built, the bot started after it.** `npm run web:build`, then start (or restart) the bot: the bot serves the map's page from `dist/web/assets/` and reads `dist/web/assets.json` once at startup.
1. **No migration, no setting, no command change:** no deploy needed. `settings.web.publicUrl` is `https://dev.example.com` and the tunnel `pine-dev` runs. Max battles on (always), Power Spots of West kept (the Wayfarer import).
2. **Parties:** Googer reports with `/max` a Dynamax battle at a Power Spot of West and a Gigantamax battle at a maps link far from any Power Spot (an unlisted power spot); a raid at a gym of West; Tester signs up for the Dynamax one.
3. **The Gyms layer's default.** In a browser that never opened the map (a private window): the Gyms layer is off ("More" on a wide screen, the phone's sheet), raids are drawn. In the browser that kept it on before the update: still on.

## Max battles on the map (A1, A2)

4. **Drawn as a raid.** At the Power Spot: the boss's picture in a ring of Dynamax's color, the time badge ("38m"), the green count; the Gigantamax battle at its point in Gigantamax's color. Light and dark. The spot's own dot is not drawn under the battle (Power Spots layer on, zoom 16). Turn the Raids layer off: both battles go, the spot's dot comes back.
5. **Their last minutes.** A battle with under five minutes left is dimmed, its badge counting down every 15 s without a reload.
6. **The popup is the post.** Tap the Dynamax battle: its post as on Discord (heading, Where with the Power Spot, the trainers, the buttons), the member's part, the map's line; Join, Here, Done, Remote, +1/−1, Group…, Meet…, Report…, and as Googer Delete…, each doing what the post's button does (compare on Discord). On a phone: the card, its summary, swiped up for the whole post.
7. **The list.** "Raids and max battles · n": the battles among the raids, named by their post's title ("Dynamax Machamp (2★)"), their place, distance, "38m left", "1 going"; Soonest puts a battle ending first first; hovering a line rings its marker; tapping it flies there and opens it.
8. **A train at the spot.** A train whose current stop is the Power Spot with the battle: its ring goes round the battle's circle. In route mode, tapping the battle's marker adds the spot as a stop (no popup).

## A Power Spot's popup and the max battle form (A3, A9)

9. **The popup.** Power Spots on, zoom 16, tap a spot without a battle: its name and "Report max battle here"; while the map is read-only (Discord away for the bot): the name alone. Logged out: no Power Spots at all (a layer for members; not offered in "More" or the phone's sheet). Raids layer off: the Dynamax battle's spot shows its dot again, and a tap on it opens the battle (on its own marker, the raids drawn while it shows), not the spot's popup (review B1); the layer still off once it closes.
10. **The form.** "Report max battle here": the bosses grouped by tier in `/max`'s order, the featured one first and marked "(featured)" while an event is on; "Another boss (type its name)" shows the name and its tier; "Ends in" empty (the usual end, as `/max` without `end:`). Compare with `/max`'s choices at the same moment.
11. **A report.** Pick a boss, Report: "Reported: …" in `/max`'s words (no post named), the form closes and the new battle's popup opens, also with the Raids layer off (review B1: on its own marker, the raids drawn while it shows); the post appears on Discord. The same for a raid reported from a gym's popup with the Raids layer off.
12. **The ending-soon question.** Within five minutes of a rotation (or of the last battle time), report a new battle without an end: the form asks the dialog's question in its words, its choices as `/max`'s dialog offers them ("until the next rotation" first, checked), the zone hint when the browser's zone is not the server's; the focus on the checked choice, a screen reader reading the question with it (review B4). Pick one, Report: made with that end. Again at another spot: check the second choice (the end about to pass) and wait past that end without reporting, then Report: "That battle has ended meanwhile: nothing was reported." under the form (read out, `aria-live`), the form drawn from fresh lists without the question, the typed end offered again, the focus on Report (review B4); Report again: the battle is made with `/max`'s usual end of now, without the question (that end no longer within five minutes; past the last battle time, `/max`'s refusal); within five minutes of that one it would be asked again with the choices of then, the first checked (review B3). The browser's clock decides nothing (review R1-5).
13. **Asked again after a refusal (A3).** With the form open and a boss picked, take that boss off its tier on Discord with `/admin boss hide pokemon:<the boss> tier:<its max tier, as the form groups it, e.g. Dynamax 2★>` (it hides a boss the feed lists; `remove` only takes an override back, so it cannot), Report: "That boss is no longer on the list: pick it again." under the form, the boss menu drawn from the new list without that boss, back at "Pick the boss", the focus on Report (review B4). Pick "Another boss (type its name)": a name that names no Pokémon, Report: `/max`'s words in the map's, "I know no Pokémon called "…". Pick a boss from the list.", the focus on the name typed (R2-3); a boss on no list typed without its tier, Report: "Which tier? Pick the tier of this boss.", the focus on the tier menu (R1-4). Afterwards `/admin boss remove` with the same Pokémon and tier puts the hidden boss back.
14. **Another boss there.** Report a different boss at the spot of the Dynamax battle: the refusal in `takenWords`, the battle there opened.
15. **Barred.** As a member kept out of West's forum: the report says the server's words ("You cannot report a max battle at this spot: you are kept out of the forum its battle would be in.") under the form, from an answer that has no text but its `error`.

## The Report button (A7, A8)

16. **Where it is.** Wide screen: "Report" in the header before "Find a gym"; at 768 and 1000 pixels the header is still one row (the title says "Pine" up to 1024). Phone (360 and 375 wide): a round "Report" over the map at the bottom right above "Show where I am", nothing scrolls sideways, the header one row; the list ("List") has a "Report" line at its top that shows the map and opens the menu. Logged out: no Report anywhere; read-only: none, the list's line too, also with a new train's start form left open in the pane (review B2).
17. **Step 1.** The menu: "What are you reporting?", Raid, Max battle, Wild spawn, Train, Meetup, each with its icon (the game's raid icon and Poké Ball load, outlined, light and dark; Pine's own burst, engine and two people); a sheet on a phone, a small panel under the button on a wide screen. Keyboard: the focus on Raid, Tab stays inside, Escape closes it and gives the focus back to Report; Cancel and a tap beside it close it. With a form open: "Close the form that is open first.". With the phone card open: Report closes it.

## Step 2 and step 3 (A4, A5, A6)

18. **A raid.** Report → Raid: the side pane (docked under the map on a phone) says "Report a raid", "Tap a gym on the map to report its raid.", the five nearest gyms with their distances (from your location once shared, else the map's middle), ← Back and Cancel; every gym drawn and ringed whatever the Gyms layer, the raids drawn even with the Raids layer off (C1), the battles, other places, rings and lines dimmed (a tap on a dimmed battle does nothing, review B7); the focus on "Report a raid" (a screen reader reads the box). Tap a gym (or its line): the gym's popup opens with the report form already open, the focus on its boss menu; report it as with `/raid`. A gym with a raid (its marker, or its line): the raid's popup opens instead, also with the Raids layer off. ← Back: the menu again; Cancel or Escape: the gyms and raids go back to what the layers say, the focus back on Report; Escape with a popup open closes the popup alone, and on a phone with the card open (a raid tapped meanwhile) the card alone (review B7). Discord away meanwhile (read-only): step 2 ends by itself.

Tap a spot (or its line): its popup with the form open. Tap the battle's marker (or the line of its spot): the battle opens. Each spot offers a target of 16 pixels round its dot (a thumb on a phone).
