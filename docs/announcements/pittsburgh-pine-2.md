# Pine 2 in Pittsburgh: the announcement

The text for `#bot-updates`, as two Discord messages. It is a draft: reword it as you like.

## How to post it

1. Copy the first block below (the copy button is at its top right) and paste it into the message box of `#bot-updates`. Then the second block, as a second message right after the first. Together they are too long for one message.
2. Before sending each one, **retype the three channel names and the bot's mention** and pick them from Discord's list, so that they become links: `#squirrelbreeze-board`, `#pogo-events`, `#feedback-n-at`, and `@Professor Pine` (pick the bot itself, not a role of the same name).
3. Keep each message under Discord's 2,000 characters. **The first one has about 20 characters to spare**, so if you add to it, move a section into the second message (it has about 1,000 to spare).

`__**…**__` shows as an underlined bold heading, and the `#` line as a large title. Slash commands in backticks are shown as code, which is how the tutorial writes them too.

After the post, a moderator runs `/admin tutorial` in `#raid-bot-tutorial` to put up the new tutorial.

## Message 1

```md
# Professor Pine is back from a long slumber!

Attention Trainers,

Professor Pine dozed off over a stack of raid reports, and woke up to a lab rebuilt around him. The `!` commands are gone: everything is a slash command or a button now. Here is what's new.

__**Every raid has its own post**__
🗂️ Raids are posts in your region's board, right next to its channel (for example #squirrelbreeze-board). The board's pinned post lists them all. No more channel per raid!
📸 Post a screenshot in your regional channel and mention @Professor Pine, or attach it to `/raid`: he reads the gym, the boss and the timer for you. Forgot the mention? Hold the message (right-click on a computer) and pick Apps › Read screenshot.
✍️ Or type `/raid` and pick the boss, the gym and the time from the lists.

__**Buttons instead of reactions**__
Interested, Join, Here, Done and Leave are buttons on the post, with the icons you know. ⬆️ ⬇️ change how many you bring, Remote says you are raiding remotely, Meet… sets your group's time and Group… moves you to another group.

__**Max battles**__
⚔️ `/max` reports a Dynamax or Gigantamax battle at a Power Spot, with a post and buttons just like a raid. `/counters` suggests a team.

__**Trains and meetups**__
🚂 `/train` starts a raid or max battle train with a post of its own. `/route add` lays out its stops, and the conductor moves it along. Trains can be planned days ahead.
🎉 `/meetup` is for trades, lure parties or playing as a group. The moderators may check the place first.

__**More to report**__
📋 `/research` and `/rocket` report field research and Team GO Rocket at PokéStops, gathered in each board's "Today's Stops".
🐾 `/wild` reports a rare spawn.
📅 #pogo-events lists the game's events, with a post for each one coming up.

__**Get notified**__
🎯 `/want` a Pokémon, a raid tier, a research reward or Team GO Rocket, and ⭐ `/target` a gym: Pine sends you a DM when one is reported. `/settings` holds the rest of your pings and DMs.
```

## What members may ask

- **"Where did my raid channel go?"** Raids are posts in each region's board now; the board's pinned post lists what is on.
- **"Do I have to mention the bot?"** No: anyone can use Apps › Read screenshot on a message with a screenshot, also on somebody else's.
- **"What does Pine keep about me?"** The [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md) says it in plain words, and how to have it removed. The [Terms of Service](../legal/terms-of-service.md) are short.
