# Meetup safety

A meetup at a place that might be private is held for a moderator before anything is posted (plan 8S-1; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-meetup-safety-design.md`, Matt, 2026-10-17): the check of a place, the requests and the moderators' posts with their decisions, expiry, place rulings, blocks and appeals. Read before changing `/meetup`, the check, or anything under `src/domain/meetups/`, `src/services/meetup-safety.ts`, `src/components/meetup-safety.ts` or `src/components/meetup-appeal.ts`.

## The check

- `meetupPlaceVerdict(check)` (`src/domain/meetups/safety.ts`, pure) answers `allowed`, `review`, `refused` or `blocked` from: whether the safety is `on`, the place (8D's `Place`: a gym, PokéStop or Power Spot as a snapshot, or a pin), whether the maker is `staff` (`isStaff`: admins, moderators, the owner), whether they are `blocked`, the server's `ruling` of the place, and the `flaggedWords`. In this order: off or staff, allowed (staff are never blocked); blocked; a pin, review (a pin has no ruling, and one given is ignored: an approval of a pin is for that meetup alone); a ruling `refuse`, refused; a ruling `allow`, allowed; a flagged title or other name (`otherTitles`), review; else allowed.
- `MeetupSafety.verdict({ serverId, place, makerId, staff })` (`src/services/meetup-safety.ts`) is the shared check: it reads the block and the ruling from the storage (nothing for staff or while the safety is off), and for a gym its official name and nickname (`MeetupSafetyOptions.gyms`), either of which flags it. `/meetup`'s guard asks it; the making itself goes through `MeetupSafety.make` (below), which plan 8M-5M (meetups from the web map) calls too, and which may end in "sent to the moderators" (`held`) instead of a post.
- Flagged words: `DEFAULT_FLAGGED_WORDS` (little free library, apartment(s), apts, apt, condo(s), condominium(s), townhome(s), townhouse(s), residence, private, salon, studio, LLC, daycare; not "home of", which public signs say) and `settings.meetupSafety.flaggedWords`, in lower case, each once (`flaggedWordsOf`); a word matches the place's title as a whole word (no letter or digit beside it; the words of a phrase across any white space), whatever the letter case (`flaggedWordIn`; the words of a list are one expression, made once per list). A gym's title is the name its post shows (the nickname first); its other name is checked too.
- Rulings are kept per server by the place's kind and id (`rulingKeyOf`: a gym's number as text, a Wayspot's Wayfarer id); a pin has none.
- The safety is on only where `settings.channels.moderation` names a channel (optional, blank unset, without `#`, never a name another channel of `settings.channels` has but `mod-bot-lab`, the moderators' own, which only staff see (Matt, 2026-11-02): the settings refuse the others, since the bot posts other things there, the public bot updates among them). Unset: every place is allowed and nothing is stored; the start logs a warning (`MeetupSafety.warnAtStart`: said once at start since plan 8M-11A, the setting being the instance's; per server for a server that lacks the channel or where the bot may not post in it) and `cutover-check` warns (`moderation` line).

## `/meetup`

- `/meetup name near start [end]` (the first three required since 8S-1; `end` optional since 2026-11-07, read from the start as `/end` reads a meetup's end, `typedNewEnd` over `typedTimedTime`, refused before the form when it cannot be read or is not after the start, the last in `/end`'s words, `end-before-start`; `none` is no end; `PartyService.createTimed` answers a refusal of the domain other than `bad-name` as `cannot-make` with its reason, never as a bad name) is refused by its guards for everything that can be known before the form, so that a refusal does not count: no region or forum, a maker somebody keeps out of that forum (plan 8Z-2: `forumBarred` in `requirePlaceForNew`, the question the map's route asks before it makes one; "You cannot make a meetup here: you are kept out of the forum its post would be in.", `newPartyBarred`; a forum only hidden until they join something in it lets them make it; refused in words of its own while Discord cannot say; asked again by `MeetupSafety.make` at the making, `kept-out` in the same words and `forum-unknown`, which gives the use back, since the form stays open up to half an hour, fix round F-A4), a bad name, a member blocked in the server (`MeetupSafety.blocked`, asked before the place and the start are read, so that a blocked member hears of the block and not of a place or start they mistyped: `NO_MEETUPS_FOR_YOU`, with an Appeal button: `blockedRefusal`, a `UserError` with `answer`, which the router shows privately; the same before the form, on its submit and after a meetup made meanwhile), a place `namedPlace` cannot take, a start `typedTimedTime` cannot read for a meetup still to be made (`newPartyTimes('meetup', now)`), and the verdict (asked with `blocked: false`, the block not read twice): `refused` (`NO_MEETUP_HERE`, "A meetup can't be held there."), `review` with a start less than two hours ahead (`MEETUP_TOO_SOON`). Past the cap of a member's open trains and meetups it is refused too (`openPartiesFull`, plan 8Z, after the block; requests waiting count, approvals are never refused; `parties-and-posts.md`). Its throttle (`MEETUP_THROTTLE`, two a quarter of an hour) counts the command, so a form left unsent counts too, and requests count like meetups; a form whose work fails gives the use back (plan 8S-3: `carriesOn`; an unexpected error, a post the bot may not make, `MEETUP_NOT_SENT`: `discord-framework.md`), on the map too.
- The guard keeps what it read for `execute` (a `WeakMap` by the interaction), so nothing is searched or checked twice before the form must open. The command then opens the form of the information (`meetupForm`, `infoInput` shared with `/info`: required here, at least one character; information of nothing but white space is refused, `NO_INFORMATION`), keeping what it was used with in a `PendingRequests` under the form's token for half an hour (`meetup:<token>:form`; a form open across a restart expires). On the submit (`meetupFormControls`) the reply is deferred privately and `MeetupSafety.make` does the rest, the one entry point for making a meetup with the safety (8M-5M's meetups from the web map call it too: the caller says whether the maker is `staff` and counts the use against its own throttle; a caller without Discord's answers has no Appeal button for a blocked maker and points them to `/meetup`). What the form does with the outcome is `makeMeetupAs` (each outcome in `/meetup`'s words), which the map calls too (`web.md`, "Meetups on the map"), and `refusalOf` is the verdict's refusal for `/meetup`'s guard and the map alike. It refuses a name or information a meetup cannot have (`bad-name`, `bad-description`, white space alone too), asks the verdict again (a ruling or block may have come meanwhile: `refused`, `blocked`), then: allowed, `PartyService.createMeetup` with the start and information (`MeetupRequest.startTime`, `.description`; the meetup is made with them, so one post and no line announcing a start), and `closeIfBlocked` after it (`posted`, with the party); review, `submit` (`held`, the member told `MEETUP_HELD`, never why; or `too-soon`, `not-sent`, `blocked`); `createMeetup`'s own refusals (`no-region`, `no-forum`, `no-post`) come out as they are. `MeetupSafety.closeIfBlocked({ serverId, makerId, partyId, staff })` looks for a block once more after a meetup is made (one that came after the check, whose sweep of the maker's meetups ran before this one was there; never for staff): it closes the meetup as Block member does (`closed`; `close-failed` when that fails, logged, the meetup still up; `false` when not blocked or the block cannot be read), and the member gets the blocked answer. Refusals of the form give the typed information back (`refusedWithText`).
- A meetup is never at a Power Spot (Matt, 2026-10-17: they are poorly curated, and many are on private property such as home businesses; the map offers "Hold meetup here" at gyms and PokéStops alone, whatever the safety will say of one, asked when it is sent): `/meetup near:` offers, finds and names a pin by gyms and PokéStops alone (`MEETUP_PLACE_KINDS`, `PlaceKindsOnly` in `src/commands/place-option.ts`) and refuses a picked Power Spot with `NO_POWER_SPOT`; `MeetupSafety.make` answers `power-spot` for one, staff too, before anything else, so the web map is held to it as well. A meetup stored before at a Power Spot stays as it is, and `meetupPlaceVerdict` still takes the kind.

## Requests and the moderators' post

- `MeetupSafety.submit` sends the moderators' post into the channel `findChannel(regionChannelId, moderation)` finds, then stores the request with it (a UUID, `MeetupRequestRecord`, `src/domain/meetups/records.ts`; a request is never kept without its post); whatever fails (no channel, Discord refuses, the record: the post is then taken back) leaves nothing behind (`not-sent`: the member is told to try later, the reason is logged). A name or information a meetup cannot have is `bad-name` / `bad-description`. Once the request is kept, the maker's block is read again: a block that came after the check, whose sweep of the maker's pending requests ran before this one was kept, withdraws it at once (`withdrawn`, its post says so, no DM; `blocked`, the member gets the blocked answer); a block that cannot be read leaves the request to the moderators (an approval reads it again). A request expires at `requestExpiry(createdAt, startTime)`: 48 hours, or an hour before the start when that is sooner; one that would leave the moderators less than an hour is not made (`too-soon`). In rehearsal mode meetup safety gets a gateway of its own (`rehearsalModeratorsGateway`, `src/app.ts`) that lets its posts (requests and appeals) into a text channel of the moderators' channel's name, so the staff who rehearse see them (Matt, 2026-10-17); the rest of the bot goes through the rehearsal gateway, which lets nothing into that channel: the exception belongs to meetup safety's sends, not to the channel.
- The post (`requestMessage`, `src/render/meetup-safety.ts`): `### Meetup waiting for approval`, the name, the start, the end when there is one (`MeetupRequestRecord.endTime`, migration 047, null without one; an approval makes the meetup with it), who asked (`memberText`), "on the map" for a request of the live map (`MeetupRequestRecord.source`, `map` or null; `RequestAsk.source`; migration 021, plan 8M-5M), in which region channel, **Where** (`nearLine`: kind, title and address cut to 100 and 200 characters, Map; a pin as `pinLine`; the Wayspot's picture beside it), **Information** (`plainLines`, never cut), when it expires, and the buttons: Approve, Approve once, Refuse, Refuse from now on, Block member (a pin: Approve once, Refuse, Block member). Custom ids `msafety:<request id>:<decision>`.
- The buttons (`meetupSafetyControls`, `src/components/meetup-safety.ts`) are for staff (`requireManagement`; others are refused privately and uncounted) with a throttle of their own (`SAFETY_THROTTLE`). The handler defers privately and asks `MeetupSafety.decide`, which reads the request from the database (a post from before a restart works): one conditional update `pending` → decided (`decideRequest`; Block member `blockMaker`), so the first press wins and a later one is told who decided what. Approve and Approve once read the maker's block again (one that came meanwhile wins: the request becomes `withdrawn`, its post says so, no meetup, no DM), then make the meetup as its maker asked (`createMeetup`, the maker's name and team as they were when they asked; a failure marks the request `failed` and says why to the moderator and the maker, and in the post for the other moderators: plan 8S-4, Matt's ruling of 2026-10-18; the reason is kept with the decision as a kind, `MeetupRequestRecord.failure` (`ApprovalFailure`: `bad-name`, `bad-description`, `no-region`, `no-forum`, `no-post`, `error`, `kept-out` (plan 8Z-2, fix round F-A4: somebody keeps the maker out of the forum its post would be in by the approval, `forumBarred` asked before the meetup is made; "its maker is kept out of the forum its post would be in", the maker's DM "you are kept out of the forum its post would be in" with nothing to try again; since 8Z-2 final fix F5 `decide` asks `forumBarred` for Approve and Approve once before the decision is stored, and while Discord cannot say (`forum-unknown`) stores nothing: the request stays open, its post and its buttons as they were, nobody is told by DM, and the moderator is answered `unchecked`, "I could not check with Discord just now whether its maker may post in the forum, so nothing is decided yet. Press **Approve** (or **Approve once**) again in a moment."; until then that became `error` after the decision was kept, and one failed member fetch ended the request), or `posts-refused` with the forum and what the bot lacks there), worded by `approvalFailureWords` (`src/render/meetup-safety.ts`; the post names the forum, the moderator's answer and the DM say "its forum"), so a post drawn anew from the row says it too; a row from before, or a kept reason that does not read, keeps the line without it), and `closeIfBlocked` after it (`closed`: the request is `withdrawn` as above; `close-failed`: the meetup is up, so the approval is settled with it and its post shows it, the place ruled as for any approval, the failure logged, and the maker, blocked, gets no DM of the approval); Approve allows the place for good only once the meetup is made (`settleApproval`, then `rule`), Refuse from now on refuses it. A meetup made whose record fails is written once more (`settleApproval` retried). Every step after the decision is caught and logged (also the first look for a block, which `closeIfBlocked` repeats), so the post is always edited to the outcome without buttons and the maker always told by DM through `DmSender` (`requestOutcomeDm`: approved with Go to post, not approved, expired; never why).
- Expiry runs with every tick of the party clock, in the background and one at a time (`PartySchedulerOptions.requests`, `MeetupSafety.expire`), also while the safety is off (requests made before it was turned off end): pending requests past `expiresAt` become `expired`, their posts edited, their makers told (a due row that cannot be read becomes `failed`, logged once); approvals left without their recorded meetup for five minutes (`strandedApprovals`) are settled with the meetup when one is active (the maker's, in the server, with the request's name and start: the bot stopped or the database failed after it was made; no DM; closed and `withdrawn` when the maker was blocked since), else become `failed`, posts edited, makers told; open appeals that are stale are closed (below). A press on a request whose time is up expires it instead of deciding it ("This request has expired: nobody decided in time.").

## After the approval (plan 8Z)

- **Who changes a meetup's text:** `/name` and `/info` of a meetup, on the command, the Submit of `/info`'s form and the map's `name` and `info` changes, ask `mayEditMeetup` (`src/domain/party/meetup.ts`: its maker or staff, `isStaff` / `isMapStaff`, no sign-up needed), then the block (`MeetupSafety.blocked`, never staff). On Discord that is `meetupChangeRefusal` (`src/commands/meetups/meetup-changes.ts`), through the guard `requireMeetupChanger`, read within a dialog's time (`readInTime`), and again at the Submit, the typed text given back. A blocked maker gets `BLOCKED_FROM_CHANGING` with Appeal… on the command, in words pointing to `/meetup` at the Submit and on the map (a blocked maker can only have a meetup left whose closing failed). No control opens either command. A train's `/name` is left to anybody in the post.
- **Who changes a meetup's times (plan 8Z-2; Matt, 2026-11-10):** its start and end are its maker's and staff's on every surface, signed up or not; a member who is only signed up is refused (the audit's M1: `/end` asked nothing and `/meet` and `/cancel-meet` only a sign-up, while Manage… and the map asked more). The rule is the domain's, asked inside the store: `setStartTime` and `setTimedEnd` (`src/domain/party/timed.ts`) take who asks as `{ id, staff }` and refuse through `timesRefusal` (a meetup: `mayEditMeetup`, `not-yours`, worded `NOT_YOUR_MEETUP`; a train keeps its rule, the start for who is signed up and its maker, the end for anybody), so no surface can differ; `createMeetup` sets an end as its maker. The block is a read, so it is asked before the write by every surface through the same `meetupChangeRefusal`: `/meet`, `/cancel-meet` and `/end` by the guard `requireMeetupChanger` (before `/meet`'s own guard, so "not yours" is said before a time is read, and such a use does not count), Manage… and the map by `meetupChangeCheck` and `meetupTimesCheck` (`src/commands/meetups/meetup-manage.ts`). A meetup's start asks no sign-up anywhere any more (`mayArrange` is the trains' alone): Manage… asks the start of whoever may open it, and the map's `panel.start` is always true.
- **The moderators are told:** `renameAs` and `describeAs`, and since plan 8Z-2 `startAs`, `endAs` and `setTimesAs` (`src/commands/parties/timed.ts`: the paths of `/meet`, `/cancel-meet`, `/end`, Manage… and the map), call `MeetupSafety.tellChange` on a change that changed something, from Discord and the map alike; `setTimesAs` (both times of Manage… and of the map's `…/times`) tells once, of what went through, the start first, at once rather than with the start's gathered announcement. It does not wait: the answer goes out at once, what fails is logged (`flushChanges` for tests). "Approved" is a `MeetupRequest` of status `approved` whose `partyId` is the meetup (`approvedRequestOf`, by server and maker, the columns of the index). The notice (`meetupChangeNotice`) goes into the request's channel through meetup safety's own gateway, so it works in rehearsal: who, the text as it is now or the time as it is now ("set its start: …", "removed its end time."), Go to post and Go to request. Staff's own changes are told too. While the safety is off nothing is read.

## Blocks and appeals

- Block member refuses the request (`decision` `block`) and blocks its maker in that server in one transaction (`blockMaker`, read committed: no gap locks, so two Blocks of one maker do not deadlock on a gap; a deadlock that still comes, on a deleted row InnoDB has not purged yet, runs `addBlock` and `blockMaker` again, `retryOnDeadlock`, staging of 8S-2; it never joins a caller's transaction: `inTransaction` throws when an isolation level is asked for inside one; `MeetupBlock`, one row per server and member, blocked again in place of a lifted one: only the last lift is kept, `liftedBy`, `liftedByName`, `liftedAt`), then, each step caught and logged so that the post and the DM always come, withdraws their other pending requests there (status `withdrawn`, posts edited, no DM each), closes their active meetups there as `/delete` does with `CLOSED_BY_MODERATORS` ("This meetup was closed by the moderators.", `PartyService.end` with `lastWords`), and DMs them (`blockedDm`) that they may appeal with `/meetup`, only when the block is new (`newBlock`): two Blocks give one DM.
- A blocked member's `/meetup` answers with Appeal (`mappeal:<server>:open`), which opens the form of an appeal (`appealForm`, "Why should the block be lifted?", `mappeal:<server>:form`) unless the member may not appeal now (`appealState`: not blocked, one appeal open, or within `APPEAL_WAIT_DAYS` (7) after a block was kept); an open appeal that is stale (its post never recorded after five minutes, or open for seven days) is closed as `expired` then (its post edited without waiting: the form must open within three seconds), and by the sweep (which edits its post and DMs the member, the server named through `PartyGateway.guildName`, that they may appeal again). The presses of Appeal count as reading (`MEMBER_READ_THROTTLE`); the submit (three an hour, `APPEAL_THROTTLE`) stores the appeal (one open per member and server: a unique `openKey`) and posts it to the moderators' channel (`appealMessage`: the member, who blocked them and when, their words) with Lift block and Keep block (`mappeal:<appeal id>:lift|keep`, staff only, first press wins; Lift block decides the appeal and lifts the block in one transaction, `liftOnAppeal`); the seven days are checked again once the appeal is stored (a keep that came in between discards it). The post keeps who blocked them after a Lift (the block is read before it is lifted) and says when they may appeal again after a Keep; the member is told by DM, the server named (`appealOutcomeDm`). Lifting never restores refused requests or closed meetups. An appeal pressed when no block stands any more (lifted otherwise) is closed as lifted, nobody told (`no-block`).
- A block also refuses `/max` a new battle at a pin, an "Unlisted power spot" (Matt, 2026-10-21; joining the battle there is allowed, and staff are never blocked): in plain words (`MAX_PIN_BLOCKED`), without an Appeal button, the block appealed with `/meetup` as above (`parties-and-posts.md`, the max battle at a pin). Likewise a spawn at a pin (plan 8Z; `blockedAtPin`, whichever kind of place it is asked of): `/wild near:` refuses a pin in its guard, uncounted (`WILD_PIN_BLOCKED`), a known place or none still theirs; `POST /api/spawns` refuses before its limit (403 `MAP_SPAWN_BLOCKED`), and since every spawn on the map is a pin, a blocked member reports none there.

- Block member stays on the post of a request refused (not by a block) or expired while its maker is not blocked (`blockableLater`, 8S-1 review W7; the post reads the maker's block each time it is drawn, `makerBlock`): a press blocks them as from a pending request (`addBlock` and the same sweep, `afterBlock`, one DM; `blocked-later`, or blocked already), and the post then says who blocked them. Every moderators' post names a gym's official name beside the nickname its title shows (`officialName`, `placeLine`: "(gym; officially **Frog Fountain**)").

## Asks about refused places

- A place refused for good (`NO_MEETUP_HERE`) gets **Ask the moderators** under `/meetup`'s refusal (`placeRefusal`, `src/commands/meetups/place-ask.ts`; the guard's refusal only, a ruling that came while the form was open is said plainly) once the refusal, or its last Keep refused, is `PLACE_ASK_WAIT_DAYS` (14) old (`askableAt`, counted per place, `keptAt`); while an ask of the place is open the refusal adds "It is with the moderators already."; a blocked member is refused by the block first (`askState` and `askAboutPlace` read the block before the place: `blocked`, the block's refusal with Appeal). The web map's refusal has no button: when the place may be asked about it says to ask with `/meetup` on Discord (`ASK_ON_DISCORD`). The button (`mplace:<kind>:open:<place id>`, stateless: the server the presser's, the place by its key, so that it works after a restart; read as reading) opens the form "Why should meetups be allowed here?" (`mplace:<kind>:form:<place id>`, the appeal's throttle) unless `askState` says otherwise (`wait`, `open`, `not-refused`) or the member's asks of the hour are used up (the form's window, checked before it: `checks`, plan 8S-3; Appeal's form likewise); the place of the ask is the ruling's (a ruling of 8S-1 kept none: the gym or kept Wayspot as it is now, `MeetupSafetyOptions.wayspots`). `MeetupSafety.askAboutPlace` keeps the ask (`PlaceAskRecord`, one open per server and place: a unique `openKey`) and posts it (`askMessage`, `src/render/place-asks.ts`: the member, the place with its official name, the ruling (`rulingLine`: who, when, from which request, report or ask), their words) with Allow place and Keep refused (`mplace:<ask id>:allow|keep`, staff, first press wins; `allowOnAsk` decides and rules allow in one step, the ask the ruling's source, locking the ruling's row before the ask's as `undoRuling` and `keepOnAsk` do, so that they never deadlock; `keepOnAsk` decides and sets `keptAt` only while a refusal stands, locked: undone or allowed meanwhile it keeps nothing, `not-refused`, nobody told); the member is told by DM, the server named (`askOutcomeDm`). The ask's post keeps showing the ruling it asked about (plan 8S-3, staging of 8S-2: after Allow place it showed the new ruling, from the ask): `decideAsk` reads the ruling before the decision and draws the post with it, and Undo ruling with the ruling it removed; no migration, since the post is drawn only then and at an expiry (when the ruling is still the one asked about). An ask nobody decides in `ASK_OPEN_DAYS` (7), or never posted, expires in the sweep of `expire()` (post edited, DM: they may ask again).
- A ruling keeps who made it by name, the place, the post it came from (`RulingSource`: a request's on Approve and Refuse from now on, a report's on Refuse place…, an ask's on Allow place) and `keptAt` (`PlaceRulingRecord`, `rulingRecord`); `/places` shows it and undoes it (`moderation.md`).

## Storage

- Migration 019 (`MeetupRequest`, `MeetupPlaceRuling`, `MeetupBlock`, `MeetupAppeal`): servers and members by snowflake without a foreign key, times in epoch milliseconds, a request's place as JSON read through `placeDocument` (`party-state.ts`; a row that does not read is logged and left alone, a due one failed), its post (`channelId`, `messageId`) never null. `isDuplicateEntry(err, key)` (`src/db/errors.ts`) tells the open-appeal key's duplicate from others. `MeetupSafetyRepository` and its twin `MemoryMeetupSafety` (`test/support/meetup-safety.ts`; its maps `rulingRows`, `blockRows`, `requests`, `appeals`, `asks`) run the same cases (`meetupSafetyCases`). Migration 023 (plan 8S-4) adds `MeetupRequest.failure`, nullable JSON (`{"kind":"no-forum"}`, `{"kind":"posts-refused","forumId":"…","missing":["Manage Posts"]}`), written by `settleApproval(id, { failed })` and read through `approvalFailureOf` (`src/domain/meetups/records.ts`), null for every request but a failed one. Migration 022 (plan 8S-2) adds `MeetupPlaceAsk` and, on `MeetupPlaceRuling`, `ruledByName`, `place`, `sourceKind`, `sourceId`, `sourceChannelId`, `sourceMessageId` and `keptAt` (null for a ruling of 019), besides the reports' tables (`moderation.md`); the store lists a server's rulings, pending requests, open asks, blocks and open appeals for `/places`.
- Migration 050 (plan 8Z-2, audit X6) gives the sweeps of each minute an index that begins with the status, since they ask for the open rows of every server and the tables' other indexes begin with the server: `meetupappeal_status_createdat_index` and `meetupplaceask_status_createdat_index (status, createdAt)` for `staleAppeals` and `staleAsks` (`status = 'open'`, oldest first; InnoDB ends each entry with `id`, the order's second column), and `meetuprequest_stranded_index (status, partyId, decidedAt)` for `strandedApprovals` (approved, no party, decided long enough ago: 019's `(status, expiresAt)` finds every approved request, nearly all of which have their party). `2026_11_10_050_sweep_indexes.db.test.ts` checks by `explain` that each sweep's own statement reads along its index beside 3,000 rows of which a dozen are open.
- A request to remove a member's data (plan 8G, the owner's `/admin forget`, `members-and-roles.md`) leaves the moderators what they need: blocks, lifted or not, and rulings are not touched, nor is what the member decided as a moderator. Of the member's own requests, appeals and asks about places, `MeetupSafetyRepository.forgetMember` takes the id and the name (and a request's team) off those that are decided and over, in every server: a request refused, expired, failed or withdrawn, or approved with its meetup made (`partyId`); an appeal lifted, kept or expired; an ask allowed, kept, expired or undone (`REQUEST_OVER`, `APPEAL_OVER`, `ASK_OVER`). Migration 051 lets `makerId` and `memberId` be null for it (Matt's ruling of 2026-11-10). What is open keeps both (a pending request; an approval whose meetup is still to be made, which `createMeetup` makes by that member, with that name and team; an open appeal or ask), and the owner is told to have the moderators decide it first. So `MakerKnown` / `makerKnown` and `memberKnown` (`records.ts`) narrow a record to one with its member, which is all a decision, a block or a DM needs: `decide` answers Block member on an old request without its maker `maker-forgotten` (the moderator hears there is nobody to block) and any other press on it `decided-before`; an appeal or ask without its member is never open; `tell`, `expireAppeal` and `expireAsk` send no DM where there is nobody. `blockableLater` is false without a maker, so such a post shows no Block member.
