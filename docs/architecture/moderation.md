# Moderation: reports, /delete and /places

Members report a party or a single message to the moderators privately, the moderators decide on the report's post, `/delete` is staff's, and `/places` lists the rulings of places and everything that waits for a moderator (plan 8S-2; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-moderation-reports-design.md`, Matt, 2026-10-17). The moderators' channel and meetup safety's requests, rulings, blocks, appeals and asks about places are in `meetup-safety.md`. Read before changing `/report`, "Report to moderators", `/delete`, `/places` or anything under `src/services/moderation-reports.ts`, `src/components/moderation-reports.ts`, `src/render/moderation.ts`, `src/render/places.ts`.

## Reporting

- `/report` (every member, staff and the party's maker too; in a party's post, `requireParty`) opens a form for the reason (`partyReportForm`, "What is wrong?", required, at most `MAX_REPORT_REASON` 1,000; `report:<party id>:party`). "Report to moderators" is a message command (`reportMessageCommand`, `src/commands/parties/report-message.ts`; `discord-framework.md`): on a party's post, a copy of it (`mirrorMessages`) or its link line in the region's channel (`partyOfMessage`) it opens the same form; on any other message of the bot it answers `NOTHING_TO_REPORT` ("Nothing to report there."); on anybody else's message (a member's chat in a party's post too) it keeps what Discord sent of the message (of a forward, which has no text or files of its own, those of its first `messageSnapshots`, marked `forwarded`: "**What it said** (forwarded)") under a token for half an hour (`PendingRequests`) and opens `report:<token>:message`. The forms' handler is `reportControls` (`src/commands/parties/report.ts`): a reason of white space alone is refused by a guard, uncounted; every submit counts in `/report`'s window (`REPORT_THROTTLE`, five an hour, `as: 'report'`, `REPORT_WINDOW` in `report-limit.ts`, which both files read as they load), whichever way the form was opened. Before a form opens (plan 8S-3, staging of 8S-2: the reason typed was lost) `/report` and the Apps menu refuse what the submit would: that window full (`checks`, counting nothing: `discord-framework.md`), and a member whose reason is on the open report of the party or message already (`ModerationReports.reportedBefore` over `ReportStore.openReport(key)`, a quick read raced against `checkTimeoutMs`, a second by default, so that the form still opens within Discord's three seconds: false without the moderators' channel, for a report stranded without its post, and when the read fails or is slower, logged; `REPORT_ALREADY`); the submit's checks stay. A form of the member's old `/delete` (`party:<id>:delete`), open across the deploy, is taken as a report. The live map reports a party the same way (plan 8M-8B, Report… in the map's line, `web.md`): its opening asks what `/report` asks before its form, uncounted, and answers the form's words (`reportFormWords`); its Submit counts in `/report`'s window and calls `reportParty` and `reportAnswer`, pinging the staff roles of `managementRoleIdsOf(guild)` without the moderators' channel (`managementRoleIds(interaction)` calls it).
- `ModerationReports` (`src/services/moderation-reports.ts`, `ctx.reports`) keeps reports in `ReportStore` (`ReportRepository`, migration 022; twin `MemoryReports`, both run `reportCases`). `reportParty` / `reportMessage`: one open report per party and per message (`reportTargetKey`, the unique `openKey`): a reason opens it, or joins the open one (`added`; its post edited, never sent again), unless the member has one there (`already`: "You already reported this."). A reason joins a report only while it is open: the repository finds the open report by its key and locks it by its id (a lock on the key's index too would deadlock with a decision, which changes the key), then inserts the reason (a second one of the same member is `already`); a report decided meanwhile is followed by a new one. A report opened is posted first, then recorded (`setReportPost`), then drawn anew when anything changed while the post was on its way (a reason more; its party ended, looked at once more); a post that fails takes the report back (`not-sent`). The post is always drawn from the report as it is kept: `show(reportId)` reads it just before each edit, one edit at a time per report (a promise tail in a `BoundedMap`), so that an older edit never lands after a newer one. A report left without its post for five minutes (the bot stopped in between) is opened anew by the next reason; the reasons of a report that is gone (stranded, its post failed, or forgotten by the sweep) are carried over to the next report of its subject (`orphans`, in memory: a restart loses them, which is accepted), so that nobody told "Sent" loses their reason while the bot runs; `forgetReporter(memberId)` drops a member's from them, which the owner's `/admin forget` calls once its removal has committed (`members-and-roles.md`). The sweep (`ModerationReports.sweep`, with the appeals' sweep of `PartySchedulerOptions.requests`, so also at start) forgets reports never posted for five minutes and ends the open reports of parties that are over (the bot stopped before the listener saw the end). The subject is checked against its schema before it is kept (`reportSubject`). The reporter is told "Sent to the moderators." and nothing more; nothing is said in the party's post and nobody is pinged. What the post shows of the party is a snapshot (`PartySubject`: the post's title, the brief's lines but its line of where, `PartyBrief.whereLine`, since the post's own **Where** says it with the official name; the region, the place, a gym's official name, the maker, a meetup's information), renewed with every reason and at a decision; of a message a copy (`MessageSubject`: author, channel, time, its text cut to `MESSAGE_COPY_ROOM`, its attachments' names, the party whose post it is in; in a party's post the head names the post once, by the title it had: "Written by **Tester** in the post of **Train · Whole Group (B)**", without the post's mention, plan 8S-3), the first report's, kept when the message is gone and when it is edited (a later report that finds its text or attachments changed marks it `edited`, `messageSeenAgain`, and the post says `EDITED_SINCE`), and shown cut after escaping (1,500 characters of text, a name 100 and all names 600). A member's message form keeps one message per member (a newer form expires the older), so that one member cannot push the others out of the store.
- The sweep's two reads (`openPartyReports`, `unpostedReports`: the open reports of every server, oldest first) go along `moderationreport_status_createdat_index (status, createdAt)` of migration 050 (plan 8Z-2, audit X6); the table's other indexes begin with the server or the party, so each minute's sweep read every report, the decided ones too (`2026_11_10_050_sweep_indexes.db.test.ts` checks the plans).
- A report outlives a request to remove its reporter's data (plan 8G, the owner's `/admin forget`, `members-and-roles.md`): `ReportRepository.forgetReporter` takes the member's id and name off every reason they gave (`reporterId` and `reporterName` null), in every server, an open report's too; the reason's words and the report stay, the moderators' record, and the post says "A former member" for that reason when it is next drawn (`memberTextOrFormer`). Migration 051 (Matt's ruling of 2026-11-10) made this possible: `ModerationReportReason` has a row id of its own as primary key, `reporterId` is nullable, and `moderationreportreason_report_reporter_unique (reportId, reporterId)` keeps one reason per reporter and report (a second is `already` by that key's duplicate), while several forgotten reporters of one report stand side by side. The reasons are read in the order they came (`createdAt`, then the row id). "You already reported this." looks for the member's id and so no longer finds a cleared reason: a forgotten member may report the same party or message again. A report about the member (the party they made, the message they wrote with its copy: `subject`) is not touched, nor is what they decided as a moderator. `heldOf` counts their reasons and the reports about them for the look. The post on Discord is not edited.
- Without `settings.channels.moderation` a party's report goes into its post as a member's `/delete` did (`PartyService.requestDeletion`: the public line, the staff roles pinged by the first that is sent, the reason cut to 500 so that the message with its pings stays within 2,000), and a message cannot be reported (`NO_MODERATORS`).

## The moderators' post and its decisions

- `reportMessage(report)` (`src/render/moderation.ts`): `### Reported meetup` (raid, train, max battle, message), what it is and when, who made it and where ("Reported by" for a raid or a max battle, "Created by" for a meetup or a train; `subject.partyType` is of `PARTY_TYPES`, so a kind added later is read back), **Where** (`placeLine`, a gym's official name beside its nickname), a meetup's **Information**, **Reasons** with every reporter (`memberText`, the words through `plain`; as many whole ones as fit, then "…and N more reasons"), and while open the buttons of `reportActions`: a raid or train Close… and Dismiss; a meetup Close…, Refuse place… (a gym or PokéStop, never a pin), Block maker… and Dismiss; a message Delete message and Dismiss; with Go to post / Go to message while it is there. Decided, it shows who decided what and when, without buttons.
- `reportDecisionControls` (kind `mreport`, `src/components/moderation-reports.ts`): staff alone (`requireManagement`, refused uncounted), `SAFETY_THROTTLE`. An action that ends a party asks first in a dialog (plan 8Q, `removalDialog` in `src/render/moderation.ts`): titled `Close this meetup`, `Refuse this place` or `Block the maker`, today's question as its text, and the required radio group `The post` with nothing picked, Archive post (`Keeps it readable and locked.`) or Delete post (`Removes it with everything written in it: …`); where the bot may not delete the post (`PartyService.mayDeletePost`) there is no choice, the text says the post will be archived and why (the forum named, `channelInDialog`), and the custom id carries `archive`. The report is read within `DIALOG_READ_MS` (`readInTime`, else the slow-read refusal) and one decided already is said at once without a dialog. Its Submit (`mreport:<id>:<action>[:archive]:~<token>`, the per-open token of `removalDialog` left out by `removalOf`; the button Archive post carries none, the same guards and throttle) is acknowledged privately and decided, the answer private; the post is redrawn for everybody as before. `ModerationReports.decide`: one conditional change of the report (`decideReport`, the first press wins; a later one is told who decided what; a question of an action pressed on a decided report says so at once), then its work with the party as it is now (its subject renewed): Refuse place… refuses the place for good with the report's post as the ruling's source (`MeetupSafety.refusePlace`), Block maker… blocks as Block member does (`MeetupSafety.blockMember`: the maker's name kept, one DM only for a new block, the maker's other meetups closed archived, this one left to the end, their pending requests withdrawn), then the party ends as `/delete` ends it (`PartyService.endAs` with "This meetup was closed by the moderators.", `removal`; the history records `cancelled`). Refuse place… on a meetup whose place is now a pin or none changes nothing (`no-place`, said so). Where a step of the work fails, the report is opened again (`reopenReport`: its buttons back) and `failed` tells the moderator what was done before it (a place refused, a maker blocked: they stay, and a second try finds them done) and whether the report is open again (if not, to close the party with `/delete`). `endAs` says how the post went: a deletion Discord refused archives it, which the report keeps as removal `denied` (`setRemoval`; the post says "archived: Discord refused to delete it") and the moderator is told ("archived: Discord refused to delete it; delete it yourself"); a deletion the network failed (`deleting`, plan 8X-H, review r1 C1) is archived for now and deleted by the hourly upkeep (`PartyService.redoClosings`, `parties-and-posts.md`): the report keeps `delete` (its post says "deleted") and the moderator is told `deletionPending` ("its post is archived for now: Discord did not answer when I tried to delete it. I will try again within the hour; if it is still there after that, delete it yourself", `DELETION_PENDING`); a party over between the press and the closing has its post deleted all the same when Delete post was chosen (`PartyService.deletePostOf`). Delete post where the bot lacks Manage Threads (`PartyGateway.mayDeletePost`, from the cache; by Submit, since the dialog offered it) is refused before anything changes (`may-not-delete`): the private answer says so with one button, Archive post (`mreport:<id>:<action>:archive`), which decides at once. The question's Delete post of before plan 8Q (`…:delete`) gets the router's stale answer. Delete message claims the report first (`decideReport` as `deleted`, so a Dismiss at the same moment wins or loses before anything is deleted), then deletes the message (`PartyGateway.deleteReported`: an archived post is opened for the deletion and archived again as it was, locked or not, also when the deletion fails, and only where the bot has Manage Threads there, else `not-allowed` before anything is opened; a refused archiving afterwards is logged and the deletion's result stands; a channel the bot cannot see, Missing Access, is `not-allowed`): `deleted`, `gone` when it was deleted first (`markGone`; the post says so); without Manage Messages the report is opened again and the moderator told to delete it themselves, and a deletion that fails opens it again too (`failed`). In rehearsal mode the rehearsal's gateway deletes a member's message only in the post of a rehearsed party (else `not-allowed`, logged).
- A reported party that ends without a moderator (the clock, its maker, a move) ends its open report (`watch`: a listener of the store, the work on the side, `flush()` in tests): the post says it was over, without buttons. One that staff close with `/delete` ends so too, and then `closeControls` calls `closedByStaff(partyId, removal, by)`: it waits for that work, and the reports it ended (remembered by party) or still open are restated as closed by that moderator with how the post really went (`ReportStore.closeEnded`, conditional on `ended`; `denied` where Discord refused the deletion), so the post reads "Closed by …" and has no Go to post once the post is deleted.
- In rehearsal mode the reports' posts go through meetup safety's gateway (`rehearsalModeratorsGateway`).

## Max battles at a pin

- A max battle reported at an unlisted power spot (a pin) is posted at once, and the moderators' channel near the region channel it was reported in gets a notice of it, for their information (Matt, 2026-10-21, final review G1; `ModerationReports.maxAtPin`, on the side: nothing waits on it, a failure is logged, nothing without `settings.channels.moderation`; in rehearsal mode through the same gateway as the reports' posts). The notice (`maxPinNotice`, `src/render/moderation.ts`): `### Max battle at an unlisted power spot`, the battle, who reported it (`memberText`, anonymous or not: the moderators see who) in which region channel, `📍 **Unlisted power spot** · [Map]`, and the buttons Delete and Go to post, in a neutral colour (`NEWS_COLOUR`: nothing waits on the moderators). Once the battle is over the notice says how, in grey, with Go to post alone (`maxPinNotice(notice, outcome)`, fix review (a)): "This max battle has ended." when the store's listener (`watch`) sees it end, "Closed by <member>." after `closedByStaff`, one edit at a time per battle, staff's the last word; the notice's message is kept per party in memory (`pinNotices`, a `BoundedMap` of 1,000), so after a restart it stays as it was and its Delete answers that the battle has ended. Delete (`maxpin:<party id>:delete`, `maxPinControls` in `src/components/max-pin.ts`: staff only, `requireManagement`, refused uncounted, `SAFETY_THROTTLE`) opens the dialog of `/delete` (`closeDialog`), whose Submit (`closeControls`) closes the battle as `/delete` does; a battle that is over answers "This max battle has ended.". The press reads nothing slow before its dialog (the slow-read rule): a press from another server opens the dialog, and its Submit is a stale control (`closeControls` checks the server). A member blocked from meetups cannot report at a pin (`parties-and-posts.md`).
- A Power Spot Pine may be missing (plan 8X-S, design `2026-10-22-max-battle-screenshots-design.md` 3): a max battle's screenshot posted with a mention or given to `/max screenshot:` whose Power Spot matches none of the server's (not even 80% alike, nor a title whose words all stand in the name read: `matchPowerSpot`'s `unknown`) and whose name was read surely (`SPOT_SURE`) has the moderators' channel near the channel it was posted in told so (`ModerationReports.missingSpot`, on the side): plain text, `missingSpotNote` (`src/render/moderation.ts`): "A screenshot in <#channel> showed the Power Spot "<name>", which Pine does not know. A new Wayspot export may be due (`/gym import`).", the name as read, `plain(truncate(name, 80))`. At most one note per name (`spotNameKey`: letters and digits only, so a lost space is the same name) and server in `MISSING_SPOT_DAYS` (7), kept in memory (`spotsNoted`, a `BoundedMap`; a restart may note a name once more; a note not sent, or refused by Discord, is forgotten, so the next screenshot sends it). It answers whether the staff were told, now or within those days, and only then does the member's refusal add "I have let the staff know Pine may be missing this Power Spot." (review R13); without the moderators' channel (not named, or not there) it is a log line (`a screenshot showed a Power Spot Pine does not know`, with the server and the channel, never the name: review r4 B2) and the refusal says nothing of the staff. Passive reading never sends it (Matt, 2026-10-22).
- Screenshots that keep disagreeing with the boss list (plan 8X-J, design `2026-10-25-max-boss-tier-fix-design.md` section 2): when `ListTierWatch` (`screenshots.md`) sees enough of them, `ModerationReports.listTier(notice)` tells the moderators' channel near the channel of the screenshot that tipped it, on the side, as `missingSpot` does: plain text, `listTierNote` (`src/render/moderation.ts`): "Screenshots show **Dynamax Sneasel** with 2 heads (2★) 3 times since yesterday, but the boss list has it at 1★. If the game has it at 2★, this would move it there: `/admin boss add pokemon:sneasel tier:Dynamax 2★ days:7`" (raids alike: "**Drapion** with 5 heads (Shadow T5) …", `tier:Shadow T5`: the tier by its choice's name, which Discord finds a choice by, review J4, in place of I6's choice value); the command a guide (review M3: the boss as `/admin boss` reads it, its catalogue key or `ids:<id>[:<form>]`), always `days:7` (I6); where an add in force puts the boss at the list's tier, "…has it at 1★, by override #4. If the game has it at 2★, that override may be wrong: `/admin boss remove override:4`" (M7). At most one note per server, family, boss, list's tier and heads' tier in `LIST_TIER_NOTE_DAYS` (7; review M4), kept in memory (`tiersNoted`; a note not sent or refused is forgotten); without the moderators' channel a log line (`screenshots disagree with the boss list`, the server and the channel). Member ids are only counted, never in the note or the log. Members' answers say nothing new.

## /delete

- `/delete` is for staff (`default_member_permissions` Manage Messages, `requireManagement`, help category `admin`): it asks in a dialog whether to archive the post or delete it (plan 8Q, `removalDialog`, titled `Close this raid` / train / meetup; `pclose:<party id>:close:~<token>`, or `pclose:<party id>:close:archive:~<token>` without the choice where the bot may not delete the post (the per-open token of `removalDialog`, as for a report's dialogs, left out by `removalOf`), the forum named as in a report's dialog; the command declares no `defer`), and its Submit (`closeControls`, acknowledged privately; a deletion the bot may no longer make is refused there with today's words; since plan 8M-8B its whole work after the dialog is `closePartyAs`, which staff's Delete… on the live map calls too, its outcome worded by `closeWords`, the dialog's words in the import-free `src/render/removal-words.ts`: `web.md`) ends the party as before, the post saying who closed it (`PartyService.end` with `removal`: `delete` deletes the post with everything in it in the place of its last showing and the archiving; a deletion Discord refuses archives it). The command has no throttle; its dialog's Submit has `CLOSE_THROTTLE` (twenty a minute) and takes no party of another server; the buttons of the question before plan 8Q (`pclose:<party id>:archive|delete`) get the router's stale answer. A deletion Discord refuses is said to the moderator as for a report; one the network failed is recorded as `delete` and said with `pendingDeletion` ("This raid is closed: its gym is free for a new report, and its post is archived for now: Discord did not answer …", a max battle's spot, a train's or meetup's "is closed, and its post is archived for now: …"; review r1 C1). `/help` tells a member who asks for `/delete` to use `/report` (`TOLD_INSTEAD`, without saying that the command exists).

## /audit: the staff audit log

- Anonymous means anonymous in the posts only (Matt, 2026-10-13; design approved 2026-11-07): staff can ask who reported or made a party and who changed what. `/audit [member]` (`src/commands/parties/audit.ts`) is for the same people as `/delete` (Manage Messages, `requireManagement`, help category `admin`, so hidden from members in `/help`; no throttle; `defer: 'ephemeral'`). In a post (a thread: `channelContext`), active or ended, it answers `postAuditMessage` (`src/render/audit.ts`): `### Log of this raid` (max battle, train, meetup by the kind of its newest creation or move; "post" without one), a line per entry, oldest first, `<t:…:t> **Ash** (<@id>) reported it (anonymous in the post): Level 5 raid (egg) at **The Stacks**, hatches …`, `… set the boss: Yveltal (T5) (was T5 egg)`, `… closed it (archived the post)`, `<t:…> The clock ended it.`, and staff's closing that names nobody (the upkeep's redone deletion) `**Pine** closed it: staff deleted its post`; the actor with `memberText` (staff read it privately), `**Pine**` for no actor; times with `clockOrDate`. How the party was made (its newest creation or move here) always stands first, outside the cap; then at most `AUDIT_LINES` (25) and as many as fit 3,500 characters, the newest; more: one line `-# …and N earlier changes` (the member's list says `more than N` where it read only the newest 26). None: `NOTHING_LOGGED`. With `member` (a user option): their newest 25 entries in this server, each `in <#post>`, under `### Changes by …` (`memberAuditMessage`). Outside a thread without `member`: `AUDIT_WHERE` ("Use `/audit` in a party's post, or give `member`.").
- What is recorded is decided by the pure `auditEntries(before, after, names, how)` (`src/domain/party/audit.ts`), one `AuditEntry` per fact: `created` (kind, tier words and boss or name, place, anonymous, hatch, start, end; a raid reported hatched without its boss says `hatched`: "(hatched, boss unknown)", not "(egg)") or `moved` (a raid moved here from another region: the old party's id, gym and channel), `boss` (`Yveltal (T5)`, `T5 egg`, `Machamp (Dynamax 2★)`, a train's boss by name or `none`), `moves`, `place` (a raid's gym in its region, a max battle's spot), `hatch`, `start`, `end`, `name`, `info` (without the text), `route` (one entry per change of a train's route, the stops `added` and `removed` by name, counted, so a reversal is no edit; worded "added 30 stops: …" within 300 characters) / `route-cleared`, `conductor`, `kind` (a train's, Manage…), `saved` (`/admin save-party`), `ended` (`clock`, `closed` with `by` staff or moderators and the `removal` they chose, `moved` with where to, `failed`). Not recorded: attendance, groups, meeting times, a train's movement, the post's own ids. A change of nothing it keeps writes nothing; names (gyms, Power Spots, bosses, moves, a region's channel) are those of the moment (`AuditNames`, `auditNamesOf`), kept as facts and worded when read (`auditEntryOf` reads them back through the schema, an unreadable row is left out with a warning).
- `PartyAudit` (`src/services/party-audit.ts`) listens to the store (`onChange` with its `ChangeNote`, `parties-and-posts.md`), so every way a party changes (commands, controls, the map, the clock, the feeds) is logged without code of its own; the actor is the cause's `by` (the interaction's name, else the mention), none for Pine; a meetup closed because its maker was blocked (`MeetupSafety`'s Block member and `closeIfBlocked`) names the moderator who blocked them (the block's `blockedBy`). It writes on the side, one write after the other (`flush()`, also at shutdown, three seconds); a failed write is the warning `could not write to the audit log` and never fails the change; a hard stop may lose what waits. At most `AUDIT_QUEUE_CAP` (1,000) writes wait: beyond that they are dropped, with one warning `the audit log drops entries` per burst (until the line drains); a waiting write keeps only what its rows need, never the parties. Each entry keeps the post the party had then; a party's first entries (written before its post exists) get it when it does (`attachPost`). `sweep()` deletes entries older than `AUDIT_KEEP_MS` (30 days) with the scheduler's hourly upkeep (`PartySchedulerOptions.audit`). A train's split logs its two parties apart.
- The table is `PartyAudit` (the migration `party_audit`; `PartyAuditRepository`, twin `MemoryPartyAudit`, both run `partyAuditCases`): `serverId` the snowflake (no foreign key), `partyId`, `postId`, `at`, `actorId`, `actorName`, `action`, `detail` (JSON). `ofPost(serverId, postId)` finds every party with an entry for that post, then follows `moved` entries back to the parties a raid moved from, and gives all their entries (the newest `AUDIT_POST_READ_LIMIT`, oldest first): the log of an ended party's post and of a moved raid's new post both work. `ctx.partyAudit` is the reader (`PartyAuditReader`).

## /places

- `/places [place]` (`src/commands/meetups/places.ts`): Manage Messages, `requireManagement`, answered privately.
