# Raid trains

The third kind of party: a route of gyms and Power Spots, a kind (raids, Dynamax or Gigantamax battles), a current stop, a conductor, modes at the end of the route (finish, loop, turn back), movement and its announcement, the dialogs Move… and Manage… of its post (plan 8X-G; the Manage train panel before), saved routes, copies in the channels of the regions a train stops in, and the commands' code paths the live map shares. Read before changing trains, routes or the dialogs (and `parties-and-posts.md` for what all parties share; `web.md`, "Trains on the map", for the map's side).

## The model

- A raid train is the third kind (`TrainParty`: `timed` like a meetup, and `train: { route, stop, conductorId, boss, finishedFrom, atEnd }`). `isTrain`, the guards `requireTrain` (with `commandTrain`, `trainOf`) and `requireRaidOrTrain` (what has a boss or a gym: `/boss`, `/where`); `bossOf(party)` is the boss of any party that has one. `route` is stops (`StopRef`, plan 8X-4: a gym by its id or a Power Spot by its Wayfarer id; below), none twice, at most `MAX_ROUTE_STOPS`; `stop === route.length` on a route that is not empty means finished; `party.gymId` is null (the gym of a train is `currentStop(party)`). It has groups like a raid and one start like a meetup; Done means leave, and whoever leaves as the conductor stops being it.
- The maker of a train is signed up as their auto-status says (None: not at all; Here counts as Coming), and with privacy Anonymous neither the post nor the new-train DM names them (`NewTrain.anonymous`, `autoStatus`). A maker who is not signed up may still arrange the route and the times as far as the train does not move (`mayArrange`, `party.ts`); moving it and being its conductor need a sign-up. Meetups know neither setting.
- A train remembers the stop it was at when a move finished its route (`train.finishedFrom`; state document version 6, version 5 read as null): Previous from the end leads back there and leaves Here as Here (`destinationOf`, `targetOf`), and to the last stop when nothing is remembered. `withTrain` in `train.ts` is the one place that keeps it (null unless the route is finished and still has that stop), so a route is changed only through the functions of `train.ts`, never by building `train.route` by hand: the state document refuses a train that breaks the rule. The panel's Previous button carries its target like Next and Skip.

## Changes and movement

- Everything that happens to a train is a pure function in `src/domain/party/train.ts` and goes through `ctx.trains` (`TrainService`: `addStop`, `removeStop`, `moveStop`, `clearRoute`, `move`, `manage` (Manage…'s one change of the kind, the route and the mode; since plan 8M-8C, review C5, the only way to reverse the route or set the kind or the mode: `reverse`, `setAtEnd` and `setKind` went with the map's panel, their last callers), `setConductor`, `clearConductor`, `setBoss`, `saveRoute`, `loadRoute`, `forgetRoute`, `savedRoutes`, `reaches`, `flush`; each change answers a `TrainChangeOutcome`). The current stop follows its stop through every route edit. An edit after which the train is somewhere else (`placeOf` differs: the current stop removed, a stop added to a finished route, a loaded route) is a movement: it needs the right to move (`mayNotMove`: the conductor or staff when there is a conductor, otherwise signed up; staff always), puts Here back to Coming and is announced. The first stop of an empty route is an ordinary edit. Finishing leaves Here as Here and changes no time.
- Who may edit is one rule each in `train.ts`, which the domain functions, the live map's view and its routes all ask: `mayNotArrange` (add, remove, move a stop: signed up, the maker, staff), `mayNotClear` (the conductor and staff; `/route clear` counts bot developers as staff), `mayNotLoad` (who may move the train, or the maker its first route), `mayNotMove`. An edit of the route may carry the route it was drawn from (`drawn`, the live map's route editor: `addStop`, `removeStop`, `moveStop`, `clearRoute`, `loadRoute` and `saveRoute` take it last): the service refuses it inside the store when the route is another by now (`routeDrawn`, reason `route-edited`); a command gives none. So may a change of the conductor carry whose the role was as the map drew its button (`seen`, `ConductorSeen`: `setConductor` and `clearConductor` take it last), refused inside the store when the role is somebody else's by now (`conductorDrawn`, reason `conductor-changed`).
- What the commands do beyond reading their options is one function each in `src/commands/trains/train-changes.ts` (`startTrainAs` for `/train`, `addStopAs` … `forgetRouteAs` for `/route`, `moveTrainAs` for the four moves, `setConductorAs`, `clearConductorAs`; `manageTrainAs` is Manage…'s Submit (plan 8M-8C: moved out of `move.ts`, so that the dialog and the map's form share it, with `manageRefusal`, `movedOnWords` and `splitRefusalFor`; the map's panel's `reverseRouteAs`, `setAtEndAs` and `setKindAs` are gone with that panel, and `TrainService.reverse`, `setAtEnd` and `setKind` with them)), answering a `TrainAnswer` in the command's words; the commands throw a refusal as a `UserError`, the live map answers it (plan 8M-5). Move… and Manage… answer in the commands' words too (plan 8X-G). `startTrainAs` makes the train with its start when one is given (the map's form: `TrainRequest.startTime`, `NewTimedParty.startTime`), so that its post and the new-train DM say it from the first; `notSignedUpWords(where)` is the clause `/train` and the map add for a maker not signed up. `setConductorAs` names the conductor it took over from by the caller's `ConductorNamer` (`DISCORD_CONDUCTOR`, `memberText`, on Discord; the map names nobody).
- A movement is announced in one message that pings the signed-up members who allow `mentions` and `mentionsTrainStops`, never the mover, gathered for five seconds and worded by the gym the train started from and where it is when the message goes ("back" only when every step was Previous; nothing when it is where it started or the route was emptied; "going round again, back to …" or "turning back at …, on to …" when the last step went round the end or turned back and the train is where it took it: `Movement` kinds `round` and `turn`, `PendingMove.lastFrom`). A gym or a Power Spot is a stop only when it lies in a region of the train's server (`reaches`, `stopRegionsInGuild`).
- What is said once a few seconds after the last change goes through `Gathered` (`src/services/gathered.ts`: `later(key, merge)`, `drop`, `flush`, with `fold(older, newer)` for an entry whose turn comes while another waits behind a slow send): the start of a timed party and a train's movement. The party services share `storedChange` (`src/services/party-change.ts`) for a change decided inside the store.
- What a train does at the last stop of its route is `train.atEnd` (`TRAIN_AT_END`: `finish`, `loop`, `turn-back`; `TrainAtEnd`, `isTrainAtEnd` in `party.ts`), set by `/train`'s option `at-end` (default `finish`), Manage…'s menu (plan 8X-G; Manage… opens only for who may move the train and staff, so `setAtEnd`'s `mayArrange` matters for a dialog opened earlier and for saved routes; `setAtEnd`: for whoever may arrange the route, `mayArrange`, and staff, since it never moves the train nor un-finishes a route; the same mode is the same object; the line names the doer through `doer`) and a loaded saved route. A route of fewer than two stops finishes in every mode (`endMode`, exported since plan 8M-11B for the live map's `MapTrain.atEnd`, by which the map draws the line's arrowheads and a loop's closing leg). `destinationOf(party, move)` is where a move leaves the train (`route`, `stop`, `turn`, `skipped`): Next at the last stop finishes, goes to stop 0 (`turn: 'round'`) or stores the route reversed and goes to its index 1 (`turn: 'back'`); Skip goes round a loop (`(stop + 2) mod n`, three stops or more) and at the last stop of a turn-back to index 2 of the reversed route (refused at its second to last stop); Previous at stop 0 of a loop goes to the last stop, of the others is refused. A move that goes round or turns back is a movement and never finishes the route, and neither does removing the current last stop of a loop or turn-back train with two stops or more left (`removeStop`: a loop goes to stop 0, a turn-back train has the rest reversed and stands at its index 0); Finish finishes in every mode. `nextStop` and `turnAhead` say what Next does from where the train is. `stopsAhead(length, stop, atEnd)` (plan 8M-15, pure, `atEnd` as `endMode` gives it; with `arrivalNumbers`, how many stops away each is, in `stops-ahead.ts`, a module of no import that the live map's page imports too, given on by `train.ts`) is the one list of what lies ahead: the indices of the route as stored now, in the order the train reaches them, until it has reached every stop once more (a train that finishes to its last stop; a loop round to the stop before the current one; a turn-back out to the last stop and back to the first, the current stop listed where it passes it again, since the turn stores the route reversed but the indices stay today's; nothing for a finished route or one of fewer than two stops). `routeAhead` takes its first stops from it, and the live map's `MapTrain.stopsAhead` is it (the numbers on its stops while it is selected and the Route list's "next" / "in N stops", `web.md`). `reverseRoute(party, member, staff, from?, first?)` (`cannotReverse`: a loop at any stop, a route that finishes at its first or last stop; a finished route of either mode only when finished from its last stop, which starts it again there, else `reverse-finished-elsewhere`; never a turn-back or a route of one stop) puts the same gyms in the other order with the train at the same gym: no movement, Here stays Here, the mode stays, said in a line without a ping ("…, and starts again at X" for a restart). Reverse leaves `placeOf` as it was (except the restart of a finished route, which moves it from `end` to the gym), so Manage… (on Discord and on the map) also carries `first`, the gym the route began with (`route-changed` otherwise). The post's line of what lies ahead says the mode on a route of three stops or fewer (`, loops` / `, turns back at the end`, on two stops or more) and marks a stop past the end `(round again)` / `(turning back)`; after a turn or a Reverse the stops are numbered in their stored order. The words of the modes are `src/render/train-words.ts`.

## The post, Move… and Manage…

## Kinds and Power Spots (plan 8X-4)

- **The kind** (design `2026-10-20-max-battles-design.md`, 6.1): `train.kind` (`TrainKind`, which is `BattleKind`: `raid`, `dynamax`, `gigantamax`; party state version 14, a train of an older document is `raid`), set by `/train kind:` (default raid; its hint `What the train is mainly for (its stops can be gyms or Power Spots)`) and Manage…'s menu "What the train is mainly for" (plan 8X-G; `TRAIN_KIND_QUESTION`; the choices `Raids`, `Dynamax Battles`, `Gigantamax Battles`, `trainKindChoice`, the same for `/train kind:`; `TrainService.manage`, `setTrainKind`: for who may move the train and staff; the same kind is no change; a line `<doer> made this a Gigantamax Train.`); the live map sets it through Manage…'s form (plan 8M-8C, `manageTrainAs`, `POST …/manage`) and makes a train with a kind (plan 8X-5, `web.md`). Its words are one set, `TRAIN_KIND_WORDS` (`Raid Train`, `Max Battle Train`, `Gigantamax Train`; kept in `src/domain/web/party-data.ts` since plan 8X-5, where the map's view and a page may take it, and given on by `render/train-words.ts`; `trainKindChoice` for the choices, which name what the train is mainly for): the post's heading and brief, `/help`; the new-train DM and the summary line name a max train's kind, a raid train's stays "train". It sets the lobby (`lobbySize`: a Gigantamax train `lobbies.gigantamax`, so Join fills its groups; a raid and a Dynamax train none; `splitLobby` only a raid train), at once when changed (groups already made stay), the forum tag (a Dynamax or Gigantamax train is tagged `Max battle` too) and `/counters` in its post (a Dynamax train the max counters at the boss's Dynamax tier where a max boss list names it, else `tier:` is asked; a Gigantamax train the Gigantamax tier; a raid train raid counters as before). `/boss` takes any Pokémon whatever the kind (`trainBoss`).
- **Planned ahead:** `/train start:` (a time as `/meet` reads it, `typedTimedTime` over `newPartyTimes('train', now)`, read by the guard first so that a refusal is not counted) makes the train with its start; its route may stay empty until the day (a train with no time still goes a day after it was made). Manage… opens on a train without a route too, with its kind alone (so a kind set wrongly at `/train` can be changed before the first stop); the post shows no Move… until the first stop.
- **Stops** (design 6.2; `src/domain/party/stops.ts`): `StopRef` is `{ kind: 'gym', id }` or `{ kind: 'powerspot', id }` (a Wayfarer id); `stopKey` names one as text (`'12'`, `'w<Wayfarer id>'`: no colon, not `p`), by which stops are the same (`sameStop`, `whereOf` for whether a change moved the train) and the map's `MapTrain.route` is made; `stopHandle` is what a control carries (a gym's id; the first 8 hex characters of an FNV-1a hash of a Wayfarer id, so the dialogs' custom ids and choices stay within 100 characters with the longest ids, tested), checked against the route when pressed (`stopsByHandle`: no match is `train-moved-on` for a place, two `route-changed`). Every function of `train.ts` works on stops. A Power Spot is shown as a gym stop (`src/render/train-stops.ts`: `PostStop`, `stopsFrom` over the gyms and the kept Wayspots, `stopLabel` `<title> (Power Spot)` in lists, the dialogs and the movement announcement, `stopLink`, `stopWhere`: Where with its address, Map and Wayfarer picture); one no longer kept as a Power Spot (gone, or an import made it a PokéStop or gym: a train's spot never becomes a stop of another kind) is `Unknown Power Spot`, refused when added (`spot-gone`) and taken off by `removeUnknownStops` as an unknown gym is (never while no Wayspot is kept, `WayspotProvider.size`). `/where` in a train at a spot names it with its Map; a gym target, a request and the moderators' place are of gyms (a spot is the moderators' place too).
- **`/route add stop:`** (renamed from `gym:`): its list is the `PlaceFinder`'s of kinds `gym` and `powerspot`, each choice by its kind and where it is (`placeLabel`), a Dynamax or Gigantamax train offered Power Spots first among places that fit as well, a raid train gyms (`mergePlaces`' `kindsFirst`; a better fit first whatever its kind); with nothing typed on an empty route the region's gyms as before, a max train's the region's Power Spots by name ahead of them (`regionSpotChoices`, shared with `/max spot:`). On a route with a stop the list puts nearby places first (Matt, 2026-10-27): the anchor is the last stop (`lastStopPoint`, through `PlaceFinder.chosen`; a stop gone is passed over for the one before it), so of places that fit alike the nearer comes first (after the kind of `kindsFirst`, as `mergePlaces` orders), and with nothing typed the list is the server's gyms and Power Spots nearest it (`nearestStops`, `PlaceFinder.nearest`), nearest first whatever the train's kind, without a radius, the route's stops left out, 25 at most, each with its distance. A value is `gym:<id>` or `powerspot:<Wayfarer id>` (an old `g:<id>` still read); typed text is a gym by `reportedGym`'s rule and a Power Spot by `clearOf`'s, and the train's own kind decides first (`typedStop`): its stop beyond doubt is taken, and its several (some fitting every word) are "Several places fit", unless the other kind's stop beyond doubt fits the typed text strictly better than that stop or the best of those several (`PlaceFit`, `TypedSide.several.best`; design 6.2: a better match first, whatever its kind; a gym beyond doubt fits at least as a word's fit; a tie is the train's kind's), so a raid train finds gyms as before unless a Power Spot fits strictly better, and a max train takes a gym's exact full name though several Power Spots contain its words (plan 8X-F, the staging's "Allegheny Station"); without one, or with a list of guesses only (no place of that kind of the server has every word as typed; for gyms, no gym of the server has every word as typed), the other kind's stop beyond doubt, else several or none. A raid train may add Power Spots. `/route remove stop:` keeps its stop number. `/route move`'s answer says where the stop stands now (`… is stop 2 of the route now, before **Y**.`, or `the last`) and, when the number of the stop the train is at changed, that the train stays (`The train is still at **Y**, now stop 3.`, or `at it` for the very stop moved); its `to` choices, `from` given, say where the stop would stand (`2. before Y`, `4. after Z`, `3. X (where it is)`; Matt, 2026-10-24: a member took the move for one of the train).
- **Saved routes** keep stops: `SavedRoute.stops`, the column `SavedRoutes.gyms` their keys separated by commas (`routeText`, `routeStops`, which also reads the legacy bot's gym ids; no migration). `removeGym` keeps a route's Power Spots and removes only a route left without a stop; `countWithGym` and `removeGym` narrow their `LIKE` by the parsed stops. `/route load` leaves out stops gone or of no region of the server.
- **Copies** in other regions go also where a Power Spot of the route lies (`stopRegionsInGuild` in `PartyMirrors.regionChannels`). The history records a spot's stop with its `wayspotId` (`history.md`). The map draws the line through the spots its data gives (`web.md`).

## Splitting a group off

- A group of a train can become a train of its own (plan 8T; spec `2026-10-17-train-split-design.md`; Matt, 2026-10-17: faster players go on at their own pace instead of per-group positions on one train). Who may is `mayNotSplit` in `train.ts`, beside `mayNotArrange`: never a train of one group (`one-group`), one without a post yet (`unposted`: the new train links to it), the new train of a split not settled yet (`split-pending`, "This train is still being split off another: try again in a moment.": from its post until the paired write its members are on the old train too, and a group split off it would outlive the split's take-back; `splitsPossible` and `splittableGroups` offer nothing of it), a group it does not have (`no-such-group`), an empty one (`empty-group`) or one that everybody on the train is in (`whole-train`, Matt, 2026-10-17: the old train would be left empty; the words name the group, `Everybody on this train is in group B (Fast): there is nothing to split it from.`, `splitRefusal`); staff and the conductor any other group; a member of the group (any status) their own (`not-in-group`, "Only a group's members, the conductor and the moderators can split a group off.") unless the conductor is in it (`conductor-in-group`). `splittableGroups` is what the dialog and the autocomplete offer; `splitsPossible` (two groups or more with somebody in them) whether the post shows the button.
- The pure parts (`train.ts`): `splitTrain(old, { id, by, anonymous, group, now })` is the new train: `<old name> (<label or letter>)` cut to `MAX_PARTY_NAME` (the group keeps at least half of the room), the same server and region, route, stop, `finishedFrom`, mode, boss, start and end (through `withTrain`), one group A with the group's label, the group's members as they are in A, made now by the presser (named unless their privacy is Anonymous; `makerName` is noted by the service), led by the old conductor who goes with the group, else the presser in it unless their privacy is Anonymous (as a `/train` made anonymously, it then has none until `/conductor set`; Matt, 2026-10-17), else nobody, with `train.splitFrom` (the old party and post, `pending: true`). `splitOffGroup(old, member, staff, group, link, drawn?, anonymous?)` is the old train without the group and its members: the other groups keep their letters, the newest left takes new sign-ups when the newest went, its conductor stays unless they went, `train.splitOff` gains `{ partyId, postId, group, label? }` newest first (at most `MAX_SPLITS_KEPT`, 10); it answers the members who moved (`moved`), the group, who leads the new train and the old train as it was then (`was`: its train part, start and end). `drawn` is the group's mark as the dialog showed it (`groupMark`, `<stamp>.<hash>`: when the group was opened, `groupStamp`, plan 8X-1B, then FNV-1a in base 36 of the label and the party id of the newest `splitOff`): refused `no-such-group` (worded as for a command) when the group went (split off, or pruned once it emptied) or its letter is a group's opened since, `group-changed` when the label changed or another group was split off since, so a letter given again (even with the same label) is never taken for the group that had it; a `splitOff` entry keeps the group's `opened`. `splitDrawn(train, group, mark)` is the split a stale mark was drawn for when that very group is the one split off last. `carriedSplit(target, start, moved, conductor, was?, now?)` brings the new train in line with the group as it was at the split, and with the old train's place and times then unless they were changed on the new train meanwhile (as for the conductor); the very same train when that changes nothing. `splitSettled(train)` clears `pending`. A group opened after a split takes a letter that no group has and no split named, the first after the last in use, else the first such; when every free letter was a split's, the first free one (`nextGroupId`, `attendance.ts`); since plan 8X-1B a group nobody is in goes too (`pruneGroups`), so a letter comes again, and the mark (by the group's stamp) keeps a stale dialog from taking the new group for the old.
- `PartyService.splitTrain` (`ctx.trains.split`, `TrainService.split`, which adds the lines) does it as `relocate` does a raid's move, one split of a train at a time: refusals known at once are answered before anything is made (`no-forum` also when the region's forum is not the old train's); the new train is stored, pending, and gets its post in the same forum (`createTracked`: link line, summary, region copies follow; no new-train DMs); then both trains change in one write (`PartyStore.updateBoth([old, new], …)`, one transaction: both or neither), which decides anew; a refusal there (the presser left, the label changed, a second split), an old train that ended, a new train that ended meanwhile (its post deleted: `no-post`), or a failed write takes the new train back (`takeBack`: its post and link line deleted, dropped from the history as never having been a party). In that write the old train loses the group (`splitOffGroup`, the presser noted first), and who is in the group at that moment goes: a Leave or a Join on the old post while the new post was made counts; the new train, as it is then, is brought in line (`carriedSplit`, then `splitSettled`): it drops who left the group, adds who joined it, keeps what members did on the new post meanwhile (who left the new train stays out), is put where the old train was at the split (a Next or a route edit meanwhile) and is settled. A press on the new post while that write waits for the old train's lock goes before it or after it, as the order of the two ids puts the locks (`updateBoth` takes them in that order); either way it counts. Nobody is in both trains or in neither once it settles. The old post is never deleted or replaced: it is re-rendered without the group and with its small print (its region copies too), and gets its line.
- No window between the writes: the old train's loss of the group and the new train's carry-over commit together or not at all, so nobody can end on both trains or on neither. What is left is the new post of a split whose write never committed: a stop of the process after the new post and before the write, or a failed write whose take-back could not end the new train. It stays pending (`train.splitFrom.pending`, in the state document) while the old train still has the group, and `settlePendingSplits` takes it back, each under the moves lock of its old train: once Discord is there after a start (`app.ts`: taking one back deletes a post), and with the clock's hourly upkeep those made a minute ago or more (`PartySchedulerOptions.splits`: the old train's moves lock keeps a split under way apart from the upkeep, which finds it settled once it has the lock; the minute's age only spares it waiting behind one), logging how many it settled and took back only when it did any; one it could not end stays pending for the next try and is not counted. A pending train whose old train is still active and lists it in `splitOff` cannot come of this code (only 8T's first build, which wrote the two trains one after the other, could leave one); it is settled as it is stored and logged as an error, since the group is on no other train. Only an active old train is seen (the store keeps no ended party): the pending train of an old train that ended is taken back, whatever the old one listed. A commit whose acknowledgement is lost (the connection drops after MySQL committed) is the same rare risk as for any `update`: the split's take-back then ends the new train although the old one lost the group, which leaves the group on neither train in the database (memory still has it on the old train) until the old train's next write stores it again, and a restart before that write makes the loss permanent (the restarted process reads the old train without the group); accepted as the same rare risk as any `update`.
- Then the old post says `<doer> split group B (Fast) off into a train of its own.` (a line, no ping) and the new post `<doer> split this train off with group B (Fast).`, pinging the members who moved and allow `mentions`, never the presser (`doer`: a maker who asked not to be named is not; `This train was split off with …` then).
- The post: last in the heading block, after the boss's facts (mockup 3), small print `Split off from <#post>` and per split `Group B (Fast) went its own way: <#post>`, newest first (channel mentions: Discord shows the post's current title, in copies too, after either train ended), cut through `fit.part` (labels shorter, then `…and N more`). The grey "Split…" (`party:<id>:split`, `splitGroupButton`, `moves: true` so region copies leave it out; "✂️ Split group…" before plan 8X-G) stands after Manage… while two groups or more have somebody in them (`splitsPossible`); the worst case (five groups, `MAX_ROUTE_STOPS`, the picture, every split line) is 32 components since plan 8X-G (Move… and Manage… for Manage train…; 31 since plan 8X-1B, each group one text; before, 44 with four groups folded to 40).
- The dialog (plan 8T-H; design `2026-10-18-train-split-dialog-design.md`, Matt, 2026-10-18: a reply under a busy post had to be scrolled to, and two presses made two): `splitDialog` (`src/render/train-split.ts`; handlers `openSplit`, `submitSplit` and `/group split` in `src/commands/trains/split.ts`), a Discord dialog titled `Split a group off the train` with Discord's own Submit and Cancel (Cancel says and splits nothing). ## Saved routes

- Saved routes are the legacy table `SavedRoutes` behind `SavedRouteRepository` (per member and region, at most `MAX_SAVED_ROUTES`, the same name without case replaces; `save` and `remove` lock the member's `User` row first). A saved route keeps the order of its stops and the train's mode (`SavedRoutes.atEnd`, migration 013, and its stops gyms and Power Spots since plan 8X-4 (above), `varchar(10)` defaulting to `finish`, which covers the legacy bot's inserts; a value this code does not know reads as `finish`; `SavedRoute.atEnd`; starting the bot migrates, so a dev database must hold 8W-1's 012 before this branch starts on it: Kysely refuses 012 after 013 has run, and a database with 012 refuses a branch without it ("previously executed migration ... is missing"); if 013 ran first, delete its row from `kysely_migration` and start again, 013 does nothing when the column exists): `/route save` writes the train's, `/route load` sets both (`replaceRoute(…, atEnd)`), `/route show` ends with `atEndSentence` on a route of two stops or more; the private answers of save and load always say the mode, the public "loaded" line in the post only when it is not `finish`. The `route` option's autocomplete value is `id:<n>` (`SAVED_ROUTE_CHOICE`); anything else typed is a name. `/boss` in a train goes through `trainBoss` (`raid-input.ts`): tier words are ignored, any Pokémon the catalogue names is accepted, `none` clears; `reportedBoss` is for raids only.

## Copies in other regions

- A train has a copy of its message in the text channel of every other region of its server that a gym of its route lies in (mirror kind `region`; `regionChannels` in `PartyMirrors`), only where the bot may post embeds (`PartyGateway.mayPost`, from the cache: without Embed Links Discord drops the embed instead of refusing). `PartyMirrors` keeps one newest copy per slot: the kind, and for `region` the kind in a channel. Such a copy is `renderMirror('region', …)`: a line at the top and the post with its controls but Move…, Manage… and Split… (the layout's `move: false`, `moves` buttons); it keeps "Route". A copy is sent again without its picture when Discord refuses that. A run of the shared queue that left something undone (a failed edit or send, a channel that refuses or is gone) is followed by one more for that party `RETRY_AFTER_MS` later, for a raid's copies too. On the fake gateway `noEmbeds` names the channels that lack the permission.
