# A server's settings

What the settings file held that is really a Discord server's lives in the database, one row per server, and its staff change it in `/admin settings` (plan 19A-1; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-multi-server-instance-design.md`, "Per-server settings"), its time zone among them (plan 19A-2, "Time zones"); and who changes what every server shares, and what a server's staff are told of its setup (plan 19B-1). Read before reading or adding a setting, reading a time in a zone, or changing the table, the lookup, the fill, the panel or its log.

## What is a server's, and what the instance's

- **A server's values** (`ServerValues`, `src/config/server-settings.ts`): the channels by their role (`bot-lab`, `mod-bot-lab`, `unown`, `elite-gym-raids`, `remote-raids`, `pvp`, `bot-updates`, `moderation`, and the events forum `events`), the PvP category (`categories.pvp`), the Unown role and the map's member role (`roles.unown`, `roles.mapMember`), `features` (`exGymChannel`, `remoteRaidChannel`, `notifications`, `roles`), `distanceUnits`, `eventsForum` (its three numbers), `flaggedWords` (meetup safety's), `blacklistWords` (the words the server's gym search ignores, plan 19B-2: its view of the gym directory reads them, `gyms-and-regions.md`), `regionForumSuffix`, `postNewGyms`, `regionMapLink`, `invite` (the map's, for a visitor not in the server), and whether the server uses passive screenshot reading and the auto-assigned role (`passiveReading`, `autoAssignRole`; on unless its staff turn them off). Channels, the category and roles are **ids** (snowflakes), never names.
- **What the file named and the fill did not find** (`unmatched`, review F1, F2): a moderators' channel or a map member role that the settings file names and the server lacks is kept as `channels.moderation` / `roles.mapMember` in `unmatched` (a list of its own, never a value in the place of an id), so that the upgrade opens nothing the name kept closed: meetup safety and reports stay on with the moderators unreachable (`moderatorsNamed`: approvals refused as `not-sent`, a report "could not be sent", never "no moderators' channel"), and the map counts administrators only (`mapMemberRole` gives `ADMINISTRATORS_ONLY`). A server without a readable row (none yet, its fill retrying or given up, or a row that cannot be read) has them too, wherever the file names the setting (`fileUnmatched`, fix round 4, I1): meetup safety stays on and the map closed until a row with ids exists. `parseServerValues` forgets a key there once its setting is chosen; `settingChanges` leaves the list out (the setting chosen is the change logged), but for a key staff leave unset (below), logged from `NOT_FOUND` (`"not found"`) to unset. The panel says above the select, whose default is only ever an id (`isSnowflake`: Discord refuses anything else, 50035), what stays closed and what leaving it unset would do (fix round 4, I2): "not found: meetups that need approval and reports are refused. Leaving it unset turns meetup safety off: meetups are no longer held for the moderators, blocks stop applying, and reports stay refused.", "not found: the map shows parties to administrators only. Leaving it unset opens the map: every member sees the map’s parties.", with **Leave unset** beside it (`server:<key>:unset`, fix round 3, H9). Its first press writes nothing: it redraws that line as a question ("Leave the moderators’ channel unset? Meetup safety goes off in this server: …") with two buttons, **Leave unset** (Danger, `server:<key>:unset:confirm`), which drops the marker, writes the change (logged, "left the map’s member role unset (it was not found)") and says what it did ("Left the moderators’ channel unset: meetup safety is off in this server; reports are refused.", "Left the map’s member role unset: every member sees the map’s parties."), and **Keep it closed** (Secondary, `server:<key>:keep`), which shows the panel as it was. A confirmation in place, not a dialog. Clearing the moderators’ channel's select asks nothing (a pick is deliberate), and its line says the same of it. There is no state "administrators only" of its own: staff who want the map closed pick a role only administrators have. Read these two settings through `moderatorsNamed` and `mapMemberRole`, never `channels.moderation !== undefined`: pure rules of the domain over the few values they read (`src/domain/servers/standing.ts`, fix round 3, H3, re-exported by `config/server-settings.ts`), so that `reportRefusal` (`src/domain/moderation/reports.ts`) imports only from `src/domain/`.
- **The time zone** (`timezone`, plan 19A-2): the server's, read through `ServerSettings.zoneOf` ("A server's zone", below). The file's `timezone` is the instance's default zone.
- **The instance's, in the file:** its `operators` and `servers` (plan 19B-1, "The operators", below), whether the instance allows passive reading and the auto-assigned role (`ocr.passive`, `autoAssignRole`: they decide the intents at login; `ctx.features` says what was granted), `helpGroups`, the emoji, the durations and lobby sizes, `rehearsal`, `wildSpawnExceptions`, `maxBattles`, `raidEvents`, `web.port|host|publicUrl|trustProxy|tiles`, `findRegion`, and everything about the reader, the feeds, the game data and Google.
- The schema (`serverValuesSchema`) checks a server's values as strictly as the file's (the same schemas: `eventsForumSchema`, `regionForumSuffixSchema`, `inviteSchema`, …), with a default for every key; the moderators' channel may be no other channel of the server but the moderators' bot lab.

## The table and the lookup

- **`ServerSettings`** (migration 052, `ServerSettingsRepository`; twin `MemoryServerSettings`, both run `serverSettingsCases`): `serverId` (the snowflake, primary key; no foreign key to `Guild`), `settings` (the values as one JSON document), `setUpAt` (when the server finished its setup; null for one that joined after the upgrade, which plan C reads), `updatedAt`, `updatedBy` (null for the fill).
- **A server's values are read through `ServerSettings.of(serverId)`** (`src/services/server-settings.ts`, `ctx.servers`), never from `config.settings`. Every row is read at start (`load`, before the login); `of` is synchronous, from memory. A server without a row has the file's defaults (`fileDefaults`: no channel, category or role, since the file names them and the values hold ids; the moderators' channel and the map's member role the file names kept in `unmatched`, above) until the fill gives it one, and so does a server whose row cannot be read. Where an interaction came from: `serverValuesOf(interaction, ctx)` (`src/discord/server-values.ts`; in a DM the file's defaults, `defaults()`). A reader with no server in hand (a scheduler over all servers) loops over the servers and asks each one's values. A channel of a role is reached by `serverChannel()` (`src/services/server-channels.ts`).
- **`set(serverId, change, by)`** changes a server's values, after the server's fill under way if one is (`filling`, which `ServerFill` tells it), and only where the server has its row: without one it refuses (`SETTINGS_LOADING`, a `UserError` that gives the use back; review F5), so that a change never writes the defaults without the names the fill finds. Then `change` gets them as they are (one change or fill of a server at a time, each after the one before; another server's never waits for it, fix round 4, I3: `inTurn` keeps a chain per server), the result is checked (`checkServerValues`; a `ServerValuesError`, with `problems` by key, for a value that is not valid: nothing written), then written with `by` (`{ id, name? }`, `actorOf(interaction)`) and the time, each setting it changed logged in the same transaction (below), then kept. **A change that changes nothing writes nothing**, neither the row nor the log. `/admin settings` and `/admin setup-forums`' new events forum are its callers.
- **No code outside `src/config/` reads a server key of the file** (`src/server-keys.test.ts`, as `src/text-distances.test.ts` refuses hand-worded distances): `settings.channels`, `config.settings.features`, `settings.web.invites`, `settings.timezone` (also `settings().timezone`, plan 19A-2) and the like are refused, in `src/` and in the tools under `scripts/`. The file's zone, the instance's default, is read only through `instanceZone(settings)` (`src/config/settings.ts`). What reads the file's server keys is `src/config/server-settings.ts`: `fileDefaults`, `fileInvite`, `fileChannelNames` (the fill's names and the name of a new events forum, `newEventsForumName`) and `fileCategoryAndRoleNames`; and `channelNamesOf` (`settings.ts`), the names `/admin cleanup-legacy` never lists beside the server's ids (review F6). The tools of the file itself are let through by name, each with its reason (review G6): `src/ops/convert-settings.ts` and `src/ops/cutover-check.ts`. The test also finds a key read as `settings['channels']`, destructured (`{ distanceUnits } = settings`, `{ web: { invites } } = settings`) and through a `web` destructured from the settings.

## The fill

- `ServerFill` (`src/services/server-fill.ts`): at ready, when a server becomes available and when the bot joins one, a server without a row gets one from the file's server keys (`filledValues`), its channel, category and role names resolved to ids in the client's cache as the readers before 2.1 found them (`serverNamesOf`, `src/discord/server-names.ts`); where the file names an events forum (none was drawn without, as `eventForumWanted` decided before 2.1; fix round 3, H7), it is the one recorded for the server (`EventForumRepository.forum`, `recordedForum`: the forum its posts are in), its name looked for only without a record (review F3). A name not found stays unset (the moderators' channel and the map's member role kept in `unmatched`, above); a name several channels or categories have (`ServerNames.howMany`) gives the one the readers took (H10). The server's moderators' bot lab is told once (`unmatchedWords`, plain text, every setting by the name the panel gives it, `CHANNEL_LABELS`: "the Unown channel #unown-sightings", "the events forum #events", never a key, H2): those left unset in one sentence, then each of the two kept closed on a line of its own, "… could not be found, so it stays closed until staff pick one in /admin settings: …" and what stays closed until then, then the names that matched several, "These names match more than one channel or category in this server, so I took one of each: <#…> as the bot lab #the-bot-lab, …"; or the log without a bot lab. The message goes out on the side (H4: never awaited; the fill is done at the write).
- A fill never rejects (H5): whatever fails in it, the names read from the cache as well as the write, is tried again a minute later (`FILL_RETRY_MS`), up to `FILL_TRIES` (30) times, a warning each time and an error when it gives up (review F5); meanwhile, and after it gives up, the server has the file's defaults (which keep closed what the file names, above), nothing waits for it, and a change of its values is refused. `stop()` at the shutdown, after which a fill under way that fails is not tried again (`stopped`, H12).
- **The upgrade:** when the table was empty at the start (`ServerSettings.upgrading`), every server present at the login is recorded set up (`setUpAt` now), and only those (fix round 3, H1): a server its shard's **first** READY named (`Events.Raw` before the shard's first ready, `ShardStart.inReady` of its first ready), those Discord said unavailable included, so that one down at the login is set up when it becomes available (`'present'`). Any other server without a row is `'joined'`, never set up: one that adds the bot (`GuildCreate`, or none between two shards' readies at the login), or one that added it while its shard was away (named by a later session's READY, then `GuildAvailable`).
- No interaction, scheduler or web route acts on a server before its row exists: the fill is begun in the client's own events, before discord.js hands on an interaction of that server; each interaction waits for its server's fill (`whenFilled`). On plan 19D's shards (`operations.md`, "Shards"), that wiring is `wireServerStart` (`src/discord/server-start.ts`, review F14, fix round 3 H1): `GuildAvailable` and `GuildCreate` begin a fill, and so does a shard's ready for each of its servers available then (one added between its READY and its ready has no event) and `ClientReady` for the cached servers no shard covered; a shard's start work (`shardStartWork`: meetup safety's warnings and the standing spawns at its first ready, which read the servers' values) waits for its servers' fills and their `Guild` rows (`syncGuilds`, review F4); `started` (the client marked connected, which the clock and the web wait for, the boss announcer, the application's emoji) once every shard's first ready did its work and the servers of `ClientReady` are filled and recorded; `InteractionCreate` routes after `whenFilled`. Tested over an `EventEmitter` with a real `ShardReadiness` and `shardStartWork`, the READY packet, `GuildAvailable`, `ShardReady` and `ClientReady` emitted in discord.js's order.
- The notifier finds a party's server by its `Guild` row (`serverOfRow`, from what the process has seen); a row it has not seen is asked of the database (`GuildRepository.snowflakeOf`) and the news told once it answers, one ask per row at a time on which every event of the row meanwhile waits, so that they are told in the order they came (`rowAsks`, fix round 3, H6: an egg's news before its boss's); a row the database does not know either tells nobody and is logged (review F4).

## Commands a server must set up

- A command whose work depends on a server's values says so with `CommandHandler.setUpIn` (the wants and gym targets by `features.notifications`, `/iam`, `/iamnot` and `/admin self-roles` by `features.roles`, `/maps` without the instance's web pages by `regionMapLink`): it is registered for everyone; in a server where it is not on the router answers "This is not set up in this server.", then to staff (`isBotStaff`, as `/help` counts them) what to set in `/admin settings`, to anybody else "The server’s staff have not turned this on." (a staff command is never named to members, review F11; so the bot-channel guard's "This server has no bot lab yet."), before its guards and uncounted, and its autocomplete offers nothing; `/help` leaves it out of that server's pages (`commandsIn`). A command that depends on the instance alone keeps `enabled(config)`.
- A report is never posted in the open: without the server's moderators' channel `/report`, Report to moderators and the map's Report… refuse (`reportRefusal`, `moderation.md`); where the file named one the server lacks they are taken and found unreachable (`moderatorsNamed`).

## The operators (plan 19B-1)

Spec "The rule" and "Operators": a server's staff change only their own server's data; what every server shares (the boss lists' overrides and their sync, the event window, the image cache) is changed only by the instance's operators.

- **The settings** (the instance's, never server keys: `server-keys.test.ts` leaves them alone): `operators`, Discord user ids (none unless set), and `servers`, a list of server ids or `"open"` (unset, or an empty list: no list). 19B-1 reads `servers` only for the tiers, through `isOpenInstance(settings)` and `listedServers(settings)` (`src/config/settings.ts`; undefined where it lists none); who may join by it is plan 19C's.
- **The two tiers**, one pure rule (`src/domain/operators/tiers.ts`): *instance operators* are `OWNER_ID` and `operators` (`isInstanceOperator`); *shared-data operators* are those, and on an instance that is not open a member with a bot-management role (admin, moderator, bot developer, as `isBotManagement`) in the server the command came from, where `servers` is unset or lists that server; on an open instance the instance operators alone; **in a DM the instance operators alone**, no member's roles being read across servers (`isSharedDataOperator`). `src/discord/guards.ts` asks it: `isSharedDataOperatorAt(interaction, ctx)`, `isInstanceOperatorOf(userId, ctx)`, `serverStaffShareData(serverId, ctx)` (whether a server's staff pass it there: the tier asked for a member of its staff), and the guards `requireSharedDataOperator` (refused in the words of where it would pass: in a server whose staff it passes, as `/admin` refuses, "Only admins, moderators and bot developers can do that."; in another server, `ONLY_OPERATORS`, "Only the operators of this Pine can do that.") and `requireInstanceOperator` (`ONLY_OPERATORS`; it took the place of `requireOwner`). Both refuse in a DM in one way, `ONLY_OPERATORS_IN_A_DM`, "In a direct message only the operators of this Pine can do that." The Wayfarer tool's owner checks stay the owner's (`script-access.ts`, `web/tool-routes.ts`).
- **`/operator`** (`src/commands/operator/`, a `commandGroup`): `boss` (list, add, hide, autoset, shiny, remove, sync), `event-window` (set, end), `clear-image-cache` and `forget` with its controls, moved from `/admin` with their behavior; `/admin` keeps what is a server's own, and its nicknames until plan 19B-4 makes them a server's: `nickname add` and `remove` are on the shared-data tier meanwhile (`requireSharedDataOperator` after `/admin`'s own guard; `list` stays staff's). Contexts `Guild` and `BotDM`, server installs; `default_member_permissions` ManageGuild, so that a server hides it from its members and the router runs its guard (the shared-data tier) for its autocomplete too. Discord applies no permissions in a DM: it lists `/operator` in every member's command picker there, so the guard (the command's, and `forget`'s instance tier) and `/help` are what keep it closed. `forget` adds the instance tier. In a DM a time typed or shown is in the instance's default zone (`zoneOf(interaction, ctx)`), and the owner's DM says "from a direct message" (`notifications-and-dms.md`).
- **`/help`** (`CommandModule.seenBy`, `SubcommandHandler.seenBy`, narrowed by `commandsSeenBy` of `src/discord/set-up.ts` as `commandsIn` narrows by a server's values): `/operator` is shown to whoever passes the shared-data tier where they ask (`isSharedDataOperatorAt`, staff or not: an operator in a DM sees the staff's groups for it), and its page names `forget` to the instance operators alone; to anybody else it is answered as a command that does not exist.

## Notices to a server's staff (plan 19B-1)

Spec "Alerts and logs": the owner's alerts are about the instance; what is wrong with one server's setup goes to that server's own staff.

- **`ServerNotices`** (`src/services/server-notices.ts`, made in `app.ts` with `noticeSender(partyGateway)`): `tell(serverId, key, text)` posts plain text into the server's moderators' bot lab (`channels['mod-bot-lab']`), at most once per server and key while the bot runs (a `BoundedMap`; a restart says it again, as the logs do), returns at once and never throws. A notice counts as said only once it was sent: without a lab, or where the lab refuses, it logs at warn with `server`, `notice` (the key) and `text` (once per server, key and reason) and is told at the next occasion, so a lab set or fixed later hears of it; one on its way is not asked again. A send that sent nothing is a refusal (fix round 2, G1): the party gateway's `sendMessage` answers a channel that is gone, or one that takes no messages, with no id rather than an error, as rehearsal mode's does outside the channels it may post in, and `noticeSender` rejects then ("nothing was sent (the lab gone, takes no messages, or rehearsal mode)", fix round 3, J4). A lab the bot may not send to is not asked (fix round 3, J3): `noticeSender` asks `mayShare` first (View Channel and Send Messages; plain text needs no Embed Links) and rejects without a send. `flush()` runs at shutdown inside the step "direct messages", beside the DMs' drain and within its twelve seconds, before the client goes (`directMessagesStep`, `src/app-wiring.ts`; fix round 2, G3). A notice names no member and pings nobody; its words are in `src/render/server-notices.ts`. Tests give a service `RecordingNotices` (`test/support/server-notices.ts`), or `labNotices` and `setLab` where a caller's test needs the real one.
- **Its callers and keys:** the fill's names it could not find (`unmatched-settings`); meetup safety's moderators' channel gone or not postable at the start (`meetup-safety:gone`, `:cannot-post`; no channel chosen is the staff's choice and a channel of the file not found was told by the fill (the row's marker), both only logged, as a lookup that failed); a region's forum the bot may not make posts in (`forum-posts:<forum>`, `PartyService.postsRefusedIn`); the boss announcer's bot updates channel set and gone or not postable (`bot-updates:gone`, `bot-updates:cannot-post`; one left unset says nothing); the events forum gone (`events-forum:gone`), a post or its listing refused for a permission (`events-forum:cannot-post`; any other refusal, AutoMod or an outage, only logged), Manage Posts refused for a lock, an archive or a rename (`events-forum:manage-posts`), and its listing not pinned (`events-forum:pin`).
- **The callers do not stop the retry** (fix round 2, G2): each asks `tell` at every occasion (each refusal of a region's forum, each draw of the events forum while it is gone, each refused pin or Manage Posts), and `ServerNotices` decides what is said; their own once per forum or server (`refusedForums`, `forumGoneTold`, `refusedTold`) gates only their lines of the log. A notice not said for want of a lab is so said at the next occasion once the staff set one.
- **A server Discord says is unavailable** (an outage; `PartyGateway.serverAvailable`, false too for a server the bot is not in; fix round 2, G6): its channels may be out of date, so none is said to be gone. The announcer sends it nothing and tells nothing, the events forum draws nothing of it and says nothing gone (`drawServer`, G4), and meetup safety's check at the start passes it over and names it in a line of its own, `could not check the moderators' channel of these servers: Discord says they are unavailable` (G5; `shardStartWork` hands it every server of the shard's READY, those Discord said unavailable among them). Telling those servers' staff once they are available (`GuildAvailable`) is a follow-up. `serverChannels` is the cache's whatever the server's state, as before: the web map, the region editor, a region's removal and the orphaned forums read it during an outage too.

## The panel: `/admin settings`

- `/admin settings` (`src/commands/admin/server-settings.ts`, its drawing `server-settings-panel.ts`): for the people `/admin` is for (`requireBotManagement`), a private components-v2 panel (`LIST_COLOUR`) that changes in place, in the house style of `/settings` (`members-and-roles.md`): set-state buttons (Turn on / Turn off: what the press sets, never a toggle), a dialog for text, the title's second line saying what the last press changed (`changesSaid`, one sentence a setting; "That was so already." where nothing did) or why not (`refusalWords` of a `ServerValuesError`: "Not changed: the moderators’ channel must be a channel of its own, not the same as the PvP channel." (the setting named by its label, review H2: the schema's own message, which names the key, is for the log), the value unchanged), always in the panel's own words, never the checker's English nor a key (review G4: the forum's numbers by their bounds, "must be 1 to 365", "must be at most the days listed"; "must be an https:// address"; "a flagged word is longer than 100 characters", "a word gym search ignores is longer than 100 characters" (the two lists have the same limits, at most 500 words of at most 100 characters, in the file too; a blank word gym search ignores is none, trimmed away before the limits, so that a file or a row holding one is read, `ignoredWordsSchema`, where a blank flagged word is refused); one Pine has no words for, "that is not a valid value"). That line fits 500 characters by whole parts (`changesSaid`, fix round 3, H11): a sentence a setting, its lists of words in a smaller room, a sentence too long said without the value it was, else without its values ("Changed the region map link."), those that do not fit dropped whole and counted ("And 2 more changes: see Recent changes."), never cut inside a word, a mention or a code span.
- **Sections**, chosen in a menu under the title (`server:panel:show`), since Discord holds 40 components in one message: **Channels** (opens here; a channel select per role, text channels, the events forum a forum; min 0, so emptying it clears the channel; the fullest section, within the 40 also while it asks whether to leave the moderators' channel unset), **Category and roles** (the PvP category, a category select; the Unown role and the map's member role, role selects), **Features** (a section with its button per feature; passive reading and the auto-assigned role only where the instance allows them and was granted the intent), **Text and numbers** (the time zone with the time there now and its Change…, below, the units menu, and Change… for the events forum's numbers, the flagged words, the words gym search ignores, the forums' ending, the region map link and the invite, each a dialog with the value in it to start with and a per-open token), **Recent changes** (the newest `RECENT_CHANGES`, 15, of the log, newest first: `<t:…> **Name** set the moderators’ channel to <#id> (was <#id>).`; channels and roles as mentions, which ping nobody in a private answer; as many as fit the panel's 3,500 characters with the title, as `/audit` fits its lines, `fitted`, and `-# …and N earlier changes` for the rest, review G1; read one more than it shows, as `/audit` reads, so that `-# …and earlier changes` says there are older ones than were read, H8). A press shows the section of what it changed again.
- **Its selects' defaults** are only ids the server still has (`isSnowflake`, and in the client's cache of the server's channels and roles, `ServerPanelState.known`, review G2): a channel or role deleted since, or one the file named and the fill did not find (`unmatched`), is offered as no default and said under the setting's name, "not found: pick a channel" (or role). **A pick is checked** before it is set (review G3): a channel select's channel of a kind its setting takes (`channelTypes`; the PvP category a category) and of this server, a role select's role other than @everyone (the server's own id); a string pick of such a key, or any other, is stale (`STALE_COMPONENT`).
- **Its controls** are `serverSettingsControls` (kind `server`; custom ids `server:<key>:<action>[:<arg>]`, as `custom-id.ts` lists them: `server:channels.moderation:set`, `server:features.roles:set:on`, `server:flaggedWords:ask`, whose dialog is `server:flaggedWords:set:~<token>`, `server:roles.mapMember:unset` (Leave unset, which asks), `server:roles.mapMember:unset:confirm` (Leave unset, which writes) and `server:roles.mapMember:keep` (Keep it closed); the server is the interaction's). A command's guards do not cover its controls: the handler has `requireBotManagement` itself, asked again at every press, and counts every press in one window (`MEMBER_WRITE_THROTTLE`). Each change goes through `ServerSettings.set` with the presser as `by`.
- **The dialogs' words:** a list of words (`WORD_LISTS`: the flagged words, the words gym search ignores) one a line (never split at a comma, which a phrase may have), each once whatever its letter case; prefilled, a Submit replaces the list; too many to show in a dialog (`wordsTooMany`, over 4,000 characters), Change… becomes Add… ("Words to add") and a Remove… ("Words to remove", whatever their letter case) is beside it, neither replacing the list (review G5); an empty ending sets `-board` back; an empty link or invite clears it; the events forum's three numbers must be whole numbers and are checked together (`postDaysAhead` at most `horizonDays`); the time zone is one line, "Time zone", prefilled with the server's zone (`server:timezone:ask`, below).

## A server's zone (plan 19A-2)

Spec "Time zones": every time a member types, every clock of a screenshot, every date or time in a post, a DM, a dialog or the web, every "today" and every local midnight is in the zone of the server it belongs to. An installation whose servers share one zone behaves as before.

- **The lookup** (`ServerSettings`, and `TestServers` in tests): `zoneOf(serverId)` is the row's `timezone`, else `defaultZone()`, the instance's default (`instanceZone(settings)`, the file's `timezone`, the one read of it outside `src/config/`). A new row gets the file's zone at the fill (`fileDefaults`), so a later change of the file's `timezone` changes only the servers without a row or without a zone, and what has no server. `zonesInUse()`: the default and the zone of every server the bot is in, each once (fix round 1, K5: the option `present`, the client's servers in `app.ts`; until the client is ready, and in a test without it, the zone of every row kept); each name in use counts by its exact string (fix round 2, P1: a `Set` of the names, no zone worked out), so two spellings of one zone (`America/Chicago` and `US/Central`) are two zones in use, each with its own timers, reads and announcements, and each serves the servers `zoneOf` gives that very name. What runs per zone (the boss lists, the events' lists, the midnight timers) runs for these. `onZoneChange(listener)`: after the write of `set`, only when the server's zone in effect changed (a change from unset to the default's own zone is none, and so is another name of the same zone, `US/Eastern` for `America/New_York`), the listener gets the server, the new zone and the old; one that throws is logged (`a listener of a change of time zone failed`) and the others are still told. `onZonesInUseChange(listener)` hears a change of the zones in use that no change of a zone made (fix round 1, N5): `presenceChanged()`, which `app.ts` calls when the client is ready and when the bot joins or leaves a server, reads them again and tells the listeners where they differ from those last told (by exact string too: one spelling taking another's place is a change of the zones in use). So does `set` when a server's zone changed its name but not its zone (fix round 3, Q4: a server without a zone given `US/Eastern` where the default is `America/New_York`, or the other way): no change of zone, but maybe a name new in use, or one no longer in use. `followZones(servers, { server, zones }, log)` (`src/app-wiring.ts`) is how `app.ts` listens: a change of a server's zone reaches its `server` hooks and then its `zones` hooks, a change of the zones in use its `zones` hooks alone.
- **A party's server** is its `Guild` row's (`PartyService.zoneOf(party)`, `zoneOfRow(row)`), named by `RowIds.serverOfRow` (fix round 1, K1): every row read at the start (`GuildRepository.loadRows`, before the parties are loaded) and every row made or read since, never bounded or forgotten, so a party never has the default zone because a memory of rows dropped its server. A row still unknown (none can be, after the start) has the default, warned of once per row (`a party’s server is not known`).
- **Which zone a reader takes:** where an interaction came from, `zoneOf(interaction, ctx)` (`src/discord/server-values.ts`; the instance's default in a DM); of a party or a region, `PartyService.zoneOf(party)` / `zoneOfRow(row)` (its `Guild` row's server, `serverOfRow`, then the snowflakes the summaries learned, else the default); in a web route under `/s/<guild>/`, `requestZone(request)` (`src/web/request-zone.ts`: the guild scope's first hook gives each request its server's zone once, `takeZone`, so the whole answer is in one zone even if staff change it meanwhile); a DM about a party, a train or a report, its server's. Only what has no server takes the default: a DM with none, `/help` there (`HelpFacts.zone`), the `/userscript` page.
- **A name typed** is read by `zoneNamed(text)` (`src/domain/time/zoned.ts`): a place's name as IANA writes it, case set right by Temporal (`america/new_york` is `America/New_York`; `Asia/Kolkata` stays `Asia/Kolkata`, never ICU's older `Asia/Calcutta`, and a link such as `US/Eastern` is kept as typed: fix round 1, K3), or `UTC`; nothing for a name it does not know, an offset such as `+05:30`, an abbreviation (`EST`, `CST`, `GMT`), a POSIX rule (`EST5EDT`) or an `Etc/GMT±N` name, none of which follows a place's daylight saving time (fix round 1, K2; `zoneAbbreviation` tells an abbreviation). Two names of one zone are the same zone for a change (`sameZone`, used only to decide whether a change is one: `settingChanges` and `onZoneChange`): writing one over the other changes nothing, logs nothing and tells no listener.
- **On the panel** (Text and numbers): "**Time zone** · `America/New_York`: it is 2:30 PM, Thursday, November 13 there now · every time members type and Pine shows here" (`zoneLine`), so that staff can see it is right, and **Change…** (`server:timezone:ask`), a dialog "The time zone" with one line (at most 64 characters, `America/New_York` as the placeholder) and a sentence of what the zone is for. A name `zoneNamed` does not know is refused in the panel's words, the value unchanged: "Not changed: “Mars/Olympus” is not a time zone I know; use a name such as America/Chicago.", an abbreviation (capitals only, `zoneAbbreviation`: fix round 2, P3; `Paris` or `est` is a name it does not know) in words of its own: "Not changed: “EST” is an abbreviation; use a name such as America/New_York." A change is stored as `zoneNamed` gives it and logged as any other (`timezone`, "the time zone", worded as code: "Set the time zone to `Europe/Berlin` (was `America/New_York`).", on the panel, in Recent changes and in `/audit member:`).
- **What a change does** (the listeners `app.ts` gives `onZoneChange`): moments already stored (parties' times, reports, windows) stay the same moments, and what is shown follows the new zone. The server's region summaries are drawn again and its active parties' posts queued for a redraw on the paced queue of the posts' emoji (`PartyService.zoneChanged`); the events forums drawn again (`EventForum.zonesChanged`); the posts of the stops drawn again on their server's day and period, a post of the day before on the new clock deleted as at midnight (`StopPosts.zonesChanged`), and the region channels' lines of a day that ended on the new clock deleted (`StopLines.zonesChanged`); every timer of local midnights follows the zones in use (`everyLocalMidnightIn`'s `refresh`: the summaries', the events forum's, the want counts', the lines'); the raid bosses read again, so that a new zone has its own list and the soonest rotation change among the zones in use is known (`raidBosses.zonesChanged`; a change asked during a read makes one more after it, fix round 1, L3); and the server's activity rebuilt in the background (`ActivityZoneRebuild`, `history.md`; stopped at shutdown before its next batch, fix round 1, N2). A server joining or leaving with a zone no other server has changes the zones in use alone (`onZonesInUseChange`): the midnight timers and the raid bosses follow it, nothing is redrawn.
- **One zone per web answer** (fix round 1, N6): a route under `/s/<guild>/` takes its server's zone once, as the request comes (`requestZone`, `web.md`), and says and reads every time of its answer in it. The services an answer reads that look the zone up themselves (the stops' periods, the boards, the tallies, the history's days) may differ from it only during the milliseconds of a change of the server's zone; accepted, no zone is passed to them.
- **One timer per zone in use** (`everyLocalMidnightIn`, `src/services/local-midnight.ts`): a second after each local midnight of each zone, with the zone and the midnight it was set for, serving the servers of that zone; the zones are read again at `refresh` and after each midnight, so a zone that came or went with a server is followed by the next midnight at the latest. Day keys (`StopPost.day`, `StopLine.day`, `WantCountDay.day`, a research period, the activity's buckets and days) are the server's local day. The scheduler's tick gives each party the rules of its server's zone (`PartyService.rulesIn(zone)`: one per zone per tick, made when the first party of that zone asks).
- **Feeds' times** (`FeedTime`, `src/domain/time/zoned.ts`; `src/domain/time/feed-times.ts`): a feed's time with an offset or `Z` is a moment (`{ at }`), one without is wall-clock time (`{ local }`), a moment only in a zone (`momentIn`: the earlier of the fall overlap's two, a time in the spring gap moved forward). Events keep the moments of the instance's default zone and their wall-clock times (`startLocal`, `endLocal`), and `timedIn(event, zone)` / `allIn(list, zone)` place them in a server's (`utilities.md`, `stop-reports.md`). Stored in the nullable `startsLocal` / `endsLocal` columns of migration 054 (`ResearchEvent`, `MaxEvent`, `ListedEvent`, `EventWindow`; `ResearchTask.endsLocal`): where one is set it is the truth and the moment beside it its moment in the instance's default zone; rows written before hold moments only.

## The log of settings changes

- **`ServerSettingsAudit`** (migration 053, `ServerSettingsAuditRepository`; twin `MemoryServerSettingsAudit`, both run `serverSettingsAuditCases`): a table of its own (`PartyAudit`'s entries belong to a party). `id`, `serverId`, `at`, `actorId` (never null: every change has a staff member), `actorName`, `key` (`SettingChange.key`: `channels.moderation`, `features.roles`, `eventsForum.onThisDay`, `flaggedWords`; `settingChanges(before, after)` names them, a channel, a feature or a number by itself, a list or a text as one), `before` and `after` (the values as stored, ids not names; null for unset); keys `(serverId, at)` (the panel's), `(serverId, actorId, at)` (`/audit member:`'s) and `(at)` (the sweep's), as migration 045 keys `PartyAudit` (review G7), and `serversettingsaudit_actorid_index (actorId)` of migration 057 for the look of `/operator forget`, which counts a member's entries of every server (`members-and-roles.md`, "What the look counts"). `ServerSettings.updatedBy` has `serversettings_updatedby_index` of the same migration, for the same look.
- Written by `ServerSettingsRepository.write` in the transaction that writes the values (`inTransaction`), one row per setting changed: neither is written without the other. The driver gives a JSON column parsed: a value that is a text (an id) is read as it comes, never parsed again (it would make a snowflake a number).
- Read by the panel's Recent changes (`ofServer`, along `serversettingsaudit_server_at_index (serverId, at)`) and by `/audit member:` (`ofMember`, along `serversettingsaudit_actor_index (serverId, actorId, at)`; `moderation.md`), each newest first by reading its index backward, without a sort (InnoDB ends each entry with `id`, the order's second column; `server-settings-audit-repository.db.test.ts` checks the plans: the index audit of 2026-11-13, X13), whose lines it joins by time: `<t:…> in the server’s settings: set the moderators’ channel to <#id>.` (`changeWords`, `src/render/server-settings.ts`, the one wording of a change).
- **Kept 30 days** (`AUDIT_KEEP_MS`, as `PartyAudit`'s): `ServerSettings.sweepLog()` with the scheduler's hourly upkeep, right after the parties' log (`PartySchedulerOptions.settingsAudit`).
- An operator's `/operator forget` counts a member's rows with the staff's log (`audit` of `MemberHoldings`) and leaves them to age out, as `PartyAudit`'s (`members-and-roles.md`); a member's last change of a row (`ServerSettings.updatedBy`) counts among their staff acts.

## Going back to 2.0

2.0 reads the file and ignores both tables, so going back needs only the migrator's step every going back needs (the rows of migrations 052 and 053 removed from `kysely_migration`, `operations.md`, "Going back a version"); but what staff changed in `/admin settings` is not in the file, and 2.0 runs with the file's values. The rows stay, and the update made again goes on from them, which says two things more (review G9):

- What was changed in the settings file while 2.0 ran does not come back: a server's row wins over the file, which only gives a new row its first values. Redo those changes in `/admin settings`.
- A server the bot joined while on 2.0 gets its row at the next start of 2.1, from the file's names as the fill finds them, but not recorded set up (`setUpAt` null): the table was not empty, so that start is no upgrade.

2.0 knows neither `operators` nor `servers` (plan 19B-1), and its settings refuse a key they do not know: remove both from the file before it starts. It has no `/operator`: the boss list, the event window and the image cache are `/admin`'s again, for every server's staff, and removing a member's data is the owner's `/admin forget`.

2.0 also has one zone, the file's `timezone`, for every server (plan 19A-2): a server whose staff set another zone runs on the file's until the update is made again, and 2.0 reads the moment columns of the feeds' times and the event window, never the wall-clock ones of migration 054 nor `RaidBoss.startsFrom` of 055 (`operations.md`, "Going back a version").
