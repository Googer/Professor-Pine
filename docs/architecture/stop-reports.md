What members report at PokéStops (plan 8R-1; design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-stop-reports-design.md`): stops of Pine's own, the field research task list, research periods, the stop reports and `/research`, the post "Today’s Stops" (in the region's board since plan 8R-9) and the lines of the region channels, and the research wants and their DMs; Team GO Rocket (plan 8R-2): the lineups, `/rocket`, grunts' messages, the post's hideouts, Rocket wants and DMs. The map's server side (plan 8R-3) is in `web.md`, "Reports at stops on the map": it shares the code paths below. Their pictures (plan 8R-6) are under "Pictures at the stops" below. Read before changing anything under `src/domain/stops/`, `src/domain/research/`, `src/domain/rocket/`, `src/commands/research/`, `src/commands/rocket/`, `src/commands/stops/`, or `src/services/` `stop-reports.ts`, `stop-posts.ts`, `research-task-service.ts`, `rocket-lineup-service.ts`, `rocket-reports.ts`, `grunt-messages.ts`, `pine-stops.ts`.

## Stops of Pine's own

- A stop Wayfarer does not list (a sponsored one) is added by staff with `/pokestop add name: at: [address:]` (`src/commands/stops/pokestop.ts`, Manage Server and `requireBotManagement`, shaped like `/gym add`): `at` is a maps link, the game's share link or coordinates (`readLocation`), and must lie in a region of the server (`STOP_NOT_HERE`). Within `PINE_STOP_NEAR_METRES` (30) of a known stop (imported or Pine's own) it asks "Add anyway?" with the stops named (`pokestop:<token>:add|cancel`, `PendingRequests`, an hour); `/pokestop edit stop: [name:] [at:] [address:]` (`-` takes the address away) and `/pokestop remove stop:` (no dialog: a report keeps its snapshot) name a stop by the autocomplete of the server's own stops (`NO_SUCH_STOP` else).
- `PineStopService` (`src/services/pine-stops.ts`, `ctx.pineStops`) keeps them in memory and hands them to `WayspotDirectory.setPineStops`: a Pine stop is a `Wayspot` of kind `stop` with `pineServer` set, keyed `pine-<id>` (`pineStopKey`, `pineStopIdOf`), placed only in the regions of its own server, so `PlaceFinder` (`/wild near:`, `/meetup` with meetup safety's checks, pin names, `/research`) finds it as any stop, and every change bumps the stop index's and the map's generation. `readPlaceValue` takes `stop:pine-<id>`; the region editor's cache leaves out other servers' Pine stops. A stop of Pine's own also waits in the line for Google as a PokéStop, by the same rules (plan 8W-6, `gyms-and-regions.md`): its places, keyed `pine-<id>`, make it findable by the businesses around it.
- The Wayfarer import's preview names the Pine stops within 30 m of an imported stop (`pineStopsNear`), for staff to remove what Wayfarer lists now.
- A file of sponsored stops seeds them at once (`npm run stops:seed -- <file> --server <id> [--apply]`, `./pine seed-stops`; `src/ops/stop-seed.ts`, the pure part `src/domain/stops/seed.ts`): a CSV of `id,name,lat,lon,region,basis` (`SEED_COLUMNS`; quoted fields, a BOM and Windows line ends read; only `name`, `lat` and `lon` make the stop, the rest are notes; an unreadable row is said and passed over). `planSeed` skips a row outside every outlined region of the server and one within `PINE_STOP_NEAR_METRES` of a known stop (`PineStopService.nearStops`: a kept Wayspot of kind stop or a Pine stop of that server) or of a row added before it; the rest are added through `PineStopService.add`, address none, `createdBy` `SEED_MAKER` (`stops-seed`, no member). A dry run without `--apply`; each row's fate printed; a second run adds nothing. The database only: a running bot reads the stops at its next start. Pittsburgh's file (beside its Wayfarer export) is kept out of the repository; the tests use `test/fixtures/sponsored-stops.csv`, made up.

## The task list

- `settings.research` has a default for every key (always on, no switch): `url` (Leek Duck's `https://leekduck.com/research/`), `refreshHours` 1, `staleAfterHours` 6 (refused below twice `refreshHours`), `throttle` `{ uses: 10, minutes: 30 }`.
- `ResearchTaskService` (`ctx.researchTasks`: `tasks()`, `byKey`, `readAt`, `stale(now)`: none read, or not anew for `staleAfterHours`, the one rule of "out of date" for its log, its shrink guard and the map's pick list, plan 8R-3) reads the page every `refreshHours` with Pine's User-Agent (`PINE_USER_AGENT`) and a byte cap (`readLimitedText`, `MAX_RESEARCH_PAGE_BYTES`), parses it (`parseResearchPage`, `src/domain/research/leekduck.ts`: tasks by category, rewards of `data-reward-type` `encounter` (a catalogue entry, shiny or not; a plain and a costumed one of a task listed twice stay two, plan 8R-4, `rewardKey` with `costumedByName`), `item` (key and quantity) and `resource` (Mega Energy of a species); the breakthrough is skipped), and keeps the last good list in `ResearchTask` so a start without the network still offers it. A read with fewer than half the tasks of the list in use is a warning and the list stays, unless the read before it listed the very same tasks or the list in use is older than `staleAfterHours` (then the page did shrink, and the smaller list is taken: a real shrink is never refused for good); a page of no task at all is a failed read (`parseResearchPage` throws), so it never empties the list, however often it comes; a list not read for `staleAfterHours` is said once in the log. The kept list keeps the page's order. **An event's tasks first** (plan 8R-8): Leek Duck's page puts an event's tasks first, under the event's own heading or headings, in a section whose class has a second word and whose `<div>` has attributes of its own (`<div class="task-category event-field-research" style="…" data-event-end="…">`, the page of 2026-10-30; the parser takes every `<div>` with the word `task-category` in its class, no other, the class as the tag's first attribute in double quotes; a section of another shape is counted, `unreadSections`, and warned of in the log), above its standing categories (Wayback: 2026-06-10 "Candela's Quest for Victory" and "Spark's Caretaking Quest", one event under two headings; 2026-08-20 "Ultra Unlock: Water Festival"; 2026-09-25 "Choose Your Path: Twilight Trails"; then Catching, Throwing, Battling, Exploring, Training, Buddy & Friendship, Team GO Rocket, Sponsored, AR Scanning). The headings do not match the events feed's names, so an event's task is told by its category alone: `isEventCategory` (`src/domain/research/task.ts`), any category but those standing ones and `Other` (the parser's name for a list without a heading), in any case. `ResearchTaskService` sets the list in use through `eventTasksFirst` (a stable partition: an event's tasks first, then the rest, each in the page's order), for a read and for the list of the last run alike, so `tasks()`, `/research`'s pick list and search (`taskChoices`: within its two groups, by text and by reward), the map's pick list and `/want`'s boxes follow it whatever order the page gives. **An event's end** (Matt, 2026-10-31; migration 034, `ResearchTask.endsAt`): the page may list an event's tasks past its end, so a section's `data-event-end` is kept with its tasks (`sectionEnd`: the wall clock read in `settings.timezone`, the offset dropped, since the page writes the event's local time with Leek Duck's own offset: `2026-11-01T10:00:00-0800` where the events feed has `2026-11-01T10:00:00.000`; one that cannot be read is no end, counted as `unreadEnds` and warned of). `Z` is that moment. Limits: the local reading rests on one sample, and an event that ends at one moment everywhere, written with an offset, would leave up to some hours off (the ends read are logged with `research tasks of an event read`); a task a standing section lists too has no end, and keeps the event's rewards and heading in the list past it, since rewards carry no end of their own; one two events list ends with the later, with both events' rewards. `tasks()` leaves out what is past its end (`taskOffered`) from that moment, with no read, so every pick list and `/want`'s boxes follow; `byKey` and `byVersion` still know it (a list open at the end, a report standing), and a report made before the end stands as its period says; its text typed past the end is a typed task (`readTask`). A task's key is `taskKeyOf` (an FNV-1a hash of `foldTaskText`), the value of its choice.
- The page shows an event's own tasks while the event runs (the snapshots above; design 4.1 had left it open); nothing reads event pages; a missing task is typed in full.

## Research periods

- The events feed's research flags (`extraData.generic.hasFieldResearchTasks`, `events.json` read where `events.min.json` lacks `extraData`) are kept as `ResearchEvent` rows by `RaidEventService` (`researchEvents()`, `ResearchEventProvider`), a stored event kept 30 days after its end, so a restart or an event dropped from the feed loses no period; one not begun that a read no longer lists (moved or cancelled) is dropped, as the raid events' (`removeNotSeen`); a read whose feed has no research flags at all (neither `events.min.json` nor `events.json`) keeps what is stored as it is, said once in the log. `onResearchChange(listener)` tells of every change (a read, the kept ones loaded): the timer and periods of the posts of the stops follow.
- **Only everyone's events** (plan 8R-7, the staging of 8R-6's F1: ScrapedDuck's Boston City Safari, local times and a research flag, cut Pittsburgh's day research at 10:00): `parseResearchEventFeed` reads an allow list of types, `RESEARCH_EVENT_TYPES` (Matt's R2, 2026-10-24: `event`, `community-day`, `research-day`, `pokemon-go-tour`, `wild-area`, `pokemon-go-fest`), and of those only everyone's (`isForEveryone`, `utilities.md`, "Everyone's events": nothing at the fixed times of another zone, the last three at local times whatever their name says, Matt, 2026-10-24, in place of the Global edition only; one whose id and name say no "global" is logged once). A City Safari and every other type are left out; a type off the list that the feed flags with research (not an in-person one, which is left out on purpose) is said once per type while the bot runs (`unreadResearchTypes`, `an event type with field research is not read`, with the type and the first such event's id), so a new type is noticed. `loadResearch` takes only the stored rows of such a kind (`isResearchEventKind`, review R1: a type on the list and no in-person type; a Wild Area, GO Tour or GO Fest row counts, as it was kept only after the zone test): a row kept before the fix (a dev database) stays stored and unused until `removeEnded` forgets it a month after its end; nothing deletes it. The dev database's `StopReport` rows keyed `event:pokemon-go-city-safari-boston-2026-rescheduled:…` drop out of the post after the fix (their period is no longer on).
- `src/domain/time/research-periods.ts` (pure): `periodAt(events, at, zone)` gives the period on (`day:<yyyy-mm-dd>` to local midnight; for a multi-day event, a rotation, `event:<id>:<yyyy-mm-dd>` per local day of it: its start and end rotate the tasks for good, and inside it the tasks roll over at each local midnight as on any day (Matt, 2026-10-22: Into the Wild's day 1 runs from 10:00 to midnight, its last day from midnight to 20:00, and the day's research after it is new); `event:<id>:<yyyy-mm-dd>` also for each day of a big event's hours, a window kept to the millisecond; an intra-day event is a window under which the day's reports wait and come back after it, `waiting`); `eventShape`: one local day is a `window`, a big event (`BIG_EVENT_TYPES`) over days whose hours end before midnight is `daily`, any other (crossing midnight included, Matt's answer 6) a `rotation`. `nextPeriodChange` is the next midnight, start or end. Midnight is Temporal's (`src/domain/time/zoned.ts`) in `settings.timezone`, DST days included.

## Reports and `/research`

- `StopReportService` (`ctx.stopReports`) reports a version of a task at one to five stops in the period on now (`reportResearch`): since plan 8R-5 each stop as `decideResearch` decides with the stop's row of the period locked (`saveUnless`; "Research rewards reported apart" below), the report it replaces given back; it ends with the period (`endsAt`). Listeners hear `changed(serverId, stops)` (the posts of the regions those stops lie in) and `reported(news)` (the notifier: only stops that did not have this very version in the period; since plan 8R-5 the news carries the reports they replaced, `replaced`, so that the notifier tells a narrowing of a "one of" from a first report). A replacement is a report of its own: the replaced row is deleted and a new one made under the same lock, so a report's version never changes what it is (its id; for Rocket, the id and `madeAt`, plan 8R-4L, review L1: a grunt refined in place keeps its id) (an old answer's Take back or "Same task at more stops…" finds its report gone and answers so; `/research remove` takes back by the id it read, and says `REPORT_CHANGED` where the report was replaced meanwhile). `takeBack(serverId, refs, by, as)` removes a member's own reports (`as` `member`), any for `staff` and for Pine's own undo of a grunt posted nowhere (`unposted`, plan 8H-2); a replaced report does not come back. ## Research rewards reported apart (plan 8R-5)

Design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-research-rewards-apart-design.md` (its review defaults R1–R15 and Matt's rulings R6, R7). The game fact: a task Leek Duck lists with several rewards is in the game several tasks of one text, each with one reward, the same for every player at a stop all day; an item or Mega Energy reward shows on the task before it is done, an encounter only after (so a task of two or more encounters can be reported before it is done as "one of" them).

- **Versions** (`src/domain/research/versions.ts`, pure): `taskVersions(task, catalogue)` gives one version per reward in the page's order (a reward the task lists twice, shiny or not, is one version, the first kept, and counts once towards "one of": review W4; value `<task key>:<reward hash>`, `rewardHash`: 8 hex digits, FNV-1a of `rewardKey`, `hexHash` of `src/domain/short-hash.ts`; label `Gible`, `Rare Candy ×3`, a costumed encounter `… (costumed)` (`COSTUMED_MARK`) where its name does not say what it wears, not where it does, `Pikachu wearing a Cosmog suit` (review L9, `wornBy`); `costumed` flags a costumed reward's version, L7; a version's costumed encounter carries `costumed: true` itself, kept in the report's detail, so `rewardsInShort` says `(costumed)` wherever its rewards are worded: the post's lines, the answer and its "was", "Already reported", the dialog and the DMs, review W1; a report of before has none, and `rewardKey` never reads it), then, for a task of two or more encounters, its "one of" (value the task's key alone, rewards its encounters only, label `one of Gible, Larvitar, Beldum`, `oneOfLabel`, the rewards `first` matches before the others). A task of one reward is that one version; of none, its key alone. `rewardKey` (moved from `leekduck.ts`, which merges a task listed twice by it) tells a costume only with the catalogue. `ResearchTaskService.versions(task)` and `byVersion(value)` (R1; `versionIndex`) work them out once per list and catalogue; the test fixture gives them too. A version gone from the list (a reward hash the task no longer has) is `TASK_LIST_CHANGED`.
- **Subjects** (`src/domain/research/reports.ts`, pure): a report's `subjectKey` is its version: `<task>`, `<task>:<hash>`, `typed:<text key>` or `typed:<text key>:<hash>` (R11: a typed task with its reward), read only by `researchSubjectOf` (never split on `:`; `taskOfSubject` the task). A report's detail keeps the rewards it stands for (the one reward, the encounters of "one of"), so the post, the DMs and the map read it as before. `isOneOf(report)` (R10): its task's key alone with two or more rewards in its detail; a report made before plan 8R-5 has every reward of its task there, items too, and reads as such a "one of" until its period ends (nothing converts it). No migration: `subjectKey` is `varchar(64)`, the longest 31 characters.
- **The decision** `decideResearch(standing, report, now, by, replacing)` against the stop's report of the report's own period only (R15: no `standingAt`, so a report under an intra-day window never meets the day's), each with the hashes of the rewards it stands for (`StopReportServiceOptions.rewardHash`, required, review L5: the game data's catalogue in `app.ts`, `fixtureRewardHash` of `test/support/stop-reports.ts` in tests; without the catalogue no reward would be costumed, and a costumed reward over its "one of" would ask): nothing standing or ended `new`; a reward over the "one of" of its task that holds it (or one of no reward, a typed task without one) `narrow`, replaced without asking; "one of" over one of its rewards `less` and the same version `again`, both reported already (`ResearchOutcome.already`); anything else a contradiction (another reward of the task, "one of" over an item or Mega Energy version, another task), `asks` (`ResearchOutcome.asks`, Matt's R7), unless it is the reporter's own report or one the member ticked (`replacing`, its id), `correct`, replaced.
