# The party history

Every party that ends is kept in relational tables beside the live `Party` table (plan 8H; spec `2026-10-16-cutover-docker-history-design.md`, 4.3), and since plan 8H-2 every report at a stop that ends ("Reports at stops" below): what it was, where and when, how it ended and how many took part, in a form that plain SQL counts and groups. The legacy bot's parties are imported once from its files. Members are named only with their opt-in. Read before changing the history, its recording, the opt-in or the import, or before writing a statistic over it.

## The tables (migrations 018, 020, 025, 026, 039 and 041)

- `PartyHistory`: one row per ended party. `source` is `live` (with `partyId`, the `Party.id`, unique) or `legacy` (with `legacyKey`, `<channel>:<creation ms>`, unique). `kind` is `raid`, `train`, `meetup` or `max` (a max battle, plan 8X-3). Where: `serverId` (the server's snowflake), `regionId`, `gymId`, `legacyGymId` (the legacy file's gym, kept also when the gym list no longer has it). None of them carries a foreign key: the history outlives a server's `Guild` row (a kick and a new invite neither delete it nor split it; Matt, 2026-10-16), and a region or gym deleted later stays named by its id. What: `name` and `description` (trains and meetups), `tier` (`T1` to `T5`, `Mega`, `MegaLegendary`, `SuperMega`, `Elite`, and the legacy `T2`, `T4`, `EX`; a max battle's `D1` to `D5` or `G`; null where not known), `shadow`, `mega`, `elite`, `ex`, `shiny` (null: not known), the boss as the catalogue knows it (`bossKey`, `bossDex`, `bossSpecies`, `bossForm`; a mega's form is `<SPECIES>_<MEGA…>`) or, where it does not, by the name it was given (`bossName`), `fastMove`, `chargedMove`. When (epoch ms): `createdAt`, `hatchTime`, `startTime` (trains and meetups), `endTime`, `endedAt` (null for the legacy parties of the cutover). How it ended: `ending` (below), and `closedBy` (migration 040, plan 8H-6: `staff` or `moderators` where they closed it, else null; "The boards" below). Who: `createdById`, `conductorId` (the conductor at the end, so null when the last one left, as one who presses Done does; every conductor is credited on their attendee row with the stops they led, `conducted`), only with that member's opt-in, else null; `anonymous` (the reporter asked not to be named in the post). Counts, so that the common questions need no join: `attendees` (members who signed up, also those who left), `interested`, `joined`, `here`, `done` (members by the furthest status they reached), the same with their +1s (`interestedHeads` …), `extras` (all +1s), `remote`, `departed` (left before the end, not by a split); `routeStops` (a train's route at the end); `trainKind` (migration 026, below); `splitFromId` (migration 020, below).
- `PartyHistoryAttendee`: a row per member who signed up, in the order they did (`seq`): `memberId` only with their opt-in, else null (the row still counts); `status` at the end (null: they left before it), `furthest` (`interested`, `joined`, `here`, `done`; asked whether they completed it counts as `here`), `groupId`, `extras`, `remote`, `team`, and the times the bot saw (`signedUpAt`, `joinedAt`: first coming or further, `hereAt`, `doneAt`, `leftAt`; null in the legacy history); `splitOff` (migration 020: 1 for a member who left with a group split off the train, 0 otherwise); `conducted` (migration 039, below: how many stops the member led the train to as its conductor, 0 for none and on every other row).
- `PartyHistoryGroup`: a row per group: `label`, `meetingTime` (the last one set, also after the clock cleared it; the steps of a group's meeting time name it by its letter and when it was opened, `B:<stamp>`, plan 8X-1B, since a group nobody is in goes and a later one may take its letter: a pruned B's time is never a new B's; a step of before the stamps names the letter alone and counts for that letter), `members` and `heads` at the end.
- `PartyHistoryStop`: a row per stop a train made, in order (`seq`; a loop makes a gym's stop twice): `gymId` (null where the gym list no longer has it, with `legacyGymId`; null for a Power Spot), `wayspotId` (migration 026, plan 8X-4: the Wayfarer id of a Power Spot the train stopped at, null for a gym), `arrivedAt`, `leftAt` (null where not known: the first stop is reached at the train's start when it was laid earlier; a legacy train's stops have no times).
- `HistoryOptIn`: a row per member and server (by snowflake, no foreign key) who opted in. `LeaderboardOptIn` (migration 040, plan 8H-6), the same shape: who switched the boards on ("The boards" below).
- `PartyHistoryStep`: what happens to an active party that its model does not keep (a member's status, a member who left, a member who went with a group split off a train (`split`), where a train is (`stop`: its place, the gym or null at the end; `spot`, plan 8X-4: at a Power Spot, `subject` the bare Wayfarer id, `value` null, so that a spot is no end of the route), a group's meeting time, the raid it went on from after a move, a member who became a train's conductor (`conduct`, plan 8H-6A) and who stopped being it (`unlead`)), read into the party's record when it ends and then deleted; it goes with its `Party` row. It holds the ids of everybody in an active party, as the party's own row does, until the party ends.
- Migration 020 (plan 8T): `PartyHistory.splitFromId`, in the record of a train that was split off another, that train's `partyId` (`char(36)`, null otherwise, no foreign key: a link only, each train is recorded on its own; Matt, 2026-10-17), with an index (`partyhistory_splitfromid_index`: the trains split off a train); `PartyHistoryAttendee.splitOff` (`tinyint(1) not null default 0`, written from `HistoryAttendee.splitOff`), so that SQL tells a member who went with a group from one who left. Each is added only when it is missing. `kind` of a step is `varchar(8)` since 018 and takes `split` as it is.
- Migration 025 (plan 8X-3): `PartyHistory.wayspotId` (`varchar(40)`, null), the Wayfarer id of a max battle's Power Spot, null for one at a pin (an "Unlisted power spot") and for every other party; no foreign key (a Wayspot an import deletes stays named by its id). Migration 026 (plan 8X-4) adds `PartyHistoryStop.wayspotId` the same way (`varchar(40)`, null, no foreign key, added only when missing), and `PartyHistory.trainKind` (`varchar(10)`, null, added only when missing): what a train went to, `raid`, `dynamax` or `gigantamax` (`HistoryRecord.trainKind`, `TrainParty.train.kind`; a legacy train is `raid`), null means the party is no train: 026 sets the trains recorded before it to `raid` (they all were), touching nothing else. A max battle has no `gymId`, its boss in the boss columns as a raid's, `shiny` as its post says it when it ends (`listedMaxBoss`: the max boss list's boss of its tier by catalogue entry or reported name; null where the list has none, final review X3-F4), and it took place (`completed`) when somebody completed it, as a raid. Added only when it is missing.
- Migration 039 (plan 8H-6A; design `2026-11-04-history-on-show-design.md`, Part D's "trains they conducted that others came to"): `PartyHistoryAttendee.conducted` (`smallint unsigned not null default 0`, added only when missing; the repository caps it at 65,535). Before it a record credited only the conductor at the end, and a conductor who leaves (Done on a train) stops being it, so most trains credited nobody and a handover only the last (Matt, 2026-11-05: record every conductor from then on; trains ended before keep no credit). The credit means the member led the train, not that they held the role for a moment (the controller's ruling in review 8H6A-1, 2026-11-05; Matt may still object): a take-over cleared at once, or followed only by Finish or a cleared route, earns nothing.
  - **The steps:** `partySteps` notes `conduct` (`subject` the member, `value` null) whenever a write leaves a train with a conductor it did not have before, and `unlead` (the same) for the conductor before whenever that one is no longer it, however either came about (`/conductor set` and `clear` and the map's, a takeover, leaving, Done, a group split off with the conductor in it; the new train of a split led by who went with it is noted at the write that settles it). Both come after the write's place step (`stop` or `spot`): where a write takes the train is the doing of whoever conducted it before that write. Member steps (`MEMBER_STEPS`), so `historyMembers` reads the opt-in of a conductor who left.
  - **The count** (`historyOfParty`, `ledStops`): for each member, the arrivals at a gym or Power Spot while they conducted, back moves and route edits included; Finish and a cleared route are not arrivals (`arrival`: a `spot` step, or a `stop` step with its gym, never a `stop` of `end` or `none`), over the steps of the lineage between their `conduct` and their next `unlead` (or the end of the steps); a member with none has 0 (absent from `HistoryAttendee.conducted`). Who conducted the train before its first step of either kind (a train going on when 039 came) is the member of a first `unlead`, or with neither kind of step its conductor at the end: they count from the first step. An arrival counts for whoever conducted the train, also when staff moved it, and the first stop of a route laid while a member conducts counts for them. The boards (plan 8H-6) credit `conducted >= 1`.
  - A conductor always has an attendee row: `setConductor` refuses a member not signed up, a split's new train is led only by a member who went with it, and the write that signs a member up notes their status. The row of a conductor who did not opt in counts them without naming them; an opt-out takes the id off the row and the count stays, as every count does, also for a train still going on when they opt out (its record finds no opt-in).
  - **Splits:** a split's new train counts its own steps only, never those of the train it was split off, and its being placed where it was split (its first place step, before its conductor's) is no arrival they led. A member who led both trains is counted in each, for what each did: the boards collapse a split lineage by `splitFromId` (each train split off another names it) so that a member is not credited twice for one train ride; how is plan 8H-6's to decide.
  - Anonymous trains are recorded the same: the boards decide what to show (plan 8H-6). Every other kind of party, every record of before 039 and the legacy history (whose files name a conductor, but no member is imported) have 0.
- Migration 041 (plan 8H-7A; design `2026-11-04-history-on-show-design.md`, Part F, "The card of an ended train" below): `PartyHistory.peakRiders` (`smallint unsigned`, null, capped at 65,535), the most members aboard a train at one moment, from its steps (`peakRidersOf` in `historyOfParty`, oldest first: a member is aboard from their first Here until they leave, go with a group split off, press Done (on a train, leaving) or go back to Interested; Coming after Here keeps them aboard, since every move of the train puts who is here back to Coming (`allAboard`); the new train of a split counts who is on Coming in the write that settles it as aboard (they rode on with the group, review R1; the same rule, `stepReach`, gives them their Here on the attendee row, so that the riders and the most at once agree, W1); the steps of one write all taken before the count; members, not heads, since the steps keep no +1s), null for a train whose steps never said a status (it ended before the history, or its steps were lost), for every other party (`HistoryRecord.peakRiders` absent), the legacy history and every record of before 041; and `PartyHistoryStop.regionIds` (`json`, null), the `Region.id`s the stop lay in when the train ended, in the region directory's order (`stopRegionsInGuild`, handed in by `PartyHistoryOptions.stopRegions` as `HistoryInput.stopRegions`, asked when the record is made in the ending transaction, the sweep's records too), `[]` for a stop in no region (a gym deleted since), null where not known (before 041, legacy). A list in the stop's own row, as `StopReport.regionIds` keeps a report's (read with the stop, its order kept; nothing counts the history by these regions in SQL). Each column is added only when it is missing.
- `EndedPartyPost`: the post and forum of a party whose `Party` row was deleted once its record was written, for what still reads them (below): no member, no party id. Its rows are kept for good, as the ended `Party` rows were before: the post can be reopened by a late message any time.
- Text a member wrote (a train's or meetup's name, a meetup's description, a group's label) is kept with every member it names written `@member` (`withoutMembers`, `src/domain/history/party-history.ts`; mentions and bare ids of 17 to 20 digits, an emoji's tag stays), live and legacy alike.
- `createdById` of a raid whose reporter asked not to be named in the post (`anonymous`) is kept all the same when the reporter opted in to the history: a statistic shown to others later must honour `anonymous`.
- What else reads the history: the activity heatmap's buckets (below, "The activity heatmap"), which the region tally sums ("Raids by region: the region tally"), and `HistoryRepository.raidsByRegion(since)` (plan 8W-6) counts each region's raids and max battles (`kind` `raid` or `max`, by `regionId`, `createdAt` from `since`), the rank of a region in the line that waits for Google (`gyms-and-regions.md`); live and legacy records alike.

How a party ended (`ending`): `completed` whenever it took place (somebody completed the raid, or was at the train or meetup), however it was closed; otherwise `expired` (the clock), `cancelled` (`/delete`, its region removed, its post deleted by hand), `cutover` (a legacy party still going on at the cutover), `unknown` (nobody said: a legacy raid nobody completed, whose files do not say whether it ran out or was deleted; a party the sweep recorded, which runs at start and hourly).

## Recording live parties

- `PartyStore` asks `PartyHistory` (`src/services/party-history.ts`) for a note of every write (`noteOf`, pure: `partySteps` and `historyOfParty` in `src/domain/history/party-history.ts`), which the storage writes with the party. Steps alone (every click) follow the party's write in one insert of their own, no transaction (`round-trips.db.test.ts` counts it); a late step of a party that has ended is dropped. A write that ends the party writes its record in the same transaction, behind a savepoint, reading the opt-ins under a shared lock (an opt-out under way finishes first). A record that fails is rolled back alone and the end stands; a deadlock (on the party's row or in the record) runs the whole transaction again (`retryOnDeadlock`, `DEADLOCK_TRIES`); when one persists, or another failure took the whole transaction with it, the party's write is run again alone. `PartyRepository` (over the pool, never a caller's transaction) hands the note to `historyFailed` once the party's write has committed: `PartyHistory.failed` tries it again after 1, 10 and 60 minutes (and once at shutdown), then gives up with an error in the log. The write gives back the record it committed (plan 8H-7A, review F-B1): `HistoryStorage.write` answers it with its server (undefined for steps alone, a move, a drop, a party recorded already or gone), `PartyRepository.save` answers `Saved` (`false` for a row that is gone, else `{ recorded }`: the record of the try that committed, never one a deadlock rolled back; none where the record failed, also when the party's write ran again alone; since plan 8H-7B a `WrittenRecord`, with its `PartyHistory.id` as `historyId`, which the insert itself gives, for the ended train's link to the map), and `PartyStore.update` hands it on as `recorded` (not `updateBoth`: a split never ends a train with a record). `MemoryHistory` and `MemoryPartyStorage` do the same.
- Once the record is written, in the same transaction, the party's `Party` row is deleted (Matt, 2026-10-16): it holds its maker's and every member's id. What still reads ended parties is the hourly `sweepPosts` (`endedPosts`: the posts of ended parties, to archive one a late message reopened) and the forums parties named (`partyForums`: `/region list`, `/admin setup-forums`); both read `EndedPartyPost` beside the rows of parties not yet recorded. Nothing else reads an ended row: the post's controls find a party in the store's memory, the copies and the post's last showing use the party in memory, access grants follow the store, and the repository has no other reader of ended rows. So once a party is recorded, no id of a member who did not opt in is left in the party and history tables (`history-recording.db.test.ts` checks every one). A member's grant to another region's forum (`PartyAccessGrant`) names the party too, but it is taken back when the party ends, and it is about access, not about what the member did.
- With the forums' upkeep (`PartyScheduler`, at start after the login, and hourly), `PartyHistory.sweep` records every ended party whose row is still there (its record failed every try, the bot stopped first, or it ended before the history existed, with steps or without) as `unknown`, then deletes its row: a record that gave up keeps member ids for an hour at most, not until a restart. A party with no post (it never was one) is dropped; a row that cannot be read is logged and left, as the store leaves one; a raid that moved is left to the party it goes on as; a party whose note waits for its next try (`failed`), as its own or as the `from` step of the raid that went on from it, is left to that try, which knows how it ended. It reads the rows first and logs how many it is about to record (nothing when there are none), then how far it got every 1,000 (the first start on a database with many old ended rows records them all, in the background). It asks the scheduler's stop before each party (`sweep({ isStopped })`) and ends there, so a shutdown does not wait for it. `partyId` is unique: a party is never recorded twice.
- A record takes along only parties it went on from that have ended, locked first, and never walks past one that has not: a move that failed (the old raid stayed) leaves the old raid to its own record. Raids that moved before migration 018 have no `from` step: the old and the new party are each recorded, so such a raid counts twice.
- A change that ends a party says why with `ended` in its result (`PartyEnd`): the scheduler `clock`; `PartyService.end` `cancelled` unless told otherwise; a deleted post `cancelled`; a raid that moved to another region `moved`; a party that never had its post `failed`, which is dropped. A change that says nothing is recorded as `unknown`. A raid that moved goes on as a new party whose first step (`from`) names the old one (and the old one's end writes it again): the new party's record takes the old one's steps and row along (one record, with the times from before the move; `createdAt` stays), and nothing records the old one by itself.
- Members, the maker and the conductor are named as their opt-ins are when the record is written. Every conductor a train had is credited on their attendee row with the stops they led (`conducted`, migration 039 above).
- A train a group was split off (plan 8T) is recorded on its own, and so is the train the group became (`splitFromId` names the old one). A write that adds a split to `train.splitOff` notes who left in it as `split`, not `left` (`partySteps`): they are attendees of the old train up to the split (`leftAt` the split, `status` null, `splitOff` 1) and not `departed`. The new train is stored with its post before the old train loses the group, and is `pending` until the one write of both trains (`PartyStore.updateBoth`: the old train's loss of the group and the new train's carry-over in one transaction, each with its note; `train.splitFrom.pending`, `trains.md`): `partySteps` notes nothing of it while it is pending, and the write that settles it as if the train were new then. So its history starts at the split and holds nobody who never was on it (a member who left the group while the new post was made): its members sign up then (`joinedAt` then; `hereAt` then too, and `furthest` at least `here`, for who is on Coming or Here: a member on Coming at the split rode the old train on to its next stop and rides the new one, so they count as having come to it, review W1, in its card, its `ending` and the boards and stats, which already collapse a split lineage into one ride), its first stop is where it was split (reached at its start when split before it), and its record's `createdAt` is its first step, the split (the `Party` row's `createdAt` is when it was made, the time its post took before). A pending train that ends, or is taken back, is dropped (`drop`), as one that failed. A member who rode both counts in both.
- Tests: `partyServiceWith()` records into `MemoryHistory` (`history`: `records`, `steps`, `posts`, `optIn(guildRow, member)`, `failing`; it shares the memory storage's rows and deletes them as the repository does) through `recorder` (`flush()` runs the tries that wait; over the database its tries go to `kept.historyStorage`; `sweep()` reads the storage's rows, and those in `storage.unreadable` as unreadable; the scheduler's upkeep runs it; `historySweepProgressEvery` sets its progress lines); `history-recording.db.test.ts` pins the repository; a `PartyRepository` in a test that expects no failure takes `HISTORY_MUST_NOT_FAIL`.

## Reports at stops (migration 036, plan 8H-2)

Design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-history-on-show-design.md`, Part A: every version of a field research or Team GO Rocket report (`stop-reports.md`) that ends is kept, from the cutover on, for the boards and a member's own stats of later parts. The boards and a member's own stats read it (plan 8H-6, "The boards and a member's own stats" below).

- **On the live row** (`StopReport`, fixed when the report is made): `cell`, the S2 cell of level 14 of its stop (`reportCell`, `REPORT_CELL_LEVEL`, `src/domain/stops/report-history.ts`, a token of `@pine/wayspot-core/s2`: about 600 m across, Q1; level 15 was turned down as a block or two); `regionIds`, the `Region.id`s whose outlines held the stop (JSON; `StopReportServiceOptions.guilds` and `.regions`: the server's row, then `regionsAtPoint` over the `RegionDirectory`, as `RocketService.channelsAt`, the notifier and the map find a stop's regions, one per channel; asked before the save, outside its transaction). `[]` and null are kept apart: `[]` is a stop in no region (or a server without a row), null is unknown (a lookup that failed, logged as `could not find the regions of a stop`, or a row of before 036), and a board by region must count neither as a region of its own; `confirmedBy`, the other members who met this very version with a report of their own (JSON, each once, never its reporter: `confirmedWith`). All three are null on a row of before 036. The row is swept within the day, so these ids live no longer than its `reporterId`.
- **`StopReportHistory`**: a row per version that ended: `reportId` and `reportMadeAt` (the live row's id and `madeAt`: a version, review L1; a grunt refined in place keeps its id, so its versions are two records), `serverId`, `kind`, `periodKey`, `subjectKey`, `detail` (the live row's JSON with every text in it through `withoutMembers`, `detailWithoutMembers`: any JSON is walked, so a row this version cannot read is kept too), `cell`, `regionIds`, `madeAt`, `endsAt`, `endedAt`, `reporterId` (only with the reporter's opt-in to that server's history, read when the record is written, else null), `confirmations` (how many members `confirmedBy` held, with a narrowing member counted once more where they were not there yet), `ending` (`varchar(20)`). No stop key, name, address, picture or point, and no reporter's name. No foreign key: the records outlive the server's row, as the party history does. Indexes: `(serverId, reporterId)` for the opt-out, `(serverId, endedAt)` for the boards, `(reportId, reportMadeAt)` for `restore` and `endedAs`. A cell is to be shown only in counts of the whole server, with the heatmap's minimum of three, never per member (the design's rule 1).
- **Endings** (`ReportEnding`), decided by the domain (`src/domain/stops/report-history.ts`, review H1), never by the repository:

  | Ending | When |
  |---|---|
  | `stood` | the sweep forgot it at its end; and any row whose `endsAt` had passed when another report took its row (review H3), `endedAt` its own `endsAt` (`endingOfRow`) |
  | `narrowed` | a reward replaced its "one of" (`decideResearch`'s `narrow`) |
  | `refined` | a grunt refined in place: the version before the refine, `endedAt` the refine's written `madeAt` (review H15) |
  | `corrected` | `correct` of the reporter's own report (review H2) |
  | `contradicted` | `correct` of a report the member ticked in "Replace field research?" (review H2) |
  | `churned` | Rocket's `report` over what stood (grunt over grunt, hideout over anything), and any other row still standing that a save takes (a grunt of yesterday past midnight, fix A2) |
  | `withdrawn` | taken back by its own reporter, whoever asked (`takeBackEnding`, review H8) |
  | `staff` | taken back by staff, somebody else's |
  | `unposted` | Pine's undo of a grunt posted nowhere (`RocketService`, `takeBack` as `unposted`), the grunt a hideout put back replaces (`restore`), a grunt `overtaken` while its messages went out where another report replaced it (`StopReportService.overtaken`: its record goes from `churned` to `unposted` by its version, `endedAs(id, madeAt, ending, from)`; review H4, 8H2-2; one taken back keeps `withdrawn` or `staff`, a version `refined` in place stays `refined`), and the version before the refine of a grunt posted nowhere and refined meanwhile (`StopReportService.refinedUnseen`, from `refined`, 8H2-1: nobody saw it) |
  | `unreadable` | a row this version cannot read (a kind it does not know, or a detail of another shape), of any kind, that a save took (`endingOfRow` with `readable` false, 8H2-5): it counts toward nothing. The sweep records such a row as it ended, `stood` |

  `researchFate` and `rocketFate` map a decision, with the standing report's reporter and the new one's, to `{ ending, confirms }`: `again` and `less` (research) and `already` (Rocket) by another member confirm the standing version (`keep`); `narrow` by another confirms the row it narrows (its record counts them). Nothing is written to the live row on a `keep` but `confirmedBy`: its `madeAt`, the version, stays. A refine's new version starts with none (`confirmedBy` null).
- **Where it is written** (`StopReportRepository`; its twin `MemoryStopReports` keeps `places`, `confirmedBy` and `records` beside the rows):
  - `saveUnless(report, decide, { standingAt, place, restoring })`: `decide` answers a `StandingVerdict` or a `StandingDecision` (`verdict`, `ending`, `confirms`); every row the save takes away is recorded in its transaction, behind the savepoint `stop_report_history`, at `savedAt` (the written `madeAt`, or `standingAt` where later: a hideout put back keeps its old `madeAt`). A record that fails is rolled back to the savepoint alone and logged (`could not record stop reports that ended; their records are lost`), and the report is saved all the same (review H11). Where the rollback to the savepoint fails too (the failure took the transaction with it; P1, as `PartyRepository` meets it), the failure itself is thrown: a deadlock runs the whole save again (`retryOnDeadlock`), any other failure runs the save once more without records, which are logged as lost (`could not record stop reports that ended; saved without their records`).
  - `restoring` (a hideout put back, review H4): that version's record is deleted in the save's transaction, not behind the savepoint (P3): where that fails the save fails, and `RocketService`'s `undo` takes the grunt back as `unposted` instead, so a hideout never counts twice. In `undoRefined` (a grunt posted nowhere and refined meanwhile) a failed restore takes nothing back: the refine's own turn takes its version back as `unposted`, and the version before it ends `unposted` (`refinedUnseen`, 8H2-1). The hideout's new row takes the record's `cell` and `regionIds` (P4; the stop's cell and unknown regions where no record is kept), and starts with no confirmations: who confirmed it before it was replaced are not carried over (restore is rare).
  - `remove(id, serverId, why, madeAt?)` (`Removal`: `by`, `as` (`TakeBackBy`: `member`, `staff`, `unposted`), `at`): the ending read from the locked row (`takeBackEnding`), behind the same savepoint, a failure met as `saveUnless` meets it. `StopReportService.takeBack(serverId, refs, by, as)` takes the place of the old `staff` flag: a `member` takes back their own only, `staff` and `unposted` any.
  - `removeEnded(before)` (the hourly sweep): in transactions of `SWEEP_BATCH` (500) rows at `read committed` (P6: no gap is locked), the ended rows taken in the order they ended (`endsAt`, then `id`, along `stopreport_ends_index`) and locked, the opt-ins of their reporters read under a shared lock, the records inserted, the rows deleted. No savepoint (review H10): a batch whose records fail is logged at error level (`could not record a batch of ended stop reports; trying them one by one`) and run again row by row, each row in a transaction of its own, so that a row whose record fails keeps only itself back for the next sweep (`could not record an ended stop report; it is kept for the next sweep`, P7). After `ONE_BY_ONE_FAILURES` (5) failures in a row the pass gives up: the rest of the batch is kept back unread and the sweep ends there, with one line a sweep (`gave up recording ended stop reports one by one; the rest are kept for the next sweep`, with how many it kept back; R2, 8H2-3), so a failure that hits every row costs a few lines an hour. A row whose record still fails `KEPT_UNRECORDED_MS` (a week) after its end is deleted without it (8H2-4), logged at error level with its id and kind and no member (`deleted an ended stop report without its record, which failed for a week`): its report is lost to the history, but no member's id stays in the live table for good. It reads in TypeScript rather than `insert … select`, since the detail goes through `withoutMembers`.
  - **The lock order, and the opt-out:** every record reads the opt-ins of its reporters from `HistoryOptIn` under a shared lock, after the `StopReport` rows it records are locked, as a party's record does; `HistoryRepository.optOut` deletes the member's opt-in row first, then takes their id out of the records. So an opt-out under way finishes first and the record finds no opt-in, or the opt-out waits for the record and then takes the id out of it; a record never keeps the id of a member who opted out. No test races the two (it would wait on real time): the order is this paragraph's. An opt-in is not retroactive: a report ended before it keeps no id; one standing when the member opts in is recorded with it, as a party under way is.

## The activity heatmap (migration 038, plan 8H-4)

Design `2026-11-04-history-on-show-design.md`, Part B: where raids and max battles happen (trains' stops among them since plan 8H-9), by place, month, weekday and hour, for the web map's Activity layer (`web.md`, "The activity heatmap on the map"). Places are counted by parties, never by people (the design's rule 1): no member column anywhere.

- **What counts** (`countsAsActivity`, `src/domain/history/activity.ts`, the one function the region tally, Part C, takes through the buckets and the raids per day, Part G, take as it is, below): a record of kind `raid` or `max` whose `ending` is not `cancelled` (review H17): `completed`, `expired`, `unknown` and `cutover` count, live and legacy alike (a legacy raid nobody completed is `unknown`; `failed` is never recorded). Meetups do not count. A train counts no party of its own, but since plan 8H-9 each stop it made counts as a raid or a max battle (`countedParties`, "A train's stops count as raids" below): the adding turns every record into the parties it counts as, and `bucketOf` and `dayOf` take each of them.
- **A record's bucket** (`bucketOf`): when, `coalesce(hatchTime, createdAt)` as the tested query `raids-by-hour` below takes it (only its "when": that query counts every raid, cancelled ones too, while the heatmap counts by `countsAsActivity`), as the local month (`yyyy-mm`), ISO weekday (1 Monday to 7 Sunday) and hour in `settings.timezone` (`activityWhen`, through `zoned`; both 1 AMs of the night the clocks go back are hour 1); a time no `yyyy-mm` holds (beyond year 9999, before year 0, no moment at all), or one outside the heatmap's months (before `ACTIVITY_FIRST_MONTH`, 2016-07, the game's launch, or after the month after that of the adding's clock, `ActivityRepositoryOptions.now`; 8H4-3: one bad time would stretch History's slider; next month allowed, review F1, since a record added before the month turns may already be of it and a bucket never moves, while the slider still ends at this month), goes to no place at `UNKNOWN_MONTH` (`0000-00`), weekday 0 and hour 0, which no window reads and the slider's months leave out, so that one odd record never stalls the server's adding; where, a raid's `gymId` (`gym`, the id in decimal), a max battle's `wayspotId` (`spot`, the Wayfarer id), else `none` with `placeId` `0` (a raid without a gym, a legacy one whose gym the import did not find among them; a max battle at a pin); the region, `regionId` or 0 where none; counts `raids` or `maxes` 1, `completed` 1 for `ending` `completed`, `heads` `hereHeads + doneHeads` (those who came). Whether a gym or Power Spot is still kept is decided when the map reads the buckets, not here: a bucket keeps its id, so a gym added back is drawn again without a rebuild.
- **`ActivityBucket`**: primary key `(serverId, regionId, placeKind, placeId, month, weekday, hour)`, the counts `int unsigned`; indexes `(serverId, weekday, hour)` (Expected now and the presets read by their hours) and `(serverId, month)` (History by its months, and the slider's first and last month). `regionId` lets the map leave out a region whose forum the member is barred from, as the party layer does. No foreign key: the counts outlive the server's row, as the history does.
- **`ActivityProgress (serverId, lastHistoryId)`** and the adding (`ActivityRepository.addNew`, a step of the scheduler's upkeep after `history.sweep`, at start and hourly; review H20: never in a record's transaction): for each server with a history, the records above its mark in batches of `ACTIVITY_BATCH` (2,000), read along the index `partyhistory_serverid_id_index (serverId, id)` that 038 adds to `PartyHistory`, each batch one transaction at `read committed`: the mark's row taken exclusively from the first statement (an upsert that changes nothing where it is there, `on duplicate key update lastHistoryId = lastHistoryId`, then `for update`: `insert ignore` would take a shared lock on a row that is there, and two adders holding one each deadlock as both ask for the exclusive one), the records read `for share`, the rows the counting reads beside them (plan 8H-9, `trainsOf`, plain reads whatever the ids of what they find: the stops and attendees of the batch's trains and of the trains around its raids by one `trainCardRows`, three reads a batch, the raids along the batch's trains by `raidsAlongEach`, W6: one read of the union of their places over their span, split in memory by each train's own bounds (`alongTheWay`, as `raidsAlong` reads for one; measured on a month of 2,035 live trains and 1,965 raids, 4,000 records: a rebuild in about 1.0 to 1.6 s against 3.4 to 4.1 s with a read per train), and the trains around the batch's raids, one read; "A train's stops count as raids" below), each record turned into the parties it counts as (`countedParties`), their buckets summed (`sumBuckets`) and upserted (`as new on duplicate key update raids = raids + new.raids …`), the mark moved to the batch's last id. The shared lock is what makes the mark safe: a record whose transaction has not committed yet (a party's end, a retry of a record that failed) may hold an id below one committed already, and a plain read would move the mark past it for good; the locking read waits for it instead (`activity-repository.db.test.ts`, a real-time lock wait). Two windows remain where a record can still be passed over, both between an insert's steps inside InnoDB: its id allocated (`auto_increment`) before its row is in the clustered index, and its row in the clustered index before its entry is in the secondary index `(serverId, id)` the read goes along; a batch that reads past an id in either window moves the mark beyond a record it never saw. The loss is one party missing from the heatmap, never one counted twice; `./pine rebuild-activity` repairs it. No gap is locked, so parties ending meanwhile write their records unhindered; a deadlock (an opt-out updating the same records) runs the batch again (`retryOnDeadlock`). The stop of the clock is asked before each batch: the first start after the legacy import adds its records over several batches, and a shutdown does not wait for them all. A record is never changed once written, as the buckets read it: an opt-out takes member ids out, which no bucket holds. So adding is enough: a train's stop and the raid it joined are counted once by whichever record came first ("A train's stops count as raids", the pairing rule, below), whatever batch or upkeep each falls in. The bot logs `added records of the history to the activity heatmap` with how many, when there were any.
- **The rebuild**: `npm run activity:rebuild [-- --server <id>]` or `./pine rebuild-activity [--server <id>]` (`src/ops/activity-rebuild-cli.ts` over `rebuildStep`, exit 0 done, 1 failed, 2 used wrongly): per server (every server with a history or buckets, or the one named), its buckets deleted and its mark set to 0 in one transaction (since plan 8H-8 its raids per day and their mark too, below) (the mark's row taken first, as the adding takes it; a deadlock with an adding under way runs the reset again, `retryOnDeadlock`), then the adding from 0; it answers each server's final mark and its records up to it (`RebuiltServer`, one count along `(serverId, id)`; 8H4-7: what the run read alone is only its share beside the hourly adding, and beside another rebuild of the same server there is no final mark to say). Safe to run again, and while the bot runs: both take the mark's row first, so no batch counts twice; the map shows a server's activity in part while its rebuild runs. It is needed only when the rules of what counts or of a bucket change (this file; plan 8H-9's trains' stops did: the buckets and days written before it hold none, so a rebuild follows its deploy, `operations.md`), or when buckets were lost; the legacy import's records are added by the hourly upkeep like any other, and the import's docs suggest the rebuild only to count them at once.
- **Reading** (`ActivityReader`: `buckets(server, window)` by a mode's hours or months, `months(server)` the first and last month of any bucket, `regionSums(server, months)` the region tally's sums below; `MemoryActivity` is its twin, both running `activityReaderCases`), and the weights (`activityWeights`) are the map's: `web.md`.

## A train's stops count as raids (plan 8H-9)

Matt's ruling, 2026-11-06: a train's stops are raids that never had a party of their own, so they count wherever the history counts raids: the heatmap's buckets (and so the region tally) and the raids per day (and so the big days and "On this day"). A legacy train's stops have no times: it counts every one. A live train whose stops have no times (it ended before its steps were kept, its stops from its route) made none the card counts, and counts nothing.
- **When:** a stop at its arrival (a first stop laid before the start was reached at the start, as the recording keeps it); a legacy train's stops at its start, else when it was made. Through `knownActivityTime`, as every "when".

Adding a train, a stop that pairs with a raid of a lower id counts nothing (`joinedStops`, the raids read as the card reads them); adding a raid or max battle, it counts nothing where a train of a lower id has a counted stop that pairs with it (`joinedRaid`, the trains `pairedTrain` gives: live, `trainStopsCount`, the card's stops). The raid's record wins where it came first (its boss, its heads); the stop's (the train's boss where the kinds match, no heads) where the raid ran on past the train. Nothing waits: a train is added at the first upkeep after it ended, and so is everything after it. The adding goes in id order and the rebuild replays the same order, so a rebuild counts what the upkeeps counted.
- **The trains around a batch's raids** (`trainsAroundQuery`, one read a batch, `ActivityRepository.trainsAround`): the server's live trains of an ending whose stops count (V5: the very list `trainStopsCount` reads, passed in the query) along `partyhistory_server_kind_createdat_index (serverId, kind, createdAt)`, made from `trainReachMs` before the first raid stood (`raidTime`) up to the end of the last, ended once the first stood, below the last raid's id; their rows by the same `trainCardRows` as the batch's own trains, their counted stops indexed by gym and Power Spot once a batch (`trainsByPlace`), so that each raid is paired only with the trains that stopped at its place (`trainsAt`). `trainReachMs` is how long after it was made a train may still stop: its end at the furthest a member may set it (`maximumMeetupLeadtime` days and one) or its start (`maxMeetupLeadupDuration`) where later, and `OPEN_PARTY_HOURS` more: with the usual settings (`maxMeetupLeadupDuration` 133,920 minutes, 93 days; `maximumMeetupLeadtime` 93) the later of 93 and 93 + 1 days, and a day: 95 days. **The window left:** a train whose times were taken away again and again, or one staff kept (`/admin save-party`, `PartyService.keep`: a saved party is never archived by the clock), goes on past that reach; a stop it made later is not seen from a raid recorded after it, and both count. ## The card of an ended train (migration 041, plan 8H-7A)

Design `2026-11-04-history-on-show-design.md`, Part F (the brief's review F-B1 to F-B15 and Matt's rulings of 2026-11-05). The post's side is 8H-7A; the web's (a month's list of trains, a still route line, legacy trains) is 8H-7B, which takes the same function over rows read back.

- **One function** (`trainCard(record, stops, attendees, raids)`, `src/domain/history/train-card.ts`, pure, over the record's plain rows as the ending transaction wrote them or as they are read back; `raids` undefined where they could not be read). It names nobody (no conductor, no rider), an anonymous train's card is the same. Its facts: `stops` (how many, and the time), `regionIds`, `riders`, `peakRiders`, `raids`, each null where the card leaves its line out.
- **Stops and time** (`stopWindows`, F-B3, F-B4, F-B10): arrivals at or before `endedAt` only (a route laid early is reached at the start, which may come after a `/delete`), every time capped at `endedAt`; a stop's time ends at its `leftAt`, else at the next arrival; an open last stop at the first of the train's `endTime` and the last rider's departure (Leave, or Done, which on a train is leaving, once every member who came had gone) that came after its arrival, else at `endedAt` (review A2: a train run past its end time, or moved after everybody left, still stayed at its last stop); any one stop at most `TRAIN_STOP_MAX_DWELL_MS` (30 minutes, Matt), for the time and the raids alike; a stop of less than `TRAIN_STOP_MIN_DWELL_MS` (a minute) is not counted (the rows stay as they are). A loop's second lap counts. The time is the sum of the counted stops' capped times (review A1: a train that sat an hour at one stop is not said to have run that hour), not the span from the first arrival to the last. A train that counts no stop (none reached by its end, or only moves made by mistake) never left: the card says so and its riders.
- **Regions crossed:** each counted stop's `regionIds` in visit order, a region once at its first visit; null where there is none to name (no counted stop knows its regions, a record of before 041, or every one lay in none).

## The questions as SQL

Each question as a query: `history-queries.db.test.ts` reads every block below by its name and checks its answer on a small history, so a change here is a change there. Run the setup first, in the same session: the server (by its snowflake, in the tables' collation, or MySQL refuses to compare it with the column) and its time zone (the stack's MySQL has the zone tables; another server may need `mysql_tzinfo_to_sql`). Times are turned into the server's local time from epoch milliseconds as `convert_tz('1970-01-01' + interval <ms> div 1000 second, '+00:00', @zone)`, which does not depend on the session's time zone.

```sql setup
set @guild = '<server id>' collate utf8mb4_0900_ai_ci, @zone = 'America/New_York'
```

The most active members (only those who opted in have an id): parties they were at, and raids they completed.

```sql most-active-members
select a.memberId, count(*) as parties, sum(h.kind = 'raid' and a.furthest = 'done') as completed
from PartyHistoryAttendee a
join PartyHistory h on h.id = a.historyId
where h.serverId = @guild and a.memberId is not null and a.furthest in ('here', 'done')
group by a.memberId
order by parties desc, a.memberId
limit 20
```

Raids per gym (the name is the gym list's, HTML-encoded as the legacy bot stores it; a gym deleted since has none).

```sql raids-per-gym
select h.gymId, g.name, count(*) as raids
from PartyHistory h
left join Gym g on g.id = h.gymId
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and h.gymId is not null
group by h.gymId, g.name
order by raids desc, h.gymId
limit 20
```

Raids per region (by its channel: the region's name is its channel's on Discord).

```sql raids-per-region
select h.regionId, r.channelId, count(*) as raids
from PartyHistory h
left join Region r on r.id = h.regionId
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and h.regionId is not null
group by h.regionId, r.channelId
order by raids desc, h.regionId
```

Raids per boss (the catalogue's key, or the name a boss the catalogue does not know was given).

```sql raids-per-boss
select coalesce(h.bossKey, h.bossName) as boss, count(*) as raids
from PartyHistory h
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and coalesce(h.bossKey, h.bossName) is not null
group by boss
order by raids desc, boss
```

Raids per tier.

```sql raids-per-tier
select h.tier, count(*) as raids
from PartyHistory h
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and h.tier is not null
group by h.tier
order by raids desc, h.tier
```

Which bosses drew the largest groups: who came (here or done) with their +1s, on average and at most.

```sql largest-groups
select coalesce(h.bossKey, h.bossName) as boss, count(*) as raids,
       round(avg(h.hereHeads + h.doneHeads), 1) as averageCame,
       max(h.hereHeads + h.doneHeads) as mostCame
from PartyHistory h
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and coalesce(h.bossKey, h.bossName) is not null
group by boss
order by averageCame desc, boss
```

Raids by hour of the day, local time: of the hatch, or of the report where there is no hatch time.

```sql raids-by-hour
select hour(t.local) as hour, count(*) as raids
from (select convert_tz('1970-01-01' + interval coalesce(h.hatchTime, h.createdAt) div 1000 second,
                        '+00:00', @zone) as local
      from PartyHistory h
      where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid') t
group by hour
order by hour
```

Raids by day of the week.

```sql raids-by-weekday
select dayname(min(t.local)) as weekday, count(*) as raids
from (select convert_tz('1970-01-01' + interval coalesce(h.hatchTime, h.createdAt) div 1000 second,
                        '+00:00', @zone) as local
      from PartyHistory h
      where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid') t
group by weekday(t.local)
order by weekday(t.local)
```

Raids by month.

```sql raids-by-month
select date_format(t.local, '%Y-%m') as month, count(*) as raids
from (select convert_tz('1970-01-01' + interval h.createdAt div 1000 second, '+00:00', @zone)
               as local
      from PartyHistory h
      where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid') t
group by month
order by month
```

Raids by season (meteorological: winter is December to February).

```sql raids-by-season
select case when month(t.local) in (12, 1, 2) then 'winter'
            when month(t.local) in (3, 4, 5) then 'spring'
            when month(t.local) in (6, 7, 8) then 'summer'
            else 'autumn' end as season,
       count(*) as raids
from (select convert_tz('1970-01-01' + interval h.createdAt div 1000 second, '+00:00', @zone)
               as local
      from PartyHistory h
      where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid') t
group by season
order by field(season, 'winter', 'spring', 'summer', 'autumn')
```

How fast groups filled: minutes from the report until the third member said they were coming (live raids only: the legacy history has no times). Change `nth = 3` for another count.

```sql how-fast-groups-filled
select count(*) as raids, round(avg(t.minutes), 1) as averageMinutes
from (select (a.joinedAt - h.createdAt) / 60000 as minutes,
             row_number() over (partition by h.id order by a.joinedAt, a.seq) as nth
      from PartyHistory h
      join PartyHistoryAttendee a on a.historyId = h.id
      where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and a.joinedAt is not null) t
where t.nth = 3
```

Who was interested or said they were coming but never came, in raids that took place.

```sql signed-up-never-came
select a.furthest, count(*) as members
from PartyHistoryAttendee a
join PartyHistory h on h.id = a.historyId
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and h.ending = 'completed'
  and a.furthest in ('interested', 'joined')
group by a.furthest
order by field(a.furthest, 'interested', 'joined')
```

Trains by length: stops on the route, stops made (at Power Spots too, plan 8X-4), the gyms among them, and minutes from the first arrival to the last departure.

```sql trains-by-length
select h.name, h.routeStops, count(s.seq) as stopsMade, count(distinct s.gymId) as gyms,
       round((max(s.leftAt) - min(s.arrivedAt)) / 60000) as minutes
from PartyHistory h
left join PartyHistoryStop s on s.historyId = h.id
where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'train'
group by h.id, h.name, h.routeStops
order by stopsMade desc, h.id
```

The busiest gyms, for hot spots on the map: raids there and train stops made there, with where the gym is (a train's stop at a Power Spot is at no gym, and left out).

```sql busiest-gyms
select g.id as gymId, g.name, g.lat, g.lon, count(*) as visits
from (select h.gymId from PartyHistory h
      where h.serverId = @guild and h.kind = 'raid' and h.gymId is not null
      union all
      select s.gymId from PartyHistoryStop s
      join PartyHistory h on h.id = s.historyId
      where h.serverId = @guild and s.gymId is not null) v
join Gym g on g.id = v.gymId
group by g.id, g.name, g.lat, g.lon
order by visits desc, g.id
limit 50
```
