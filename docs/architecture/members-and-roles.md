# Members and roles

What the bot knows of members without a member list: their settings and rows, one member looked up, who of many can see a channel, and self-assigned roles with their menus. Read before changing member settings, member lookups or `/iam`, `/iamnot` and `/admin self-roles`.

## Settings and rows

- Member settings are read through `ctx.users` (`get`, `getMany`, `update`, `withNickname`). Whoever is about to ping several members reads them in one go with `settingsOf(users, ids, log)` (`src/services/party-change.ts`): a member without a row has the defaults, and a read that fails is logged and pings nobody. Tests use `MemoryUsers` (`test/support/users.ts`).
- Whether a member is named in the party history is per server (`ctx.historyOptIns`, the history of `/settings`; `history.md`), like new trains; it is no column of `User`. So is whether they are ranked on the server's boards (the boards of `/settings`, plan 8H-6: `LeaderboardOptIn`, `isOnBoards`, `joinBoards`, `leaveBoards` of `ctx.historyOptIns`; `history.md`, "The boards"): off by default, switching it on turns the history opt-in on with it where it was off (and says so), and the history's opt-out takes the member off the boards too. The panel of `/settings` and `/admin member-settings` show both; a DM's panel says they are set per server.
- **`/settings`** (plan 8S-5) is one command without options (`SimpleCommand`, `defer: 'late'`, `MEMBER_READ_THROTTLE`; offered in servers and in a DM with the bot): a private components-v2 panel (`src/commands/settings/panel.ts`, one container of `LIST_COLOUR`, 39 components at most of Discord's 40 since plan 8H-10's section of the old bot's history: the next control the panel gains needs a rework of its layout, a second panel or lines that share a component) laid out as the lines of `settingsLines` (`settings-lines.ts`, one function for the panel and `/admin member-settings`, its words neutral for both: "Pings in posts: **on**", "History in this server: **off**", "Name on reports and trains: **Visible**"), each with its control: the four pings share one row of buttons that carry their name and state ("Shouts: on"), the boss DM, new trains, the history and the boards a button beside each (`Turn on` / `Turn off`, the history's `Turn off…`; green, Success, means only "on now": a ping that is on, while `Turn on` is blue, Primary, and what is off or turns off gray, Secondary), the status and the privacy a string select each with the stored option `default`, the Pokebattler id "Change…" (a dialog with the saved id in its line, `openToken`; its Submit checked by the handler's guard, before it counts or is acknowledged, A1: 1 to 2,147,483,647, empty clears it, a Submit without the line stale, A2; opening the dialog counts as a use of `/settings`, in the command's `MEMBER_READ_THROTTLE` window, never in the writes'), the friend code and in-game name read-only with `/friend-code register` and `/nickname register`, and in a server where notifications are on the counts of `/wants`'s lines and of the gyms, each with Show. The old bot's history (plan 8H-10, `history.md`): with the history on, `ctx.legacyIndex` there (`LEGACY_INDEX_DIR` set) and a file of the member in the server's member index (`hasFile`, which reads no file but asks whether the bot may read it), a section of its own, "Your raids, trains and meetups with the old bot in this server can be added to your history." with "Add my old-bot history" (blue; `settings:legacy:add`, a write of the panel's window); once asked, its line in the member's words, one name throughout ("your old-bot history", B7): "Your old-bot history is being added.", "Your old-bot history is added: 1,234 raids, trains and meetups." ("There was none of your old-bot raids, trains or meetups left to add to your history." for none), and a failed one "Your old-bot history could not be added. Ask staff, then press **Try again**." in a section with "Try again" (the same control, A1: it queues the request again; still 39 components), where the index is there with a file of theirs, else "Your old-bot history could not be added: ask staff." alone, a text display; nothing where there is nothing to say, and nothing of a waiting request where the installation has no index any more (B5). These rules are `legacyLine`'s (`panel.ts`), which staff's lines use too. `settingsLines` words it neutrally for staff ("Old-bot history: **not added**", "**being added**", "**added** (1,234 raids, trains and meetups)", "**could not be added** (why)", the worker's words for staff, B8); the panel draws the member's own words from `legacyWords` beside it. A press queues the fill-in (`ctx.legacyFillIns.request`) where the file is there and nothing waits or is done, starts the worker and says "Asked: it takes a few minutes."; where the request waits or is done it says so and asks nothing; with the history off meanwhile, "Your history is off: turn it on first.". Turning the history on, also through the boards (B12), adds " Your old-bot history can be added too: press **Add my old-bot history**." where it can be. The panel reads the member's row first, then the rest at once, the fill-in's row and the file's existence among them (the row's read remembers its id for the list reads, `round-trips.db.test.ts`: 8 reads, 10 for a press). In a DM (`!interaction.inGuild()`) the per-server lines are one: "New trains, your history, the boards, wants and gyms are set per server: use `/settings` in that server." Its old subcommands are gone; a client that still sends one (`formerSubcommands`) opens the panel, its options unread.
- The panel's controls (`settingsControls`, kind `settings`, `custom-id.ts`): every control **sets** a state, never toggles what it reads (`settings:<setting>:set:on|off`, the menus `settings:auto-status:set` and `settings:privacy:set` with the value picked); the member and the server are the interaction's. A press reads what it changes (the member's row, or the write's own answer: `newTrains.set`, `optIn`, `joinBoards`, `leaveBoards`, `optOut` say whether anything changed), writes, and draws the panel again from a fresh read (`panelNow`) with a line of what changed under its title ("Pings for shouts are off.", "That was on already." where it was so: a panel drawn before a change elsewhere), shown in place (`pressAnswer`, `readOrDeferPress`); the slow writes (`joinBoards`, `optOut`) acknowledge with `deferUpdate` first. Show answers apart (`answerApart`) with exactly what `/wants` / `/targets` answer (`wantsAnswer`, `targetsAnswer`), a late read acknowledged with a private `deferReply`, never `deferUpdate`; the panel's counts may go stale after a Remove there (accepted). The server's settings and Show are refused outside a server, Show where notifications are off (`requirePanelServer`, a guard: nothing counts). Turning the history off asks first where it takes something away (`namedIn` > 0 or on the boards, read in time, `SLOW_READ` past it): the dialog `settings:history:forget` (one of the one-shot dialogs of `discord-framework.md`), "Turn your history off", "I will take your Discord id off N entries of your history (the server still counts them) and take you off the boards. Submit turns your history off."; its Submit `deferUpdate`s, opts out and says what the opt-out took away by its own count; with nothing to take away it is off at once. One window for every control but Change… (`MEMBER_WRITE_THROTTLE`), the Submit five in ten minutes in its own (`HISTORY_OFF_THROTTLE`), whose button is refused while that is full (`checks`). Two quick presses may redraw out of order (accepted). Accepted too (review A3, A4): "Turn off…" acts at once where there is nothing to take away (S2), so its `…` promises a question that does not always come, and it is refused while the Submit's window is full even then; a menu pick that is refused (a value it does not know, the window full) leaves the client showing the option picked until the panel is drawn again (S17, S18).
- A repository method that changes one member's rows of a server runs inside `withMemberLocked` (`src/db/repositories/member-rows.ts`): the member's `User` row is locked first, by snowflake, and the rows are read after the lock. The row ids of servers and members are remembered per process in `RowIds` (`row-ids.ts`; one made in `src/app.ts` and given to the repositories of guilds, users, wants and gym targets; `RowIds.none` remembers nothing): a row made inside a transaction is remembered only after its commit, never inside a caller's transaction, and a remembered row that is gone is forgotten and the change run once more (the legacy bot deletes `Guild` rows). `src/commands/round-trips.db.test.ts` pins how many round trips the common commands make.

## Looking members up

- One member of a server is looked up with `ctx.members.find(guildId, userId)` (`MemberDirectory`, `src/discord/member-directory.ts`): a fresh question to Discord per member, the answer (also "not a member") kept for a minute; an id that is no snowflake is never asked about. `fetchGuildMember` is the one place a member is fetched (`DiscordPartyGateway.memberAccess` uses it; so do the web pages' `DiscordRegionStaff` and `DiscordWebMembers`, fresh). A member's name and team are read by `memberInfo(interaction)` from an interaction and by `guildMemberInfo(member)` from a fetched member (a press on the live map), by the same rule. Tests use `FakeMemberDirectory` (`test/support/members.ts`). There is no member list and there will be none: production has no privileged intents.
- Who of many members is in a server and can see a channel is `ctx.members.viewers(guildId, channelId, userIds)`: batches of 99 (and the bot itself, whose absence from an answer means the answer is no answer) through the gateway's request for members by user id, one request a second and thirty a minute at most (`MEMBER_REQUEST_BUDGET`), single REST look-ups when it fails and a rest that doubles up to six hours (`GatewayWithFallback`); "sees" is kept ten minutes, "does not" one (`src/discord/member-viewers.ts`). `FakeMemberDirectory` has `viewers`, `holding`, and members who are not there or cannot see.
- The names of many members (the boards', plan 8H-6) are `ctx.members.named(guildId, userIds)`: every id in batches of 99 by user id over the gateway (allowed without the GuildMembers intent; Discord's docs name the intent only for the whole list), never from discord.js's cache, which without that intent never hears that a member left (review A2); only while the gateway rests after a failure are the cache's members taken, the rest asked over REST one by one. Its requests and those of `viewers` go through one line (`BudgetLine.run`, A7, R-A4): one request for members at a time, the next after the last has settled and on one `MEMBER_REQUEST_BUDGET`; the notifier's (`viewers`) go ahead of names waiting (each turn chosen after the budget's turn is taken, a turn of the event loop later, so that the next batch of a lookup chained on the one just settled is in line first, F2), and names may start `NAMES_PER_MINUTE` (10) times a minute by the directory's clock (F4), a share of the line's thirty, all the batches of an ask reserved at once or none (`reserveNames`, F1), beyond which `named` rejects at once, spending nothing (`MembersBusy`; W1: a busy history page never holds back the DMs of a raid). The web then answers 503, `/stats boards` its words for Discord being busy. A server the cache lacks rejects (A8): nothing is kept of it. Somebody not in the server is not among them. `ctx.memberNames` (`MemberNames`, `src/services/member-names.ts`) keeps each answer a minute (`MEMBER_NAMES_FRESH_MS`, "not in the server" too; a failure is not kept), lets a second caller wait for a name already on its way rather than ask again (B4), and gives only members with a name, so that a board leaves out the rest and its ranks close up (never a mention in their place). `FakeMemberDirectory.named` answers from its members and records each ask in `namedAsks`.

## Self-assigned roles

- Self-assignable roles are `ctx.selfRoles` (`SelfRoleService`, `src/services/self-role-service.ts`): `roster(guildId)` is the server's rows (the legacy tables `Role` and `Alias`, with the Discord role's id in `Role.roleSnowflake` and the groups of `RoleGroup`) resolved against the roles the server has now, each entry with its `problem` if it cannot be given; `change(guildId, member, request)` plans with the pure `planChange` (`src/domain/roles/self-roles.ts`) and carries the plan out one member at a time, give first, then take, re-reading the roster before every further step, and answers (`RoleChangeOutcome`) `unavailable` (the bot does not know the server's roles), `refused` (`busy`: three changes of that member already wait; `expired`: the interaction can no longer be answered), `done` (the plan carried out; it may have been empty, and a role it skipped is in `plan.skipped`) or `partial` (what was given, taken, failed and not tried). A member still in the server's screening is refused (`requireJoined`). A handler tells the member exactly that (`src/commands/roles/words.ts`: `gaveWords` and `tookWords` say what a change of one role did, for `/iam`, `/iamnot` and the web map). A change that took a role away is passed on to `onRolesTaken`'s listeners whoever asked for it (plan 8M-20, W2: `app.ts` has `AccessService.memberChanged` settle the forums of the member's parties). `change` has a second surface since plan 8M-20: the web map's "Your regions…" (`POST /s/<guild>/api/regions/<region>/join|leave`, `web.md`, "Your regions on the map"), whose `RoleMember` is the member fetched afresh (`held` their roles, `seenAt` when Discord answered), counted in the window of the role menus; which roles open a region is `regionChoices` (`src/domain/roles/region-roles.ts`) over the gateway's `viewsWith`. `addRole`, `removeRole`, `saveGroup`, `removeGroup` are staff's.
- The bot never writes a member's role list. `RoleGateway` (`src/discord/role-gateway.ts`) has `server(guildId)` (the roles from the cache, the bot's standing) and `giveRole` / `takeRole`, one role per request: a list built from what an interaction showed would undo what anybody else changed meanwhile. Only the id of a current roster entry without a problem reaches `giveRole`. Tests use `FakeRoleGateway` and `selfRolesWith()` (`test/support/roles.ts`), `MemorySelfRoles` (`test/support/self-roles.ts`); the fake interactions take `roleIds` and the member's server `permissions`.
- What may be self-assigned is `roleProblem` (never `@everyone`, a managed role, a role at or above the bot's highest, or one that adds a permission of `MODERATION_PERMISSIONS` to what `@everyone` has, by itself or through a channel overwrite, or one whose name makes its holder staff of this bot: `addedModeration`, `channelModeration`, `src/discord/role-permissions.ts`; `isStaffRoleName`, `guards.ts`), checked when staff add a role and every time one is given; staff may offer only what they could give by hand (`offerProblem`: the owner, or Manage Roles on the server, read from `interaction.member.permissions`, never the channel's `memberPermissions`, and a role below their own highest). A new permission flag of discord.js must be sorted into moderation or harmless (a test fails otherwise).
- A legacy `Role` row is linked to its Discord role the first time it is resolved (exactly one unclaimed role of that name, without case), and its `roleName` follows a renamed role so the legacy bot keeps finding it (`buildRoster` says what to write, `SelfRoleRepository.remember` writes it, also during autocomplete). A group is pick-one or not; roles without a group are "Other".
- `/iam` without a role shows menus (`roleMenus`, `menuPages`, `menuRequest` in `src/domain/roles/menus.ts`; `renderRoleMenus`; the handler `roleControls` in `src/commands/roles/menus.ts`): one per group, a group over 25 split into ranges, five on a message or four with Previous / Next; custom ids `roles:<group row id|other>:pick:<part>:<page>` and `roles:page:prev|next:<page>`. The message is one components-v2 container of `LIST_COLOUR` (plan 8B-4): `### Your roles on this server` and a line of help, each menu under `**<group>** · pick one|pick any number`, "Page 2 of 3" with Previous / Next, and what the last change did in small print at the end. A menu gives only what it showed unticked and is now ticked, and takes away only what it showed ticked and is now unticked, read from the pressed message (inside its container, or in the rows of a message from before 8B-4); a pick-one menu asks for a take only with a "None" that was not ticked already. After every press the message is made anew from the roster and the roles the member holds now (`menusNow`), never copied, and shown through `showInPlace` (`OLD_MENUS` on a plain-text message of menus, the new ones privately below it); without the roster the answer is the sentence and "Use `/iam` again for the menus.". Nothing about an open menu is kept in memory.
