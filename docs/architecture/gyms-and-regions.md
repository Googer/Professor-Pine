# Gyms and regions

Gyms held in memory and searched, which server a gym belongs to, gym administration (`/gym` with its views, questions and announcements), what Google knows of a place, maps and the image cache, regions and their administration with KML, `/bounds` and `/find-region`. The Wayfarer import has its own file (`wayfarer.md`). Read before changing gyms, regions, maps or their commands.

## Finding gyms and regions

- **Gyms belong to servers** by the link table `GymServer` (migration 056, plan 19B-2; a server is its `Guild` row, as `Region.guildId` says): a server's staff, members and web pages see, search, pick and change exactly the gyms linked to it, wherever they lie, and a gym of another server, wherever it lies, is as if it did not exist (never offered, accepted or named; a refusal says what the server-scoped words say, "This server has no gym #N."). The upgrade linked every gym once, when it made the table (`upgradeLinks`, `src/domain/gyms/gym-server.ts`, by the rule the directory uses for "a gym lies in a region"): to every server whose regions hold it, else to the servers whose party history used it, else to every server; Pittsburgh's one server has all of its gyms. A gym several servers share (from the upgrade) is one row with its curated text: a change by either is seen by both.
- Gyms are found through `ctx.gyms` (`GymProvider`; every gym and link held in memory by `GymDirectory`): a server's view, `of(guildRow)` (`GymView` of `ServerGyms`, `src/services/server-gyms.ts`: `search(channelId, text, { limit })`, `get`, `all`, `isRegion`, `regionsOf`, `inRegion`, over the server's gyms placed in the server's regions only, searched without the server's `blacklistWords`; `serverGyms(interaction, ctx)` where an interaction came from, `src/commands/util/server-gym.ts`), a stored reference by its id, `stored(id)` (a party's gym, a train's stop, a history row, a target, a ruling, a saved route's stop: the server's when it was stored), and a gym's servers, `serversOf(id)`. A gym id that comes from a member (an option, a typed `#n` or `g:n`, a custom id's argument, a web route or body) is read only through the view. A check made under a party's lock reads the party's server's view (`RaidChangeService.setGym`, `TrainService`'s stops gone, `removeUnknownStops`), so that a shared gym the server deleted is gone for its parties though another server keeps it; a stored reference is read with `stored` only to show it. The web map's heatmap and the history's trains place a stored gym through the server's view too (a gym the server deleted leaves its heatmap and its trains' map, as a deleted gym always did: the controller's ruling). Every server's gyms as one list (`GymDirectory.all`, `size`) are read only where the instance's own data needs them, or where they are narrowed to the server's at once: the instance's upkeep (`GymUpkeep`: the Google line, each gym's stored nearest gym, of the gyms that share a server with it (plan 19B-3: another server's row of its place never makes its circle of places tiny; `nearestGymChanges(…, together)`), and the places' circles; the post pictures by address), `GymAdminService.near` (the few gyms within ten meters of a point, then the server's of them), `addBlock` (each new gym's stored nearest gym, of the gyms that share a server with it; what it may stand beside, the server's view) and the Wayfarer import's `gymWayfarerIds` (the Wayspots that are gyms, which plan 19B-3 makes a server's). A view is built when its server first asks, from that server's gyms alone (a set of gym ids per server: its cost is the server's gyms, not the instance's), kept current as gyms change, link or unlink (`refreshGym`, `forget`, `unlink`), made anew with the regions (a load) and when the server's words change (`wordsOf`, read at every ask); at most `MAX_SERVER_VIEWS` (200) are kept, the one asked for longest ago going first. So a view is asked for again after an await, never kept (`GymView`): one kept may have been dropped or made anew. A server without a row has no gyms. A hit says where it lies (`place`: `local`, `neighbouring`, `anywhere`), whether every word matched (`allWords`; false means a guess), whether a word had to be read as a misspelling (`corrected`) and how far the reading is (`typos`, `typoCost` of `src/domain/search/text.ts`: per typed word nothing as typed, a quarter for a word it only begins, else the fewest wrong letters, read by the words found in the gym's name, nickname or keywords, a word found only in its places, description or address costing 3: `namedReading`; a stop's or Power Spot's by its title, its street counting so): corrected matches and guesses come by how many typed words they lack (`missed` of `reading`: a guess that found more of the words first, whatever its misspellings), then by it, then by score (plan 8W-3: the score weighs a rare word high, so "libary" read as "Vicary" with two letters wrong came before the libraries with one). Act only on a clear hit; offer the rest as a choice. After a gym edit call `GymDirectory.refreshGym(id)`; after regions change, `load()`.
- `ctx.regions` (`RegionDirectory`) has the regions by id, channel and forum; `GymDirectory` holds its own copy, so after a region changes load both. Every change to what either directory holds bumps its `generation` (`GymDirectory`: every load, gym re-read or forgotten; `RegionDirectory`: every load): the web map's cache (`GeoCache`, `web.md`) rebuilds a server's view only when a generation differs, so a new way of changing either directory's state must bump it too, or the map keeps showing the old state. `regionChannelOf` (`src/discord/guards.ts`) reads the regions (a region with an outline, as `GymLookup.isRegion`), not the gyms, and also answers for a region's forum and a post in it. `ctx.users.get(snowflake)` gives a member's settings.
- Gyms, PokéStops and Power Spots together are found through `ctx.placeFinder` (`PlaceFinder`, `src/services/place-finder.ts`; plan 8W-2, Matt's rules of 2026-10-15): the server's gyms through the gym search (`GymLookupOptions.within`, the gyms of its regions: a gym elsewhere with every word leaves the server's guesses alone; `PlaceFinder` takes what it needs of `GymDirectory` by `Pick`, and `GymProvider` has no `all()`), the stops (the kept Wayspots and, plan 8R-1, the stops of Pine's own that staff add with `/pokestop`, `stop-reports.md`) and Power Spots each through a `WayspotIndex` of their own (`src/domain/search/wayspot-index.ts`: title, and counting little the street and town and (plan 8W-6) the places Google gave around it, `places` at 0.5 as a gym's, the point's answer through `WayspotPlaces.placesAt` (every server's copy of a place alike, plan 19B-3); every word as typed or as a beginning; one letter wrong only when nothing matched as typed, or when a word as typed looks misspelt (a word one letter off is in at least `MISSPELT_TITLES` 3 titles and three times as many as it: "Little Free Libary" beside the libraries, plan 8W-3), whose corrections then follow its hits as typed; guesses by some words only when nothing else matched; common words weigh little by MiniSearch's BM25, the legacy stop words not at all), never one mixed index; merged by `mergePlaces` (`place-merge.ts`: `placeFit` exact, word, beginning, loose, guess, the guesses by `guessRank` across kinds (fewer typed words missed first, then the fewest letters read wrong); then the member's region; then gym, stop, Power Spot; then fewer title words beyond those typed, `extraWords`, counted in the name the fit came from (`placeFitBy`: a gym's name or nickname), tuned on Pittsburgh's export; then the nearer to the places named before, `PlaceHit.distance`, where `find` was given `anchors` (its trailing `{ kindsFirst?, anchors? }`; Matt, 2026-10-27, "nearby stops first": `/research`'s other stops, a train's last stop; without anchors the order is as before); then score). The distances are one pure path, `src/domain/search/nearest.ts`: `anchorMetres` (to the nearest of several anchors, by `metresBetween`), `nearerFirst` (the comparison `mergePlaces` uses) and `nearestPlaces` (nearest first, as near by name, an optional radius and count), which `PlaceFinder.nearest(guildId, kinds, anchors, { within, count, except })` uses for the list of an empty option (the server's places of those kinds called something, those whose `placeValue` is in `except` left out, each told where it is; measured as kept, gyms and Wayspots, and only the places listed made `PlaceChoice`s: a city's thousands would hold up the bot) and the live map's `nearestLines` too. Such a list labels a place with its distance (`placeLabel(choice, distanceWords(…))`: ` · 350 m` at the end where the whole label fits uncut, else the label without it); `nearChoices`' `before` (`NamedBefore`: the other options' values, of which only a choice of the list read through `chosen` and of the option's kinds counts, left out of the list; `points` to measure from as well, whose places stay in it: `/rocket report`'s last report, a function read only where the list can use it (review P2: not for a maps link or coordinates, nor where the server is not known); and the radius) gives a `near` option both. Only what lies in a region of the server is found (`find`, `chosen(value)`); a choice says where it is (`where`: its street, or "near <gym>", the nearest of the server's gyms within `NEAR_GYM_METRES` 1 km, never the place itself; with none so near, nothing); a value is `<kind>:<id>` (`placeValue`, `readPlaceValue`); typed text is acted on only through `clearOf(found)`, of the list one `find` gave (`clearPlace`: the one hit, of every kind and region, whose every word matched exactly, `exact` or `word`; one that names it outright beside another with every word is no clear hit). Each server's indexes are its own (plan 19B-3), built on its first search after its Wayspots changed (`ctx.wayspots.contentGenerationOf(guildRow)`: a load of its rows or a change of its stops of Pine's own; not a change of the regions, nor a forget: a forgotten Wayspot is passed over at once because the search takes only what `inRegion` has) or after `WayspotPlaces` told it of new places (`generationOf(guildRow)`: a server's first answer at once, then at most once an hour while the line's answers arrive, `PLACES_TOLD_EVERY_MS`), in a turn of their own (`setImmediate`, about half a second for a city; one server per turn), never inside a search: a search before its server's first build finds the gyms alone, one after a change what was built until the new build is done. One server's change builds no other server's index; at most `MAX_SERVER_VIEWS` servers' indexes are kept (a `BoundedMap`, the one searched longest ago going first). `built(guildRow?)` has a server's (every server's, without one) built and waits for it (tests; the bot never calls it).
- The live map's Find (plan 8M-12, `web.md`, "Find on the map") asks `PlaceFinder.find` for its places by name (`GET /s/<guild>/api/find-places`): every kind, the member's point as the anchor, at most ten of each kind in the merged order, a guess that misses a word typed left out; the gyms too, so that the map and Discord rank a gym, a stop and a Power Spot by one search. A long text goes by its first 16 words (`firstWords`, `src/domain/search/text.ts`), in the gym, Wayspot and Pokémon searches and `PlaceFinder.find`: what an option's list is asked has no throttle, and a search holds the gateway's thread at a cost of every typed word for every hit.
- Whose a gym is, is its links (`GymServer`); where in a server it lies, the server's view (`regionsOf`, the server's regions only), never the channel cache: `gymRegionsInGuild` / `gymInGuild` (`src/services/region-directory.ts`) give the regions of a server that a gym of that server lies in, by the regions' rows. A region without a `guildId` belongs to no server. What the instance does for a gym whoever's it is (the Google line's rank) goes by each of its own servers' regions (plan 19B-3: a row of the line per server, `GoogleRanks.ofGym(guildRow, gymId)`), never another server's region that happens to hold it; it asks `regionsIn(guildRow, gymId)` of `GymDirectory` (`ServerGyms`: the same rule, `gymsByRegion`, for that one gym against that server's regions), which builds no view, so that ranking every gym of every server costs no index. `/bounds`, `/region list` (its gyms per region, and "N gyms of this server lie in none of its regions."), a region import's counts and a removal's stranded gyms count the server's gyms only. A raid report always names the server it was made in (`RaidReport.guildRow`, required; `PartyService.regionFor(gym, guildRow, regionChannelId?)`, never the gym's first server): the raid goes to a region of that server the gym lies in, the region it was reported from first when that channel is a region of this server (a region row of another server, or of none, is no region there: from it the server's gyms are searched as from any other channel), and it is stored only while that server has the gym.

## Maps

- Map pictures come from `ctx.maps` (`gym(gym)`, `region(region)`): a file path under `var/images/`, or undefined when there is no API key or the download failed; a message must go out without the map then. A message shows a map at full width as a gallery (plan 8B-4): `mapPicture(await mapOrNothing(ctx.maps.gym(gym), ctx.log, 'a gym', { gym: gym.id }), alt)` (`src/render/v2.ts`, `src/services/static-maps.ts`; `mapOrNothing` logs a failure and never throws) gives the `gallery` to put where the map stands (`mediaGallery`, `attachment://<file name>`) and the `files` to send with it (`V2Message.files`, local paths, which `discordPayload` and `v2Answer` send). `ImageCache` names a file by what it shows, so a moved gym gets a new picture by itself. It downloads a picture only from the address it was asked at (`redirect: 'error'`; a redirect is logged as `the server answered with a redirect, which is not followed…` with the picture's folder and id; the brief of plan 8Z-2 named `POKEMON_IMAGE_BASE` there, which nothing reads: the cache's addresses are Google's maps, Discord's emoji and Wayfarer's pictures, none of them a setting), reads the body piece by piece and gives it up the moment it passes its limit (`readLimitedBytes`, 5 MB), and lets go of the body of an answer it does not take. A map of a place (`ctx.maps.place`, `/find-region`: a member chooses the place, and Google bills every map downloaded) takes one of the month's maps before its download (`ImageRequest.spend`, `ctx.mapBudget`, `MapBudget` of `src/services/map-budget.ts`; none for a picture on disk): counted in `GoogleUseDay` as the kind `map` beside `places`, `geocode` and `hand` (no migration: the column takes it), capped a Pacific month at `settings.googleByHandPerMonth` in a count of its own, so that members' maps and staff's lookups by hand do not use each other's up; past it, or while the count cannot be read or written, no map is downloaded and the answer goes without one (warned once a month), and `/gym google` says "N of M maps of `/find-region` are used this month." Maps have that count of their own and do not count against the gym line's Google budget (the places, geocodes and lookups by hand of the line's month), unlike every other call to Google, on purpose: a member's typing must not hold back the gyms' places. A member has at most `MAX_MAPS_PER_MEMBER_DAY` (20) newly downloaded maps a Pacific day (in memory, a `BoundedMap` by member, the day by the injected clock; a picture on disk costs nothing), and past it that member's answers go without a map as past the monthly cap (logged once a day per member; fix round of 8Z-2: with `/find-region`'s five uses a minute one member used the month's maps for everyone in under two hours). The Google API key is only ever in the address of a download: never in a log line, a file name or anything sent to Discord.

## Gym administration

- Gyms are administered through `ctx.gymAdmin` (`GymAdminService`, `src/services/gym-admin-service.ts`): `readLocation`, `add`, `edit`, `setElite`, `move`, `references`, `delete`, `refresh`. Adding, moving and deleting run one at a time under one lock, and what a question promised (no other gym within 10 metres, the gym still where it was) is looked at again inside that lock: `add` and `move` answer `near` or `elsewhere` and the command asks anew. A gym is made only with its server (`GymStorage.create(gym, guildRow)`, `createBlock(gyms, guildRow, nearest)`, the link in the gym's transaction): `/gym add`, an import's new gym and `gyms:seed --server` make a new row linked to the one server, also where another server has a gym of the same Wayfarer id or at the same place. Every change is logged with who, which gym and what, never the texts. After a change the service refreshes `GymDirectory` and has the posts that show the gym rendered anew; commands never call `GymRepository` or `refreshGym` themselves.
- The deletion of a server Pine left 30 days ago (plan 19C-3, `servers.md`) takes its gyms by the same rule, link by link: `GymRemovalRepository.remove(gymId, { guildId, regionIds: [] })` (its routes and targets went with its regions and members' rows before), a gym others keep unlinked, one no other server has deleted with its curated text; then `GymDirectory.forget` / `unlink` per gym, and `GymUpkeep.serverGone` once for all of them: what `removed` / `unlinked` do (below), the nearest gyms worked out in one pass over every gym (`nearestAfterGone`), whatever the server's count.
- A deletion is refused while an active raid is at the gym. Otherwise: one transaction (`GymRemovalRepository.remove(gymId, { guildId, regionIds })`, the gym's links locked first, so that two servers deleting one gym at once decide in turn which is the last; a deadlock runs it again, `retryOnDeadlock`, which reads the links anew) for the server's link, the server's saved routes (of its regions) and gym targets of it, and, when no other server is left, the row with its `GymMeta` and whatever still names it, every server's targets and routes of it; the answer counts the deleting server's alone (its targets, the routes of its regions), as its question said them. Which server's is always named: the targets' `membersTargeting` and `removeGym` take a `guildRow | 'every'`, the routes' `countWithGym` and `removeGym` a `regionIds | 'every'` (and find the routes through the index of a route's gyms, `SavedRouteGym`, `trains.md`), never every server by default (the twin `MemoryGymTargets` refuses a row it does not know). Then `GymDirectory.forget(id)` (the row went) or `unlink(id, guildRow)` (other servers keep it: it is gone for this server alone; the nearest gyms of the gyms that pointed at it and its own are settled anew among the servers that keep them, `GymUpkeep.unlinked`, plan 19B-3), then the gym is taken off the route of every active train of that server (best effort; a train that could not be changed is named in the answer), then the announcement. Nothing visible happens before the gym is really gone. From `forget` on, whatever picks the gym late (a report, `/location`, a relocation, a train's `addStop`, a loaded route) is refused with the reason `gym-gone`; `PartyStore.addRaidUnlessTaken` asks `gymExists` under the gym's lock. The hourly upkeep takes stops their train's server does not know off active trains (`TrainService.removeUnknownStops`, each train by its server's view; never against an empty directory). Lock order: the admin lock, then a gym's lock, then a party's.
- A server's staff administer exactly their server's gyms, wherever they lie (plan 19B-2): `mayTouch(gymId, guildRow)` of the service is the server's view (`of(guildRow).get`), at the command and again under the lock; a gym of another server is neither offered nor accepted, as if it did not exist. A gym may be placed anywhere (regions of different servers overlap freely); one in none of the server's regions stays the server's, but raids cannot be reported there ("It now lies in no region of this server: raids cannot be reported there."). The gyms within ten meters that `add` and `move` ask about (`GymAdminService.near(point, guildId, exceptId?)`) and the nearest gym of the view (`nearestFor(gym, guildId)`: the stored `nearestGym` when it is the server's, else the nearest of the server's view, `of(guildRow).all()`) count only the server's gyms, so another server's gym is never named; a gym at another server's gym is a second row. A gym's stored `nearestGym`, and with it the circle its places are asked in, is of the gyms that share a server with it (plan 19B-3: another server's row of its place never makes the circle tiny; `nearestGymChanges(…, together)`, which looks again at a gym without a nearest only when the gym placed may be its nearest), and a PokéStop's or Power Spot's circle of its own server's gyms and Wayspots.
- A location staff give is read by `readLocation` (`src/domain/geo/location-input.ts`, pure): coordinates, or a maps link. The link Pokémon GO shares for a gym ("View in map") is the main way and reads as an exact point; a Google Maps pin wins over where the map is centred; a route is read by where it ends; a place read only from a map's centre is `approximate`, and the question says so. A short link is followed by `GeoGateway.followShortLink`: `https`, the Maps short-link hosts and Google's own only, five hops, five seconds, no body read. A new shape of link gets a test before it is read.
- Google's answers are the place's, kept by the point and the kind of question, never by a gym's or a Wayspot's id (plan 19B-3): `GoogleAnswers` (`src/services/google-answers.ts`; `GoogleAnswerRepository`, table `GoogleAnswer` of migration 059, primary key `(kind, lat, lon)`; twin `MemoryGoogleAnswerStorage`; both run `googleAnswerCases`). A point is rounded to a millionth of a degree (`answerPoint`, `src/domain/geo/answer-point.ts`, about 0.1 m: two servers' copies of one place, and a gym and the Wayspot it is made of, are one key); `kind` is `address` (the geocode's parts as a gym's `geodata`) or `places` (the names, `placesText`); `answer` null where Google found nothing (the place was asked); `askedAt`. It holds Google's answers alone: Wayfarer's address a Wayfarer import or review gives a gym is the gym's own (`GymMeta.wayfarerAddress`, `Gym.wayfarerAddress`, written with the gym's row), shown before its point's answer, so that another server's gym at the place keeps Google's and Google's answer is never replaced by it. Every answer is read into memory at start, before the gyms and Wayspots (every reader stays synchronous), and written through; the table is bounded by the places of the instance's gyms and Wayspots: once a day, with the scheduler's upkeep (the first at start, after both are loaded), `sweep([gyms, wayspots])` drops every answer that no gym, Wayspot or stop of Pine's own of any server lies within `WAYSPOT_MOVED_METRES` of (`KeptPlaces.anyNear` of each `PlaceKeeper`'s `keptPlaces()`, asked by a sweep that sweeps and let go when it ends: `GymDirectory` itself, by the gyms' cells, `ServerGyms.near`; `WayspotDirectory.keptPlaces()` a cell index of every server's Wayspots and stops of Pine's own built for that sweep alone, never kept between sweeps), looking at `SWEEP_ANSWERS_A_TURN` (2,000) answers a turn with a `setImmediate` between, deleted by the primary key with its `askedAt` (`GoogleAnswerStorage.remove`, an `or` of whole keys, explain test: `PRIMARY`), so that one saved anew at its point meanwhile stays; one swept in the very moment a place came there costs one question again. Its methods: `at(kind, point)` gives the nearest answer within `WAYSPOT_MOVED_METRES` 10 (cells of a thousandth of a degree, `answerCell`), what is shown meanwhile; `ownAt(kind, point)` the answer of a place's own point (within `OWN_ANSWER_METRES` 1), which alone decides whether a gym or Wayspot was asked and waits in the line (two gyms 8 m apart are each asked); `carry(moves)` keeps what was answered about a place's old point itself (`ownAt`, never a neighbor's shown there) at its new one, unless the new one has its own, for a gym or Wayspot an import moved without a new question (a gym up to `REFETCH_METRES`, `GymUpkeep.placed(…, { places: false, from })`; a Wayspot up to `WAYSPOT_MOVED_METRES`, the directory's load of its server), so that it is asked about again neither then nor at a later start; one moved farther is asked again; `save(answers)` replaces the answer at its point for every server (`/gym refresh` replaces Google's answer about the place, not a server's text; curated text, a nickname or a notice, is never in it; a gym refreshed that showed its own Wayfarer address gives it up and shows Google's, only the address it showed when Google was asked (one an import wrote meanwhile stays), the refresh's `updated` comparing Google's with what was shown) and tells `whenSaved`. Whether a gym has an address is one rule, `GymUpkeep.hasOwnAddress` (its Wayfarer address, or its own point's answer, never a neighbor's): the line, a gym that gym administration adds (`GymAdminService.hasOwnAddress`) and the Wayfarer import's match decide by it. It is the one source of every list of places, and of every address but a gym's own Wayfarer address, that Pine shows or searches: `GymDirectory` puts them on its gyms (its option `answers`, required, as `GymUpkeep`'s: one instance for both; an answer saved shows at once on the gyms within ten meters of its point, found by the cells around it, `ServerGyms.near`, never by looking at every gym), `WayspotPlaces` reads the places around a PokéStop or Power Spot, `WayspotAddresses` a Power Spot's address. `GymRepository` neither reads nor writes `GymMeta.geodata` and `places` any more (its gyms come with null; 2.0's columns stay, as `GymGoogleAsked`, `WayspotPlaces` and `WayspotAddress` do, filled into `GoogleAnswer` by the upgrade: of two at one point, one that found something over a null, then for an address a gym's over a Power Spot's, then the newer; `GymMeta.wayfarerAddress` is left empty by it, since 2.0's `geodata` does not say whose an address was).
- What Google knows of a place is behind `GeoGateway` (`src/services/geo-gateway.ts`; `GoogleGeo` calls the Geocoding API and Places API (New) with `fetch`, the key in the address of the one and a header of the other): each call answers a value, `none`, `no-key`, `denied` (remembered for an hour) or `failed`, never throws and never logs an address; an answer is read piece by piece and given up the moment it is longer than `MAX_GEO_ANSWER_BYTES` (1 MB; `readLimitedJson`, plan 8Z-2: `failed`, logged as `the answer is longer than 1048576 bytes`; an address is some tens of kilobytes at its longest), and the body of a refusal or a redirect is let go of unread. In the upkeep's line of lookups (`GymUpkeep`) a refused geocode holds back only the gyms that want an address: the refused gym stays first and is asked no Places, the line goes on with the gyms that want their places alone (`GymLookups.next({ placesOnly })`), and it waits out the refusal only when every gym left wants an address. The address parts also hold the address as Google writes it (`FORMATTED_ADDRESS`), which the search does not read and `addressLine` shows (`/gym show`); a row without it gets a line made of its parts. `GymUpkeep` (`gym-upkeep.ts`) keeps a gym's address parts, nearby places and nearest gym: the nearest gyms of the gyms concerned only (`nearestGymChanges`), the places of gyms whose nearest gym changed in a slow queue, a generation per gym so that a stale answer is never written. Without a key a gym is saved all the same.
- The line that waits for Google is a priority queue with a monthly budget (plan 8W-6; design `2026-10-20-google-line-priority-design.md`). The arithmetic is pure (`src/domain/geo/google-line.ts`): the month as Google counts it (`googleMonth`, the 1st at midnight in `America/Los_Angeles`, `GOOGLE_ZONE`, whatever the servers' zones are), `lineAllowance` (gyms may use the whole month above the reserve; the line stops when only `settings.googleLookupsReserve` of `googleLookupsPerMonth` is left, until the month resets; addresses within `googleGeocodesPerMonth`), `regionRanks` and `bestRank` (a region's raids and max battles over `RANK_DAYS` 90 from the party history, `HistoryRepository.raidsByRegion`, divided by its server's busiest region's: each server's busiest is 1; a server without raids has every region at 1; a region of no server has no rank; a server's row of a gym in none of that server's regions (plans 19B-2, 19B-3: `GoogleRanks.ofGym(guildRow, gymId)` by `regionsIn`) is `NO_REGION_RANK`, last), `lineOrder`, and `estimateLine` (below, `/gym google`). `GoogleRanks` (`src/services/google-ranks.ts`) reads the raids at start and at the line's first turn of each Pacific day (`GymUpkeep.rerank`, which ranks the line anew), never per question, and of the servers the bot is in only (plan 8Z-2: `GuildRepository.snowflakes`, the servers with a `Guild` row, since the first reading comes before the login; the read then goes by server, `history.md`). ## Regions

- Regions are administered through `ctx.regionAdmin` (`RegionAdminService`, `src/services/region-admin-service.ts`): `preview`, `import`, `list`, `exported`, `remove`, under the `AdminLock` (`src/services/admin-lock.ts`) it shares with `GymAdminService`. An import creates channels and categories first (at most `MAX_NEW_CHANNELS` per import; each in the very category the plan named, by its id while it is there), writes every outline in one transaction (`RegionRepository.saveOutlines`), reloads both directories, takes back an orphan forum beside its channel or sets up a forum (`PartyService.setupForums(guildId, only)`), all inside the lock; what Discord still refuses for want of room (a race, the server's 500) is said plainly: `CreatedChannel`'s `category-full` (with the category's name, `NotImported.category`) or `server-full` (`channelLimitOf`, `src/discord/party-gateway.ts`: the server's code, or Invalid Form Body with `CHANNEL_PARENT_MAX_CHANNELS`, no other refusal of the parent), and a forum refused so says which category is full (`setupForums`' reason); nothing is written before staff press Import. A removal closes the region (`PartyStore.closeRegion`: an add for it throws `RegionClosedError`, which reports, meetups, trains and moves answer as no region), ends its parties, deletes the row (forum and summary ids from the locked row; `SavedRoutes` go by their foreign key), reloads, and keeps or deletes the forum. Regions are never deleted by themselves. Creating channels and deleting a forum need the staff member's own Manage Channels; audit-log reasons carry their id. `/region remove` asks in a dialog (plan 8Q, `removalDialog` in `src/commands/regions/remove.ts`; no `defer`): titled `Remove a region`, what goes with the region counted (`removalQuestion`, read within `DIALOG_READ_MS`), the text channel's line and the forum's, then `Submit removes it.`, channels named (`#west`, `regionInDialog` and `channelInDialog`), not mentioned. With a forum the staff member may delete, the required radio group `Its forum` (`FORUM_INPUT`) with nothing picked: Keep it as history (`Its posts are archived and locked.`) or Delete it (`Deletes every post in it for good.`); with one they may not delete (no Manage Channels, or rehearsal mode) no choice and the line that says it stays and why; without one, that line. Its custom id carries the region, `region:<region id>:remove` (`:keep` without the choice; then a per-open token `:~<token>`, left out at Submit): no pending request. Its Submit (`submitRemoval`, a `modal` of `regionControls`) is acknowledged privately, refuses deleting the forum in rehearsal mode and to staff without Manage Channels (as the press did, nothing removed), and answers `outcomeLines` in a message of its own; Discord's Cancel replaces Cancel, and the buttons of the question before plan 8Q find no request and say that it expired. `preview` takes the regions removed in the same save (`removing`, the region editor's): `planImport` counts their gyms among those left in no region.
