# Parties and their posts

A party is a raid, a max battle, a meetup or a train: an immutable object changed by pure functions, kept by `PartyStore`, shown as a components-v2 post in its region's forum through `PartyGateway` and `PartyService`, with attendance, raid changes, the clock, lines in the post, copies in other channels, access for members of other regions and moves between regions. Trains have their own file (`trains.md`). Read before changing anything about parties, posts, attendance, raid changes, meetups or the scheduler.

## The model and the store

- A party (a raid, a meetup, a train or a max battle) is an immutable plain object (`src/domain/party/party.ts`). Everything that can happen to it is a pure function in `src/domain/party/` (`attendance.ts`, `raid.ts`) that returns a changed party with what came of it, or `{ ok: false, reason }` (`Change`); a function returns the very same object when nothing changed. Commands turn a `reason` into a sentence. Never mutate a party.
- `Party` is a union, `RaidParty | MeetupParty | TrainParty | MaxParty`. Code that is about a raid narrows: `isRaid` in the domain and services, the guard `requireRaid` with `commandRaid(interaction, ctx)` in a handler, `raidOf` elsewhere; likewise `isTimed` / `requireTimed` / `commandTimed` and `isMeetup` / `requireMeetup` / `commandMeetup`, and `isMax` for a max battle. Every guard names its kinds outright (plan 8X-3: `isTimed` is a meetup or a train, `requireRaidOrTrain` a raid, max battle or train): a kind added later is none of them by elimination. A battle (`isBattle`, `BattleParty`: a raid or a max battle) is one where Done means the member completed it, and its present members are asked whether they did (`completeRaid`, `askGroupWhetherComplete`, `completeUnanswered`; the egg's refusal stays a raid's). A change inside `PartyStore.update` keeps the kind (`asKindOf(original, changed)`; the store throws when the kind changes). Never cast to a kind.
- What the clock does is `partyTick(party, input)` (`tick.ts`; it dispatches to `raidTick`, `maxTick` or `timedTick`; a raid and a max battle share `battleClock`: the meeting times, the questions, the archiving warned `deletionWarningTime` ahead): it returns the party as it should be now, `effects` (`stage`, `ask-complete`, `completed`, `warn`, `end`) and `render`. It is idempotent and catches up after downtime by itself. `end` comes alone and repeats until the caller has ended the party with `endParty`.
- A party changes only through `PartyStore.update(id, change)` (`src/services/party-store.ts`): one change at a time per party, written to the database first, then replaced in memory. `change` is synchronous and decides inside the callback (a `get` made earlier may be stale); the result has `written`. Two parties that must change together (a train's split: the old train loses the group, the new one is made to match) change through `PartyStore.updateBoth([id, id], change)`: `change(first, second)` gets both as they are (undefined for one that is not active: it decides) and returns `parties` in the same order, both written by one storage call in one transaction (`PartyStorage.saveBoth`; the notes as `save`'s: an end behind its savepoint inside the transaction, steps after the commit; a deadlock runs the whole transaction again, `retryOnDeadlock`, and one that persists or another loss of the transaction writes both rows again alone), memory replaced only after the commit, both before the listeners hear of either; a failed write leaves both as they were, a row that is gone writes neither. It takes both locks in the order of the ids, so a change of either waits for it and two such changes never deadlock; nothing inside it takes a lock again (`KeyedLock` is not re-entrant). A reported raid is stored only through `addRaidUnlessTaken`, a reported max battle only through `addMaxUnlessTaken` (below). Handlers read through `ctx.parties` (`get`, `byPost`, `active`, `inRegion`, `raidAt`, `maxAt`).
- Groups (plan 8X-1B; design `2026-10-20-join-group-meet-buttons-design.md`): a party keeps its groups in letter order, A to E (plan 8X-G, Matt 2026-10-22: before, in the order they were opened, so a letter given again came after later ones, C D E A; a new group goes in its place, `withGroupAdded` in `createGroup` and `chooseGroup`, and a stored party is read sorted, `inLetterOrder`, with no new state version), so the post, the dialogs, `/shout group:`'s choices and the map list them by letter; each remembers when it was opened (`PartyGroup.opened`; a party's first group and every group of a document before version 12 count as opened with the party: `groupOpened`). A group nobody is in goes (decision 6, a wish of legacy Pine's): `pruneGroups` (`attendance.ts`, pure) drops a group no attendee of any status is in (who is interested and who is done count), unless it has a label or a meeting time, never the party's last group (of all empty ones the default group stays); the others keep their letters, and when the default group goes the newest left takes over (by `groupOpened`, `newestFirst`; so does a train's split). `PartyStore.update` and `updateBoth` (both parties) run it on every change that returned a new party, never on a refusal or a change of nothing; it is silent (no line, no ping, the post shown anew). A new group takes `nextGroupId`'s letter (after the last in use, else the first free one), so a letter can be given again: whatever names a group across time names it by its letter and stamp (`groupStamp`, base 36; `stampedGroup` finds a group only while the letter is still that group): the custom ids of the dialogs about groups and Meet…'s, the train split's mark (`groupMark` is `<stamp>.<hash>`; a mark of a group that went, or whose letter a group opened since has, is `no-such-group`, worded as before; a label changed or another split since is `group-changed`), and the party history's meeting times (`history.md`). A group a change opens is stamped with `AttendanceLimits.now`, which the services give with every change (`limitsOf`; `applyAutoStatus` takes it too), and in any case after the party's last write and every group it has (`openedGroup`): stamps strictly increase, also within one millisecond. Who signs up without choosing a group is placed silently as before: +1, −1, Remote and the commands (`/join`, `/here` …, without `group`), and a report's automatic status, go where `setAttendance` puts a newcomer (`chooseGroup` with a lobby size, else the default group); only the post's Interested, Join and Here ask (below). A new group's sentence pings who allow it at most once per party in five minutes (`GROUP_PING_MS`, plan 8Z; `/group new` and Group… alike, in `announceGroup`, kept in memory by party, a restart forgetting it): the group still opens and the sentence is still said, without mentions; a sentence that could ping nobody takes no turn. The map has no way of its own to open a group: its Group… links the post.
- Every party that ends is recorded in the party history (`history.md`): the store asks `PartyHistory` for a note of each write, which the storage writes with the party, and a change that ends a party says why with `ended` in its result (`PartyEnd`: the scheduler `clock`, `PartyService.end` `cancelled` by default, a move `moved`, a party that never had its post `failed`). Once recorded, an ended party's `Party` row is deleted (it holds its members' ids; Matt, 2026-10-16): what reads ended parties (`endedPosts`, `partyForums`) also reads `EndedPartyPost`, and nothing may count on an ended row being there.
- `PartyStore.onChange(listener)` tells of every added party and every stored change, `(before, after)`, right after memory was replaced; a listener decides and returns (never `async`), and what takes time it does on the side. It is for what follows from a change whoever made it.
- A raid's boss is stored as a reference, `{ key }` (catalogue key) or `{ name }` (a boss the game master does not know): make it with `raidBossRef(boss)` from a `RaidBoss` of `ctx.raidBosses`, never by passing that object itself. An egg has no boss; `raid.reportedHatched` says whether a raid without times was seen hatched.
- `PartyRepository` keeps what is searched by in columns and the rest in one versioned JSON document (`party-state.ts`), checked when written and when read; a row it cannot read is reported and left alone. Attendees are a list there, because MySQL sorts the keys of a JSON object. Any new field in the document is a new version with a reader for the old one.
- Lobbies (plan 8X-1; design `2026-10-20-max-battles-design.md`, section 4): `settings.lobbies` (`{ raid, dynamax, gigantamax }`, each 1 to 100, defaults 20, 4, 100, `DEFAULT_LOBBIES`; `raid` is only the size above which a group of a raid or a train is told to split up: `splitLobby(party, lobbies)`, a second exhaustive switch beside `lobbySize` (raid and raid train `lobbies.raid`, meetup, Dynamax and Gigantamax train none: plan 8X-4), `TrainersView.splitLobby`, null where a kind never splits up) and `lobbySize(party, lobbies)` (`src/domain/party/lobby.ts`): how many trainers one group should hold: a max battle's by its tier (`dynamax`, `gigantamax`; plan 8X-3), a Gigantamax train's `gigantamax` (plan 8X-4, by `train.kind`, at once when the kind changes; groups already made stay), undefined for a raid, a raid train, a Dynamax train and a meetup (Q6); an exhaustive switch on the kind. A group's lobby is `lobbyCount(party, group)` (`party.ts`, beside `headCount`): the heads of its committed members (`isCommitted`: Coming, Present, CompletePending), remote included; not Interested, not Complete. Where a party has a size, Join fills its groups: the services put `lobbySize` into the `AttendanceLimits` of each change (`AttendanceService.limitsOf(party)`; `PartyService` passes it to `applyAutoStatus`; both take `lobbySize` as an option, which tests use to give a raid a size), and `setAttendance` places a member who is not yet an attendee and names no group, whatever the status, and an Interested one who becomes Coming or Present (their own group while it has room for them) through `chooseGroup(party, member, heads, size)` (`attendance.ts`): the default group while they and their +1s fit, else the first group by letter with room, else a new group while there are fewer than five, opened silently (no line, no ping, unlike `createGroup`) and made the default, else the group with the most room, the first by letter on a tie, over its size; the member is never counted against their own group. A member larger than a lobby by themself goes straight to the group with the most room (no new group would hold them either), and a party without groups (a meetup) keeps its one. `+1` and Remote sign a new member up the same way. Nothing else moves anybody: a count or a change to Coming that puts a group over its size leaves them there, and `/group join`, Group…, the group a newcomer chose in the dialog "which group?" (plan 8X-1B), the letter Join of a post drawn before it and anything that names a group put the member where they asked, over the size if so. Without a size every change is what it was.
- How long a raid lasts comes from `raidDurationsAt` / `hatchTimeForEnd` / `longestRaidDurations` (`src/domain/time/raid-durations.ts`) with `raidDurationRules(settings, ctx.raidEvents.events())`: the configured durations, changed for a standard raid that hatches during a Raid Hour or Raid Day (`settings.raidEvents`; events come from the feed and the Raid Hours of the events' Leek Duck pages, `utilities.md`, and the weekly Raid Hour of the settings applies in a week for which neither lists one). Pass `longestRaidDurations(...)` as `durations` to `resolvePartyTime`.

## Commands and controls in a post

- Commands that act on the party of the post they are used in have the guard `requireParty`; the handler asks `partyOf(interaction, ctx)` again and copes with undefined.
- A time a member types for an existing raid is read by `typedPartyTime(ctx, party, 'hatch' | 'end' | 'meet', text, now)` (`src/commands/raids/raid-input.ts`); handlers read `Date.now()` themselves (their tests fake only `Date`). A boss with only one fast or one charged move has it set wherever a raid gets its boss (`certainMoves`: report, `/boss`, hatch).
- The raid command for another gym is `/location`, not `/gym`: `/gym` is the gym administration of Phase 5, and one slash command cannot be both a command and a group of subcommands. A gym of another region of the same server moves the raid there: `/location` asks first (its own file, `src/commands/raids/location.ts`), in a dialog since plan 8Q: titled `Move the raid to another region`, today's question with the region's channel named (`#east`, `channelInDialog`; the answers keep their mentions) and `Submit moves it.`; refusals before asking (no such gym, several, a gym with a raid of any kind, none of a region with a forum) stay private replies, and a move within the region asks nothing. The dialog carries the party and the gym (no pending request); its Submit (`locationControls.modal`, `requireGuild`) is acknowledged privately, refused as over (`This raid is over.`) unless the raid it names is the one of the post it is submitted in (`raidOf`), and moves the raid as the service decides then (`raids.setGym`), the answer (`This raid is now at … Its new post is …`) given before the old post closes; a refusal that is none of the member's doing gives back the Submit and the use of `/location` that opened the dialog (`carriesOn`, plan 8S-4: when it was counted is the custom id's last part, `location:<party id>:move:<gym id>:<counted at>`), and a failure after the move was answered is only logged. Command and Submit have no limit of their own (the `CHANGE_THROTTLE` of every change of a raid applies; Matt, 2026-10-11); the buttons of the question before plan 8Q (`location:<token>:yes|no`) get the router's stale answer.
- A `group` option is free text with autocomplete of the groups the raid has (`respondWithGroups`), read by `typedGroup` (`src/commands/parties/reply.ts`); text that is no group is refused, never ignored.
- Sentences name the kind of party with `partyWord(party)` (`partyOver(party?)`, `problemText(reason, limits, word)`, `answerOutcome` takes the party): no member of a meetup reads "raid". `requireParty` says "a raid or meetup"; a control of a party that has ended says "This raid is over." only for what a raid alone has (`isRaidAction`: Set time… and Screenshot feedback; Done, the completion question's answers and Meet… are a max battle's too, plan 8X-3), otherwise "This has ended.", also on the map and in Meet…'s refusal
- Private answers to clicks on a post's controls go through `answerClick` (`ctx.clickAnswers`, `ClickAnswers`): the first click of a member on a party is answered with `reply`, later ones within two seconds are only acknowledged (`deferUpdate`), and the first answer is then edited to the answer of the last click. Only the first interaction of a burst may be given `editReply` (on a `deferUpdate`d click it would edit the post's own message). A refusal is thrown and shown at once. The test context answers every click by itself (window zero).

## Raids

- **One raid per gym, of any kind** (plan 8E; Matt, 2026-10-26: an elite raid shows its egg on the gym, usually from the evening before, so it blocks a standard raid there, and a standard raid blocks an elite one; EX raids once coexisted with normal ones, elite raids do not). The rule is `raidStandingAt(raids, guildId, gymId, now, except?)` over `standsAtGym(raid, guildId, gymId, now)` (`src/domain/party/raid.ts`: a raid of the server at the gym and `blocksNewRaid`, whatever its tier), and nothing else decides it: `PartyStore.raidAt(guildId, gymId, now)` (every report through `addRaidUnlessTaken`, with the reported party's `guildId`: `/raid`, screenshots, the map's `POST /api/raids`, `relocate`; and `/location`'s refusal before its dialog) and `RaidChangeService`'s check of a change of gym, tier or times (`gym-taken`; a raid that already stood at the gym may be changed whatever else is there, its tier from standard to elite and back included). A raid stands at a gym for its server only (review E7, as `maxAt`): a gym that two servers share takes a raid in each, though the gym's lock (`withGymLock`) stays one per gym. A report that meets a raid of the other kind joins it as one of another tier does (`exists`, the automatic status; nothing of the report, tier, boss or times, is applied to it). Raids are not compared by kind (`raidKindOf`) for this anywhere; `raidKindOf` serves the durations only. Two raids stored at one gym before plan 8E (a standard and an elite) are left as they are: each runs out by its clock and may still be changed at its own gym, and `raidAt` answers the older (`active()` is oldest first); the map's gym popup lists more than one raid only for them (`web.md`).
- **An Elite egg is reported with its hatch time** (review 8E-1 of plan 8E; Matt, 2026-10-29): without one it would stand for the whole elite incubation (`eliteRaidIncubateDuration`, about a day) and, by the rule above, keep every other report off its gym. `reportedTime` (`src/commands/raids/raid-input.ts`) refuses it with `ELITE_EGG_NEEDS_TIME`, the one check that `/raid`, the map's `POST /api/raids` and a typed report beside a screenshot that could not be read all go through (the page has no check of its own). An Elite raid seen hatched (a boss named, or `hatched`) without a time stands its usual 30 minutes, as before. Which eggs need the time is `eggNeedsHatchTime` (`src/domain/pogo/raid-tier.ts`), asked by every path below. Plan 8F-1 closed the two other paths (Matt, 2026-10-29): a screenshot whose result is an Elite egg without a time (its stage not read, also an Elite egg typed over such a read) is refused by `decide` with `elite-egg-time`, before any gym is asked; and `RaidChangeService.setBoss` (`/boss`, the map's Manage…) refuses a change whose result is an Elite egg without a hatch time, whatever the raid was, also when it names an Elite boss (a raid not reported hatched stays an egg until it hatches; one reported hatched may change), with the `PartyProblem` `elite-egg-time` ("once staff delete this egg, report it again with `/raid` and its hatch time": `/hatch` judges a time by the egg's current tier, so it cannot set one hours ahead). 8X-J's `moveBossTier` moves only raids with a boss, so it never makes an Elite egg (pinned by a test).
- What members change about a raid itself goes through `ctx.raids` (`RaidChangeService`): `setHatchTime`, `setEndTime`, `setDuration`, `setBoss`, `setMoveset`, `setGym`, and staff's `moveBossTier` (plan 8X-J: an `/admin boss add` that moves a boss to another tier of its group moves the running raids through the same `apply`, with no member, so no `noteMember` and no notifier; a raid the move would put in the way of another is left, which since plan 8E cannot happen for a raid: only a running raid moves, `blocksNewRaid`, and a running raid stands at its gym already, so the `in-the-way` count stays only as a guard), each answering `RaidChangeOutcome` (`gone`, `refused` with `reason` and for `gym-taken` the `other` raid, `ok` with `changed`), which a handler turns into the private answer with `answerRaidChange` (`src/commands/raids/change.ts`; the words are `raidChangeAnswer`'s, `src/commands/raids/raid-changes.ts`, which a refusal or a raid that ended throws). `/hatch`, `/end` (of a raid), the "Set time…" form, `/boss` (of a raid) and `/moveset` go through `changeRaidTime`, `changeRaidBoss` and `changeRaidMoves` there (read, change as the member, answer), the one code path the live map calls too (plan 8M-4, `web.md`); `/raid` reports through `reportRaidAs` and words the outcome with `reportWords` (`src/commands/raids/report.ts`), which the map shares; a report that would put the member in a forum somebody keeps them out of (the forum the new raid would go to, or that of the raid that stands at the gym now, `raidAt`, which may stand in another region's forum; a raid over by the clock bars nothing, review 8E-2: `reportBarred`, the map's check too) is refused with `REPORT_BARRED` (plan 8M-6), by `/raid`'s guard before its throttle for a gym typed outright, and once the gym is known (`withGym`) for one chosen from a menu or read from a screenshot (counted then), and again on "Report it there" after the question about a gym of another region (the access may have changed since); a failure of Discord to say propagates. A screenshot posted in a region channel is checked the same way before it is reported, unasked or once its gym is picked (`reportShot`, `screenshots.md`). The check reads `parties.active()` and asks Discord before the report, outside `PartyStore.update`, so when two members report a gym of two regions within moments, the first one's raid may appear in a forum the second's check did not see; the report therefore asks again where it would join a raid already at the gym (plan 8W-3: `RaidReport.mayJoin`, `mayJoinAs` / `mayJoinNoting` of `report.ts`, asked once the raid to join is known, not for the region's own forum, which every caller checked already; inside `PartyStore.update` a raid whose forum is not the one asked about any more is not joined either) and answers `barred` (`REPORT_BARRED`; the map 403, Discord away 503; a screenshot `unknown-access`) without signing them up; such a refusal comes after the throttle, so it counts as a use of `/raid` (on the map too, `raid-report.test.ts`). What is left is a change of the member's own access between that question and the write, as for any check of access. It decides inside the store under the locks of the gyms concerned, refuses a change after which the raid would stand at a gym where another raid of its server stands (`raidStandingAt`, plan 8E), moves or cancels meeting times that no longer fit (`fitMeetingTimes`), releases who is asked when a correction makes the raid an egg again, and says a line in the post. A meeting time is `ctx.attendance.setMeetingTime`.
- A raid that moves to a gym of another region becomes a new party (`PartyService.relocate`, reached through `ctx.raids.setGym`; `RaidChangeService.gymMove(party, gym)` says `local`, `region` or `nowhere`): the new party is stored and gets its post, the old post is told where the raid went (pinging who is signed up and allows it) while its party is still active, then the old party ends inside the store and the new one takes over what the raid was at that moment (`relocatedRaid`, `carriedOver` in `src/domain/party/relocate.ts`); the old post is closed by the usual end path. Whatever fails before the old party has ended takes the new post back (`takeBack`) and leaves the raid where it was; a raid that ended meanwhile does not come back. The party id changes with a move. `moved` (of `relocate` and `setGym`) is the answer to an interaction in the old post, given before that post is closed.
- The post of an egg shows the game's egg (`raidEggName`, `src/domain/pogo/raid-eggs.ts`): the wormhole for a T5 of ultra beasts, the primal egg for primals, none for a super mega, the plain egg for a shadow one. `raidLook(view)` (`src/render/raid-post.ts`) gives the live map the post's picture, the boss it names and where Where says it is (`place`: the gym's name as the post shows it, its point and address line; plan 8M-6), decided as for the post. The pictures are square (plan 8D-F, Matt, 2026-10-17: PokeMiners' icons are 112 by 128 and a phone crops a thumbnail to a square, cutting the egg's bottom off): `scripts/eggs/make-eggs.ts` (`npm run eggs:make`, the one step that downloads them, from `RAID_EGG_PICTURES_URL`'s pinned commit) padded each with transparency to a square of its longer side, the egg centred and unscaled, into `pictures/eggs/`, which is committed and in the image; nothing is fetched at run time, and `settings.raidEggPicturesUrl` is retired (`RETIRED_SETTINGS`). `EggPictures` (`src/services/egg-pictures.ts`, `PartyServiceOptions.eggPictures`) says where a post takes one from: once the web server listens (`src/app.ts` calls `serving()`, through `picturesServed` of `src/app-wiring.ts` with the pictures of the stops, when `startWebServer` answered true, so a port it could not take leaves the files) with `settings.web` at a public address that is not this machine's, the bot's own web server (`<publicUrl>/eggs/<name>.png?v=<8 hex>`, the start of the file's SHA-256, so that a cached picture never hides an egg made again; `web.md`); otherwise, and until then, the message carries the file and shows it as `attachment://<name>.png` (`RaidPostView.eggFile`, `PostLayout.pictureFile`, `PartyBrief.pictureFile`). Every post message says which files it has (`files`, `[]` when none: `postMessage`), and an edit that says so replaces the files the message had (`editAs` sends `attachments: []` and the files uploaded again; a conversion sends them through the REST route with `attachments` by index, `uploadsOf`), as Discord documents it: an edit keeps only the attachments it names. So the egg's file is uploaded again with every edit while it is an egg (some 22 KB), and the edit at hatch takes it away. A copy in another channel and the direct message's brief carry the same file. Where the bot may not attach files Discord answers Missing Permissions (50013), not Invalid Form Body: `isRefusedFile` (`src/discord/party-gateway.ts`, over `looksLikeRefusedFile`: 50013, 50035, 40005 or an error of reading the file, for a message that carries files) has a post, its edit and a copy sent once more without the file (`PostVariant.files: false`: the egg left out, pictures by address kept; `postForms`, `sendInForms`, `src/services/refused-files.ts`), and then in its other forms if Discord refuses one (`FormTrail`, below the controls); once a form without the file goes through (also one that needed plain emoji or no pictures as well), the forum, the post or the channel of copies is remembered for ten minutes (`RefusedFiles`, shared by `PartyService` and `PartyMirrors`), so that its next messages go without the file at once, with no refused call and no warning each time; a post made by its line of text after the first way had its file refused edits the line in without the file at once. Invalid Form Body is never noted as refused files (when a post is made it is taken as a refused first message; an edit or a copy refused so may have been refused for what it holds): only another refusal of the file is.

## Max battles

- A max battle (plan 8X-3; design `2026-10-20-max-battles-design.md`, section 3) is `MaxParty` (`type: 'max'`, `max: { tier, boss, spot }`; `src/domain/party/max.ts`): a tier of `MaxTier` (`D1` to `D5`, `G`), a boss that is always known (no egg), and a spot, a Power Spot as it was named (`PowerSpot`, a snapshot shown as it is now while it is the same place, `spotNow` over `shownPlace`) or a pin, an "Unlisted power spot" (Q7, `UNLISTED_SPOT`). `gymId` and `hatchTime` stay null; its end is always set. ## Copies and access

- A raid's message stands, with the post's own buttons, also in the server's channels for remote raids and for raids at elite raid gyms (`channels['elite-gym-raids']`; found by the names in `settings.channels`, only where the bot may post embeds; `raidMirrors` says which a raid wants, none once it is over by the clock), and at the bottom of its post after `/status` (kind `status`). `PartyMirrors` (`src/services/party-mirrors.ts`, owned by `PartyService`) keeps them: the party's `mirrorMessages` is the list of what exists (a copy is recorded when sent and taken off only once it is deleted; the newest of a kind counts and the render deletes the others), the copy in the post is synced in the post's own render, the copies in the shared channels in a slower queue of their own, so a post never waits for them. The link line of the region channel is `New raid: <#post>` and is never edited: Discord shows the post's current title in the link. A channel that refuses the bot for lack of a permission is left alone for ten minutes (edits and sends; deletions are always tried). Whoever ends a party waits for `mirrors.idle(partyId)` before deleting the copies and archiving.
- Access for members of other regions is `AccessService` (`src/services/access-service.ts`), which listens to the store: a member who is signed up (in any status, also done: Matt, 2026-10-11) for an active party whose post is in a forum they cannot see gets a permission overwrite of their own on that forum, and loses it when they are signed up for no party of that forum any more (they left, or the party ended or moved). The record (`PartyAccessGrant`, `AccessGrantRepository`) is written before the overwrite is made and removed after it; only an overwrite that is still exactly what the bot made is ever deleted (`PartyGateway.dismissMember` answers false otherwise), and a member somebody keeps out (an overwrite of their own that denies something, or one that denies the view to a role of theirs: `memberAccess` says `barred`) is left alone; an overwrite of their own that is empty (Discord's "reset to default" leaves one behind) or only allows (a member added to a private channel keeps "Connect") counts as none and is replaced by the bot's. The sweep (at start and hourly, with the forums' upkeep) takes back what nothing calls for and lets a member in again whose record has no overwrite; it never lets in anybody without a record. A self-assigned role taken away (`/iamnot`, the role menus, the web map's "Your regions…": `SelfRoleService.onRolesTaken`, wired in `app.ts`) has `memberChanged(guild, member)` settle the forums of the active parties the member takes part in, from a forced fetch of the member (`memberAccess(…, true)`: the cache may still hold the role), so that a party they are in keeps its forum in their sight (plan 8M-20, W2). Nothing awaits it: tests call `access.flush()`.
- What a member sees of a forum (or a region's text channel) is one rule (plan 8M-20): `viewsWith(channel, member, roleIds)`, discord.js's `memberPermissions` over any set of roles, which `memberAccess`'s `visible` uses with the roles they hold and the web map's "Your regions" with a role more or less; `memberGates(guild, member, gates, fresh)` answers it for every gate of a server from one look at the member, with what denies each the view (`denials`; `web.md`, "Your regions on the map").
