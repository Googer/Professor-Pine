# Notifications and direct messages

What members ask to be told about (wants and `/want`'s boxes, gym targets, new trains, boss-set), how the notifier matches a change of a party against it, how direct messages are queued and sent, the DMs themselves with their Stop and Manage… buttons, and spawn reports with Unown sightings. Read before changing wants, gym targets, the notifier, DMs or `/wild`.

## Wants and gym targets

- A want (`PokemonNotification`) is per member and server and is one of: a species (every form, megas included), one form (`form`, a catalogue key), the shadow raids of either (`shadow`; since plan 8R-2 shadow raids and Team GO Rocket catches, the Shadow box), a raid tier (raids only), perfect or zero IVs (spawns only); a mega is raids only. The columns are read and written only through the codec (`notification-target.ts`: `readNotification` gives the target and the scope in effect; a row it cannot read is skipped, counted and never deleted on its own) and the pure functions of `src/domain/pogo/wants.ts` (`readWants`, `planSetWant`, `rowsOfWant`: duplicate legacy rows are one want with the union of their scopes, two keys of one catalogue entry are one want, a row is rewritten only when its meaning changes). `ctx.wants` (`WantStorage`: `list`, `set`, `remove`, `removeAll`, `count`) is the repository; `MemoryWants` (`test/support/wants.ts`) is its twin and runs the same functions. `pokemonWantFits(catalogue, target, entry, shadowRaid)` says whether a want is for a boss or a spawn. Since plan 8R-1 a raid scope means raids only (no max battles), and the legacy `!wants` codec and the scope rules (`planSetWant`, `widerScope`) are unchanged.
- **Costumed wants** (plan 8R-4, migration 030; design `2026-10-22-costume-form-wants-design.md`): `PokemonNotification.costumed` and `TargetWant.costumed` (tinyint, default 0) make a Pokémon's want costumed only, a property of the row like the scope, never part of its target, token, unique key or id (`Want.costumed?: true`, a Pokémon's `TargetWant.costumed?: true`; `boxTarget(kind, dex, form, costumed)`, `withCostume`, `isCostumedTarget`; a Stop or Remove takes the row whatever its costume). Rows of one target that disagree read as plain (`readWants`: plain wins, as the wider scope); `planSetWant(…, costumed)` rewrites a row whose costume differs; a `TargetWant` added again takes the costume of its add (an upsert per costume, `createdAt` kept). Every sighting carries `costumed` (`PokemonSighting`): a raid's boss as the list has it in the raid's tier (`ShownBoss.costumed`), a spawn as `SpawnNews.costumed` says (an event on lists it costumed, "Spawn reports"), a research reward by `rewardCostumed` (its Leek Duck name and picture, worked out when read), a max boss and a Rocket catch never. `pokemonWantFits(…, costumed)` fits a costumed want only to a costumed sighting, under the entry the costume copies; a plain want fits either. A mega or primal is never costumed (Matt's ruling 4: written plain, left out of Costumed), so a costumed want of the whole species (no form) fits its mega and primal entries as a plain want would (the catalogue's `kind`): every form and Costumed ticked still tells of the species' mega raids, while a costumed want of one form is no mega's. The matchers (`raidWantsFiring`, `spawnWantsFiring`, `rocketShadowFiring`) give the wants themselves, so a reason carries `costumed` (`wantReason`) and the DM names it "Costumed Pikachu" (`wantTargetLabel(…, costumed)`, `pokemonWantName({ costumed })`: no "(every form)", "Costumed Corsola"), a target "Costumed Pikachu (research)" (`targetWantLabel`); members told of one event share a message only where their reasons read alike (`shownReasonKey`).

## The notifier

- **A DM's times are its server's** (plan 19A-2; `servers.md`, "A server's zone"): a DM about a party says its times and names its boss in that party's server's zone (`posts.zoneOf(party)`, `PartyService.zoneOf`), one about a report in the report's server's (`servers.zoneOf(guildId)`: the research's "until"); a DM with no server, the instance's default.
- Whoever changes a party tells `PartyNotifier` (`src/services/notify-service.ts`) explicitly, with who acted: `maxReported` and `maxChanged` (plan 8X-3: a max battle's report, once its post exists, and a boss that is another; who wants the boss, `maxWantsFiring`: since plan 8R-1 the Dynamax box of a Dynamax battle and the Gigantamax box of a Gigantamax one (`TargetWant` `dynamax:` / `gigantamax:`, of the species or of the boss's form; Matt's answer 9 of the stop reports design), never a raid, tier, shadow or spawn want, no gym target; at most one DM an hour per member, boss and kind (a Gigantamax Machamp is told within the hour of a Dynamax one), `MAX_WANT_QUIET_MS`, kept in memory and lost on a restart, read when the members are gathered and written as each message is put in line, so two reports of one boss moments apart at two spots may both reach a member; the DM says so in small print, `maxHourNote`: "You won't be told about more Dynamax Machamp battles for an hour."; the spot's and the boss's names through `plainNoLinks`), `raidReported`, `raidChanged(before, after, actorId)` (the one call for every change of a raid; the notifier decides whether boss, tier or gym differ; the clock passes null), `raidMoving` / `raidMoved`, `trainCreated` (the DM names a train of max battles by its kind, "A new Gigantamax Train has been announced", plan 8X-4; a raid train "A new train" as before), `meetingTimeChanged` (a group's meeting time in a raid or a max battle, `isBattle`; the DM names `the max battle at <spot>`), `startTimeChanged`, `spawnReported`, `researchReported` (plan 8R-1, `stop-reports.md`, "Wants and DMs": a research report's DM per region channel, with a member's own variant of the stops not told yet, `Announcement.variant`), `rocketReported` (plan 8R-2, the same section: a Rocket report's DM per region channel; a grunt at the spawn priority, one an hour per member and grunt type; a hideout once a day per stop; since plan 8R-4L a lineup's report is wanted, quiet and announced by its type, `typeKeyOf`, its Shadow wants judged by its own catches, and a grunt refined in place by a lineup is told again as a report, the hour per type keeping a second DM away, never to its reporter or the reporter of the grunt it changed, `RocketNews.alsoActors`, review R3, who have heard of the type for the hour; DMs sent before a refine are not changed). Every method returns at once and never throws (`safely`), and is called after the party's own effects. `NotifyService` matches with the pure functions of `src/domain/notify/reasons.ts`, asks `viewers`, and remembers per party which reasons it told which member: one DM per member and event, only for reasons not yet told, never to the actor (whose reasons count as told); the memory is shared by the two parties of a move across regions and forgotten at the party's end. A boss-set member is told every boss that differs from the last one they were told. Messages to everybody signed up (meeting and start times, and a raid moved to another gym) have a line of their own per party, ask nobody and remember nothing. A raid moved to another gym (`/location`; `raidChanged` with another gym, or `raidMoved` to another region; follow-ups of 2026-11-07, Matt) tells every attendee who is not done, but for who moved it, by a DM of its own (`tellMoved`, `raidMovedSentence`: "The raid you signed up for has moved to another gym: from **The Stacks** ([Map](<…>)) to **Old Stone Fountain** ([Map](<…>)) in #east.", the region only where it changed, the gyms with their Map links as a post's Where, the brief of the post and its Go to post button, the new post after a move to another region; `DM_PRIORITY.urgent`); nothing for a change that keeps the gym. Only the DMs to who is signed up (meeting and start times, a move to another gym) are sent where `features.notifications` is off. The research and Rocket DMs show their picture by the rule of the stops and have it made first, waiting 2.5 seconds at most, and show none where its source is gone (plan 8R-6, `NotifyServiceOptions.pictures`, `StopPictures`: `stop-reports.md`, "Pictures at the stops").
