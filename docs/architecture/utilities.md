# Pokémon, raid bosses and the utility commands

The game data and its catalogue, finding Pokémon, raid tiers and bosses with their administration, nicknames, pictures and emoji, and the utility commands (`/rank`, `/counters`, `/find-gym`, `/request`, `/maps`, `/help`) with the outside services they use (Pokebattler, Nominatim, GitHub). Read before changing game data, Pokémon search, bosses or a utility command.

## Game data and the catalogue

- Species, forms, names, types, stats and moves come from the game master through `ctx.gameData.catalogue` (`src/domain/pogo/`); search nicknames come from `CURATED_NICKNAMES` and `PokemonRepository.nicknames()`. The new bot never reads `data/pokemon.json`, `types.json` or `weather.json`; they stay only for the legacy bot and the parity test.
- A Pokémon is identified by its catalogue key (`rattata_alola`, `charizard_mega_x`). Legacy names are translated with `keyForLegacyName` / `legacyNameForKey`.
- One identity per form: a species with a named default form is known by that form (`giratina_altered`, `landorus_incarnate`; there is no `giratina` entry). `catalogue.byKey` and `byGameMasterId` also accept aliases (the bare species id, costumes, appearance-only forms), so store the `key` of the entry they return, never the text that was looked up.
- Costumes (plan 8R-4, `GAME_DATA_VERSION` 7): `GameData.costumes` is the aliases that are costumes, the flagged costumes that copy an entry of their species (every Pikachu in a hat; `corsola_spring_2026` copies Galarian Corsola) and the unflagged appearance-only copies (`UNFLAGGED_COSTUMES`: `*_COPY_2019`, `PSYDUCK_SWIM_2025`), never the wild legendaries of GO Tour: Johto (`RAIKOU_S` and the rest of `COSMETIC_FORMS`, Matt's answer 1) nor a flagged costume with stats of its own (Armored Mewtwo is a form); `catalogue.isCostume(id)` and `hasCostume(dex)` (any entry's). Whether a sighting is costumed is `isCostumed(name, image, { ids, lookup })` (`src/domain/pogo/costumes.ts`, pure; `costumeLookupOf(catalogue)`): the form id a costume of the game data, a form id it does not know that `isCostumeForm` calls a costume, the picture's own costume part (`.c<COSTUME>`, the third number of an older `pokemon_icon_<dex>_<form>_<costume>.png`, `pictureSaysCostume`), last a name "<Pokémon> wearing …" (`wornBy`). `matchBoss` places such a name as the Pokémon that wears it ("Zubat wearing a top hat" with Leek Duck's hand-named picture is Zubat), for raids, research and Rocket catches alike; `costumedSighting(catalogue, name, image)` gives a named Pokémon's key, dex and costume.
- The bot downloads the game data at start when its saved copy (the parsed data, `var/game-data-cache.json`) is older than `refreshHours` or missing, and again every `refreshHours` while running (URLs and `refreshHours` in `settings.gameData`); it can start offline once it has run once, and deleting that file forces a fresh download.

## Finding Pokémon

- Pokémon are found through `ctx.pokemon`: `exact(text)` is the one entry a text names outright (act on this); `search(text)` is a list ordered best first that includes near misses (offer it, never act on its first entry); `find(text, { limit, filter, plain })` is the same list with how each hit fits (`exact`, `word`, `beginning`, `loose`, `guess`) and whether it was found by its name (`named`). The order: a whole word of a name, then a beginning, then looser hits, guesses last; among hits led by the species' own name or a nickname, a legendary, mythical or ultra-beast species (`PokemonEntry.class`, from the game master) first, at most `CLASS_LIFTS` entries of one species (`plain: true` switches that off). A nickname is never read as a misspelling, a costume word alone does not find its species, a word of up to five letters must keep its first letter, and a text that names a Pokémon outright brings no guesses. A list that puts current bosses first asks for them by themselves (`filter`), and offers a guess only where the shown name has the guessed word (`showsGuessed`, `src/commands/choices.ts`). After a nickname change call `PokemonSearchService.reloadNicknames()`.
- Places are not Pokémon: gyms, PokéStops and Power Spots together are `ctx.placeFinder`, with an index per kind and the merge of `src/domain/search/place-merge.ts`, whose fits are worded as the Pokémon search's (`gyms-and-regions.md`).
- Short fixed lists (status and privacy words, a boss's moves) use the strict matcher `matchNamed` (`src/domain/search/word-match.ts`), not an index: a misspelling must keep the first letter and may not add words.

## Raid tiers and bosses

- Raid tiers are `RaidTier` values (`src/domain/pogo/raid-tier.ts`), never bare numbers. `PokemonNotification.pokemon` is read and written only through the codec in `notification-target.ts`.
- There is no EX raid tier (removed in plan 4F; none since 2020): `RaidLevel` has no `EX`. What is stored with it is tolerated, never written: a raid's state document reads `EX` as `Elite`, `RaidBoss` rows with an unknown level are left out, an override with one is logged once, the feed's EX heading is an unknown heading, the retired settings keys are stripped before the strict check (`withoutRetiredSettings`), and migration 009 made EX wants elite wants. Elite raids are rare (Matt, 2026-10-12): wherever they compete with another feature the other feature wins. Elite raid gyms are another matter: they keep their data, staff's views and their copies, but members no longer see the mark (plan 8D; see `gyms-and-regions.md`).
- Raid bosses come from `ctx.raidBosses` (`bosses()`, `bossesOf(tier)`, `autoset(tier)`): the feed's list (`settings.raidBosses`, synced into the `RaidBoss` table by `RaidBossService`) with the moderators' overrides (`RaidBossOverride`) applied. A boss newer than the game master has no catalogue entry (`entry` is undefined): show its `name` (a mega of a known species: below, "A mega or primal"). The legacy `Pokemon.tier` / `exclusive` / `mega` / `elite` / `shiny` columns and `AutosetPokemon` are never read. The bosses come from Leek Duck's raid rotations, ScrapedDuck's `raids.json` (Leek Duck's raid boss page as data) as the fallback (plan 8B-L, below); both give the same entries: a boss is a display name, a heading text and a picture address, and `matchBoss` (`src/domain/pogo/raid-boss-feed.ts`) gives it a catalogue entry only when the picture's file name (`pm<dex>.f<FORM>.icon.png`) and the name do not contradict each other; otherwise it stays unresolved (listed by the feed's name, without stats), never the wrong Pokémon. `raid-boss-sweep.test.ts` checks every entry of the catalogue; a heading Pine does not know is reported, its bosses left out. `npm run raid-bosses:check` shows how the live feed is read. A boss of the list in effect is `costumed` (plan 8R-4) when a feed row of it in its tier is by `isCostumed` (its name, picture and ids; `RAID_OPS.costumed`), an override by its ids alone; a costumed and a plain row of one tier are one boss, costumed; never a max boss (`MAX_OPS` has no `costumed`). `shownBoss` copies it from the boss of the raid's own tier only (`ShownBoss.costumed`), never from another tier's.
- **Leek Duck's raid rotations** (plan 8B-L, finding B1 of the 8R-9F staging: ScrapedDuck scrapes the Raid Bosses page as served, which can lag the game by a rotation, since the page picks its rotation in the browser): a read of `RaidBossService` with a `manifest` (`settings.raidBosses.manifestUrl`, `RAID_MANIFEST_URL` unless set; `app.ts` always gives it, with `settings.timezone`) is `readRaidRotations` (`src/services/raid-rotations.ts`), through `BossFeedService`'s `FeedSource` (the family's `parse` reads the document it gives, as if the feed had sent it). The domain is `src/domain/pogo/raid-manifest.ts` (pure): `parseRaidManifest` reads `regular_raids` and `shadow_raids` (zod, lenient: a rotation with a bad slug, which must be lower-case letters, digits and `-`, a bad time, an end not after its start, or a slug listed twice is skipped and counted; only a document without a list of regular raids throws); a rotation with `local_time` is at the wall-clock time of `settings.timezone` (the `-0800` and the timestamps are Leek Duck's Pacific placeholder, never read), one without it is at its offset's time, and a zoned time is an in-person event's, left out by the one rule of everyone's events (`isForEveryone`, below; counted as `elsewhere`). `rotationsAt(rotations, now)` is those in force: the running ones (`start <= now < end`), and of a kind (regular, shadow) of which none is running, the ones of that kind that ended last, kept until the next of the kind starts but for at most 24 hours after their end (`KEPT_AFTER_END_MS`; review R1: Leek Duck leaves gaps, Halloween Part I ended at 20:00 and the Giratina week began at 06:00 the next day, and the game keeps its bosses meanwhile); `nextRotationChange` the first moment after now at which that set changes (a start, an end no gap keeps, the end of a gap's 24 hours), `rotationAddress` a rotation's page beside the manifest (`<slug>.html`, as Leek Duck's own script asks; a 308 leads to `/raids/<slug>`, which `fetch` follows), `parseRotationFragment` a page's `tier` blocks (the `tier-label`, else `data-tier`) and `card`s (`p.name`, `.boss-img img[src]`, `svg.shiny-icon` inside `.boss-img` only) as ScrapedDuck's entries (`name`, `tier`, `canBeShiny`, `image`), read with the small scans of the research page, a card without a name skipped and counted. Overlaps (`mergeRotations`): each tier heading (by `readHeading`, else its text) is given by the regular rotation that started last (of two begun together, the one that ends first), the others keep the headings it lacks (the Super Mega Raid Day adds its tier to the week's; a heading both list, say 5-Star Raids, is the Raid Day's for its hours, the week's boss of that tier out of the list meanwhile; Leek Duck's page shows only the first-ending rotation; both agreed by the coordinator, as is leaving rotations tagged `Exclusive` to the zone rule alone); the shadow rotations are merged among themselves the same way and added beside, never taking a regular heading, their headings given as `Shadow <heading>` (review R4), so a boss of theirs is a shadow boss whether or not its name says `Shadow `. A read asks the manifest, then the page of each rotation in force only (sequentially, each with the service's time limit: a few requests). **Fallback** to ScrapedDuck's feed (`url`, with its validators, as before) for a structural cause: the manifest is missing (a 4xx), is no JSON or no manifest, no regular rotation is in force even with the 24 hours of a gap (the reason says how many rotations were left out at a city's own times: "no regular rotation is in force (2 at a city's own times left out)", review R6), a page in force is missing or lists no boss, or the merged entries give no boss Pine can read; no shadow rotation in force with a readable manifest is no shadow bosses (Leek Duck lists them ahead), not a fallback. **An outage is no fallback** (review R2): a download of the manifest or a page that times out, gets no answer or a 5xx (`isPassing`; the source hands `get`'s rejection over as `cause`) while the list stored came from the manifest (or is of an earlier run: the source of a stored list is not kept) is a failed sync, the list kept, retried after `retryMs` by the quick retries; the read that comes when the three quick retries are spent (`failures` at `QUICK_RETRIES`) reads the fallback instead. With no list stored, or one from the fallback, an outage falls back at once. A list from the manifest carries no validators, so the fallback's first request after one is unconditional (a 304 never vouches for the manifest's list). The source is logged at information once each time it changes (`raid bosses read from Leek Duck's raid manifest` with the rotations' titles, `raid bosses read from the fallback feed` with the `reason`); the rotations and cards skipped are added to the parse's `skipped` (the warning `raid boss feed entries were left out`). Caps: the manifest 200,000 characters (`MAX_MANIFEST_CHARS`; about 7,000 on 2026-10-27), a page 500,000 (`MAX_ROTATION_PAGE_CHARS`; about 20,000). **The read at a change of rotation:** the source gives its next change (also when it fell back with a readable manifest), and `BossFeedService` reads again `AFTER_CHANGE_MS` (5 seconds) after it when that comes before the refresh; every read learns the next change anew, so one that passed during a read is read again at once, never in a loop. A read the timer did not start (`/admin boss sync`, a catch-up) that learns a change before the pending timer sets the timer again (`followChange`, review R3); and with a source the first read of a run is at once whatever the stored list's age (its next change is not stored, so a restart at 09:55 would miss 10:00). The rest (the stored list at start, the out-of-date warning, the overrides, the announcer, `tierMoves`) is unchanged; a read whose list did not change says nothing. A "Super Mega Raids" heading is the `SuperMega` level (`readHeading`); a heading Pine has no tier for is an unknown heading as with the feed. Tests read the manifest and pages of 2026-10-27 (`test/fixtures/raid-manifest/`, `test/support/raid-manifest.ts`). The feeds of raid bosses, max bosses, events, game data, research and Rocket follow a redirect on their own host only (`fetchOnItsHost`, `src/services/same-host-fetch.ts`), since Leek Duck moves its pages (a rotation's `<slug>.html` answers 308 to `<slug>` since about 2026-11-01): a feed that leads to another host fails.
- Staff change the raid boss list only through `ctx.raidBossAdmin` (`RaidBossService` as `RaidBossAdmin`: overrides, `feedBosses()`, `sync()`; override writes and the end of a sync run one at a time). A sync's change is `FeedChange` with `seen`, the difference of `effectiveBosses` before and after with the same overrides (`diffBosses`, `src/domain/pogo/boss-diff.ts`): what members see. `RaidBossAnnouncer` posts `seen` in every server's bot-updates channel when `settings.raidBosses.announceChanges`, and the out-of-date warning (time, never the error) in the moderators' channel; nothing before `ready()`, nothing after `close()`. A failed sync has a `kind` (never show `reason`). A new autoset for a tier replaces the tier's earlier one. A boss the game data does not know has a key starting with `UNKNOWN_KEY_PREFIX` (`~`), never equal to a catalogue key and never stored; `howIdentified` says how a feed boss was matched.
- Both boss lists are one machine with two families (plan 8X-2): `BossFeedService<T>` (`src/services/boss-feed-service.ts`: the stored list at start, the first read when it is older than the refresh (with a `source`, the first read of a run is at once; review R3 of 8B-L), retries, the out-of-date warning, override writes one at a time) with a `BossFeedFamily` (its noun in the log, its parser, its list in effect, its override rules); `RaidBossService` and `MaxBossService` are its families. The list rules (`familyBosses`, `familyOverrideKeys`, `familyReport`, `diffFeedIn`, `diffBossesIn`) take the family's tier operations (`RAID_OPS`, `MAX_OPS`); `effectiveBosses`, `bossReport` and the rest keep their raid signatures. The tables are kept apart per family by their levels (`RaidBossRepository`: every level but the max tiers, so the retired EX level stays the raids' to report; `MaxBossRepository`: `MAX_TIERS`): every read and write filters by the family, and an override without a tier (a shiny one for every tier) is a raid override.
- **An add moves its boss** (plan 8X-J, design `2026-10-25-max-boss-tier-fix-design.md`): a boss has one tier at a time within a group of tiers, `BossFamilyOps.oneTierAmong` (`MAX_OPS`: `dynamax` for D1 to D5, none for G; `RAID_OPS`: `plain` for T1, T3, T5 and Elite, `shadow` for the shadow tiers, none for the mega tiers). One rule, `tierMoves` (`src/domain/pogo/raid-bosses.ts`), read by `familyBosses` and `familyReport`: the feed's slot of a boss at a tier is left out when the boss has an `add` or `autoset` in force at another tier of the group that no `hide` of the add's own tier cancels, and no add of its own at the feed's tier (two adds of one boss in a group both stay); a costume is a boss of its own (a costumed add moves nothing, a costumed slot is never left out). It is applied after the adds and before the hides; the boss at the add's tier is shiny when the add or a moved row says so. The implied hide lasts as long as the add (`/admin boss remove` brings the feed's tier back); there is no new override row. The max list works the moves out over the whole feed, begun and upcoming (`FamilyView`: `maxBossesAt` lists the begun rows, `comingMaxBosses` the upcoming ones without the adds), so an upcoming row of a moved boss does not come back; `/admin boss list`'s Coming reads `coming()`. `familyReport` gives each add its `moved` tiers (the add's answer "The feed lists it at Dynamax 1★: that one is left out while this lasts.", the list's "moves it from Dynamax 1★"), and marks an add "the feed lists it now" only when the feed lists it at its tier and at no other tier of its group (a costume's tier not counted, review J7). An `autoset` moves as an add does and says the same (review J2). The answer also names a hide in force of the boss at a tier the add leaves out as no longer needed, only when the add moves (review J3). Running battles move with the add (Matt's answer 4): after an add or autoset whose `moved` is not empty, `ctx.bossMoves` (`BossTierMoves`, `src/services/boss-tier-moves.ts`) changes every active party of every server (`PartyStore.active()`; the boss list is the installation's, review J5) that `bossTierMoved` (`src/domain/party/boss-move.ts`, pure: a max battle or a raid of the boss, by key, or for a boss the game data lacks by the names the list had for it at the moved-from tiers, `ReportedOverride.movedNames`, review J6; at a moved-from tier, its end still ahead; a max battle keeps its end and `bossSince` through `setMaxBoss`, a raid its moves through `setBoss`; the same object otherwise) changes. A max battle goes through `PartyStore.update`; a raid through `RaidChangeService.moveBossTier` (review J1), under the rules of every raid change (the gym's lock, meeting times fitted, an egg again releasing the asked, a move to Elite working its times out again), and one that would then stand in the way of another raid at its gym (`gym-taken`) is left at its tier and counted apart (since plan 8E only a guard: only a raid not over by the clock moves, `blocksNewRaid`, and it stands at its gym already). Each post is shown anew and told "Staff moved **Dynamax Sneasel** to 2★." (no ping, no DMs, no notifier); the answer counts them ("2 battles running were moved.", "1 raid running was left at its tier: …"). Removing the add or its expiry does not move them back (`/boss` fixes one battle).
- A Pokémon as the boss of a raid of a tier is `bossAtTier(entry, tier, bosses)` (`src/domain/pogo/boss-at-tier.ts`; Matt, 2026-10-25: every surface decides a domain fact with the code the commands use): `listed` (the list has it in that tier), `unlisted` (the list does not have it: that Pokémon at that tier), `other-shadow-kind` (listed in that level as the other shadow kind), `listed-elsewhere` (listed in another level; the list is right), `not-a-mega` (a Mega or Mega Legendary raid takes a mega form only). `/raid` reads a tier typed before a name by it (`reportedWithTier`, its refusals worded there), and so does the screenshot reader for a boss the list does not have (`screenshots.md`).
- Max battle tiers are `MaxTier` (`D1` to `D5`, `G`; `src/domain/pogo/max-tier.ts`), the one spelling in the domain, the state document, `RaidBoss.level` and the history; `BattleKind` (`raid`, `dynamax`, `gigantamax`, `battleKindOf(tier)`) is only for lobby sizes and train kinds; their words are there too (`maxTierLabel` "Dynamax 2★", `maxBossTitle` "Dynamax Machamp (2★)", `maxBossChoiceName` "Machamp · Dynamax 2★" / "Gigantamax Machamp").
- Max bosses come from `ctx.maxBosses` (`bosses()`: the list in effect, D1 to G, then name; `feedBosses()`, the feed as stored), staff's list from `ctx.maxBossAdmin`. Max battles are always on (Matt, 2026-10-21, plan 8X-3: trains, meetups and raid bosses cannot be switched off either): `settings.maxBattles` has a default for every key, and a file without the block has them all. The feed is Pokebattler's `GET /raids` (`settings.maxBattles.url`, `MAX_BOSS_FEED_URL` unless set, read every `refreshMinutes`, 6 hours unless set; about 700 KB): `parseMaxBossFeed` (`src/domain/pogo/max-bosses.ts`) reads `RAID_LEVEL_1_MAX` to `_5_MAX` as `D1` to `D5` and `RAID_LEVEL_6_MAX` as `G`, verified or not (Matt, 2026-10-20), never the `_FUTURE` and `_LEGACY` twins nor the raid tiers (Pokebattler is no raid boss source); a max tier it does not know that has bosses is reported. `maxBossOf` maps an id: the entry of that Pokebattler id, else the entry the id names as a catalogue key or alias (a bare species is the species, or the default form of a species known by one); a Gigantamax id drops `_GIGANTAMAX` and is that Pokémon with the tier `G`, never an entry of its own; a mega is no max boss; an unknown id is a boss by a name made of it, logged once. An upcoming boss is a boss from the start of its `startDate`'s day in `settings.timezone` (Pokebattler gives one instant for an event held at a local hour: the lake trio's 14:00 Max Battle Day is 17:00 in New York), stored as its `firstSeen` (`FeedBoss.startsAt`), so the list leaves it out until then, after a restart too; Pokebattler gives no end in any max tier, so `/max`'s list leaves an upcoming boss out again once an event that named it is over and no event names it now or later (`withoutEndedUpcoming`, plan 8X-3: one the feed first had from that event's day on; a boss of the pool from before stays). It needs the ended event still known: ScrapedDuck drops an event once it has ended; the max events are kept in the database since plan 8X-F (`MaxEvent`, below), an ended one while it started less than eight days ago, so only a restart later than that offers the boss again until Pokebattler drops it (step 11 of the 8X-2 staging, after 2026-10-24, decides whether more is needed). `dynamaxPokemon` is not read. Max boss changes are not announced.
